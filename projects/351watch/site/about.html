Who makes Agenda Ahead

Agenda Ahead is made by one person, not by a company or a town, and no town or company pays for it. It exists because the agendas that decide these things are posted as PDFs a few days before each meeting, and most people find out after the vote. A program reads the agendas every morning and writes each sentence from them; the link under a sentence is its source. If a sentence is wrong, use the contact page and we’ll fix it and say so.

How the sentences are written

Every morning a program reads the agendas that 315 towns in Massachusetts, New York and Rhode Island post online, picks out the items about solar, battery storage, data centers, moratoriums, clean-energy permit rules, zoning changes, affordable housing, cell towers and wetlands permits, and writes one sentence for each that says what the board is doing with it. It quotes the agenda and links to it, and it never adds a fact the agenda doesn’t carry. When an agenda doesn’t say whether people can speak, the page says so. More about how it works.

What it costs

Nothing, for your town and the towns next to it. You don’t need an account, and there is nothing advertised. If that changes, this page will say so first.

Getting it wrong

A sentence can be wrong: an agenda is scanned badly, a board changes its name, a hearing moves. Tell us through the contact page and we fix it and note the fix on the page. The agenda itself is always one tap away, and the town clerk is the last word on dates.
