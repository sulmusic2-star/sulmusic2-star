About Agenda Ahead

Agenda Ahead reads the meeting agendas that 646 towns, cities and counties in Massachusetts, New York, Rhode Island, Virginia, Maryland, Michigan and Ohio post online, every morning, and lists the items about solar, battery storage, data centers, moratoriums, zoning changes, affordable housing and cell towers, with the day, the time, the room and a link to the agenda.

Who makes it

Agenda Ahead is made by one person, not by a company or a town, and no town or company pays for it. It is not a government website.

How the pages are made

Each row shows the agenda’s own words for the item, then the facts around it: whether it is a public hearing, a vote or a discussion, the board, the day, the time and the place. The words are the clerk’s, trimmed; nothing is added that the agenda does not carry. The agenda itself is one tap away under every row. How it works.

What it costs

Nothing. Following a town and the towns next to it is free, with no account and no advertising. If that changes, this page will say so first.

Getting it wrong

A row can be wrong: an agenda is scanned badly, a board changes its name, a hearing moves. Tell us through the contact page and we fix it. The town clerk is the last word on dates.
