What’s free

Alerts for your town and the towns next to it are free. So is everything else on the site: town pages, topics and search.

Free alerts include

  • One town plus the towns next to it.
  • Every board we read there: planning board, zoning board of appeals, conservation commission, select board or council, and town meeting warrants.
  • Every topic: solar, battery storage, data centers, moratoria, clean-energy permits, zoning changes, affordable housing (40B), cell towers and wetlands filings.
  • Items before the meeting. We read agendas every morning, and for meetings still ahead we first saw items a median 3 days before the meeting (how we measure it).
  • Delivery by email (starting soon), a calendar feed, RSS, or a Slack, Teams or Discord channel.
  • No account. A private link is the only key.
