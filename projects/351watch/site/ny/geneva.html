    Town Board Agenda (PDF): Geneva Town Board, Tue Oct 13

    Speaking: No hearing on this law tonight; anyone can speak at ‘privilege of the floor’ (items 3 and 23). The 6:00 pm hearing is on the 2027 budget.

    • Town Board: Final vote on Geneva’s battery storage law (Local Law No. 5)Vote

      Resolution 68-2026 adopts Local Law No. 5 of 2026, which adds a Battery Energy Storage System Law (new Chapter 58) to the town code and changes the zoning chapter (165) to match. The law sets rules on where battery storage can go and how it is built and run, with Tier 1 (up to 600 kWh) and Tier 2 (over 600 kWh) systems.

      Before this: Public hearing held Sep 8, 2026, 6:00 pm, Geneva Town Hall, 3750 County Road 6 (from the resolution). Our Sep 8 agenda record also carried the SEQR finding and this same adoption resolution.

    • Final vote on changes to Geneva’s solar energy rules (Local Law No. 6)Vote

      The board votes on adopting Local Law No. 6 of 2026, which amends the town code’s rules on solar energy systems. The agenda doesn’t say what changes.

      Before this: Public hearing held Sep 8, 2026, 6:00 pm, Geneva Town Hall, 3750 County Road 6 (from the resolution). Our Sep 8 agenda record also carried the SEQR finding and this same adoption resolution.
