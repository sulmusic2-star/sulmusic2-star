On the agenda

    • 24-unit 40B housing at 210, 214 and 218 First Parish Road: hearing continues from Sep 21Hearing

      First Parish Village, LLC seeks a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build 24 dwelling units at 210, 214 and 218 First Parish Road, with 25% of them restricted for low and moderate income households. The hearing continues from September 21, 2026.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 21 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda; the Conservation Commission’s Oct 5 agenda had the wetlands hearing for the same lots.

      Topics
      Affordable housing
      Where
      210, 214 and 218 First Parish Road, Scituate, MA (Assessor Parcels 44-1-13A, 44-1-13B and 44-1-13C)
      Size

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      II. APPLICATION 210, 214 and 218 FIRST PARISH ROAD, Continuance from September 21, 2026 Hearing First Parish Village, LLC seeks a Comprehensive Permit pursuant to Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40B … to allow for the construction and use of twenty-four (24) dwelling units of which twenty-five (25%) percent will be restricted for low and moderate income households
