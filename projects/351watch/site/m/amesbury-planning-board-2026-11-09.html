    Words on the agenda. 40R district: a smart-growth zoning district for denser housing with a share kept affordable, which the state pays the town to adopt. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting.
