Pepperell Zoning Board of Appeals

Wed Nov 18, 7 pm Open the agenda

Speak for or against in writing before the hearing, or in person at the hearing (yourself or through an agent or attorney). In person only.

    • 40 homes, 10 affordable, at 24 Bancroft Street (Woodland Ridge 40B): hearing continues

      Woodland Ridge, LLC (through attorney Melissa Robbins of Farrell, & Robbins, P.C.) wants a 40B comprehensive permit for 40 single-family homes for sale on 45.5 acres between Bancroft Street and Orion Road: 32 two-bedroom and 8 three-bedroom homes, with 10 of them (25%) affordable to households at or below 80% of area median income.

      Before this: Also a continuation hearing on the Sep 16 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda.
