Gardner Conservation Commission, Mon Oct 26, 6:30 pm

  1. Mon Oct 26, 6:30 pm Gardner City Hall

    • Cell towers

      The Conservation Commission will hold a public hearing on a wetlands permit request for a cell tower by SBA Network Services at 677 Timpany Blvd.

      Public hearing.

      Can I speak?

      Yes. Anyone can attend a public hearing and ask to speak. You can also send written comments to the board through the town clerk before the meeting. Ask the clerk about sign-up rules and time limits.

      Gardner’s website lists the clerk’s office.

      Show the agenda text

      Quoted from the agenda:

      PUBLIC HEARING The Gardner Conservation Commission will hold a Public Meeting under MGL, Ch. 131, § 40, Wetlands Protection Act and the City of Gardner Wetlands Protection Ordinance on the Notice of Intent filed by SBA Network Services, LLC, 8051 Congress Ave, Boca Raton, FL, 33487, represented by Fieldstone Land Consultants, PLLC, for the construction of a telecommunication facility at 677 Timpany Blvd (Map R12-16-1), Gardner, MA, 01440.
