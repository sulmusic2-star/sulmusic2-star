On the agenda

    • 32-unit 40B at Viking Lane/Ward Street (River Stone): hearing continues from 9/28/26Hearing

      River Stone wants to build 32 residential units with a roadway and infrastructure at Viking Lane/Ward Street under M.G.L. Ch. 40B (the state’s affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning). The Conservation Commission’s hearing is on the project’s wetlands permit application (a notice of intent), continued from 9/28/26.
