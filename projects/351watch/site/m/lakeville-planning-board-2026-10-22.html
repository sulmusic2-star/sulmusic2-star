Lakeville Planning Board

When
Thu Oct 22, 6:30 pm
Where
Lakeville Police Station Meeting Room, 323 Bedford Street
Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak. After the hearing the Planning Board makes a recommendation; Town Meeting votes on the bylaw.
Agenda
Open the agenda

On the agenda

    • Battery storage (BESS) zoning bylaw: Planning Board hearing and possible voteHearing

      Proposed amendments to the zoning bylaw would cover battery energy storage systems (BESS). The Planning Board’s public hearing is set for 6:30, and the board may vote on its recommendation.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 Planning Board agendas and the Sep 15 and Oct 20 Select Board agendas (the Oct 20 item: send the bylaw to the Planning Board for a hearing).

      Topics
      Battery storage, zoning changes
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Public Hearing (6:30) — Proposed amendments to the Zoning Bylaw — Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) — Discuss and Possible Vote

    • New rules for battery storage plants: Planning Board hearingHearing

      A new zoning section (§270-12) would regulate battery energy storage systems: permit rules by tier (Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3), a noise study and noise limits, commissioning and decommissioning plans, a decommissioning fund, proof of insurance, safety, what happens on ownership change or abandonment, site plan applications and special permit criteria.

      Before this: A BESS draft was on the Planning Board’s Sep 10 and Sep 24 agendas; the Select Board read and commented on the BESS bylaw Sep 15; the Planning Board met with Town Counsel on edits Oct 8.

      Topics
      Battery storage
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      This Article would amend the Lakeville Town Code: Zoning By-Law and would add a new section _§270-12, Battery Energy _ Storage Systems-BESS. This new bylaw would create regulations for the installation and use of battery energy storage systems

    • Two new zoning bylaws, battery storage and open-space subdivisions: hearingHearing

      One hearing on two new zoning sections: §270-12, rules for battery energy storage systems, and §270-13, Open Space and Residential Development (OSRD), which would let subdivisions use smaller lots in return for permanently protected open space, with a special permit, a five-step design process, and subsidized and senior housing parts.

      Before this: The Select Board voted Oct 6 on sending the OSRD bylaw to the Planning Board; the BESS draft was on Planning Board agendas Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 and before the Select Board Sep 15.

      Topics
      Zoning changes

      The Select Board voted Oct 6 to send the OSRD bylaw to the Planning Board for a hearing and a report at the Special Town Meeting.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      will hold a public hearing to discuss proposed amendments to the town's zoning by-laws. The public hearing will be held as follows: Place: Lakeville Police Station-Meeting Room, 323 Bedford Street, Lakeville, MA Date: Thursday. October 22, 2026 Time: 6:30 P.M.

    Words on the agenda. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Decommissioning: taking a project down at the end of its life and restoring the land. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
