    • Changes to the approved solar farm at 391 Church St: board may vote

      RI Church St Solar, LLC (Ontario, CA) asks for a major change to its already-approved solar energy facility plan at 391 Church Street, Wood River Junction. The agenda doesn't say what the changes are.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 24 Planning Board agenda.

      Solar391 Church Street, Wood River Junction, RI 02894RI Church St Solar, LLCPossible vote

      The board 'will discuss, consider, and possibly vote' on the changes the same night.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      RI Church St Solar, LLC, 401 S. Doubleday Avenue, Ontario, CA 91761 (applicant/owner) for proposed changes to an approved solar energy facility plan for property located at 391 Church Street, Wood River Junction, RI 02894 (Assessor’s Plat 11A, Lot 5). The Planning Board will discuss, consider, and possibly vote on the applicant’s proposed changes to the previously approved plan.

    • 4.5 MW solar farm proposed at 299 Church St: early review, no vote

      Charbert Solar 3, LLC wants to build a 4.5 MW AC (5.625 MW DC) solar farm at 299 Church Street, Wood River Junction, on land owned by F & E Enterprises, LLC. This is a pre-application review; no votes will be taken.
