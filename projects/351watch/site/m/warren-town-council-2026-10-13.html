Warren Town Council

Tue Oct 13, 7 pm Town Hall, or join online Open the agenda

Public comment period at the start. Written comments to be read into the record must reach Town Clerk Sandrea Speroni (ssperoni@townofwarren-ri.gov) by 4:00 pm on Oct 13.

    • Rural Business zone use list, including cell antennas on existing buildings: 1st reading

      Ordinance O-2 sets out the uses allowed by special use permit in the Rural Business zoning district. The list includes telecom antennas attached to existing structures, along with offices, vet hospitals, restaurants and others. The agenda doesn't say what is new in the list.

      Cell towers

      First reading only. The agenda's other zoning item shows the pattern: a public hearing comes at second reading. No date is given.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      O-2 Chapter 32-ZONING, Article XXVIII.-Rural Business Zoning District, Sec. 32-166.-Permitted Uses. (1st reading) All uses permitted within the rural business district shall require a special use permit, unless otherwise indicated.
