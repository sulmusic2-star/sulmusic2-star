Truro Select Board

Tue Oct 13, 3:30 pm Truro Town Hall, or join online Open the agenda

    • Truro hires a lawyer to negotiate taxes and benefits for the NextGrid solar project

      The Select Board votes, on its consent agenda, to appoint Matthew J. Thomas as special counsel to negotiate taxes and community benefits for the NextGrid solar project on the Noons property. This agenda gives no size or address for the project.

      Before this: Our Oct 7 Planning Board record: NextGrid Pecan LLC, 2 Sand Pit Road and 9 Noons Drive, site plan approval and special permit for an approximately 7.68 MW DC ground-mounted solar array with battery storage; hearing continued.

      SolarNoons PropertyMatthew J. Thomas; NextGridVote

      If appointed, the lawyer negotiates a tax and community benefits deal with the developer; no timeline is given.
