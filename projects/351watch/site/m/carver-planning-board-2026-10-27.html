      To consider proposed amendments to the Carver Zoning Bylaw and Zoning Map to establish a new Article 4-13, Smart Growth Overlay District.

    Words on the agenda. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Assessors Map and Lot: the town’s own number for the piece of land. 40R district: a smart-growth zoning district for denser housing with a share kept affordable, which the state pays the town to adopt. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Overlay district: a zoning layer with extra rules on top of the zone underneath.
