Mon Oct 19, 6 pm

    • New Roof-mounted wind turbines in every zoning district: joint hearing (not a solar item)Hearing

      This section adds roof-mounted wind energy systems to the dimensional note that already covers medium-scale ground mounted solar energy systems, and allows them 5 feet over the building height limit. It is a wind ordinance; the solar rules are only referenced.

    • New Small roof-mounted wind turbines allowed in every zoning district: joint public hearingHearing

      Salem would amend its zoning ordinance to allow roof-mounted wind energy systems (up to 10 kilowatts per turbine and up to twenty (20) feet tall) as an accessory use in every zoning district, let them rise 5 feet above a building’s height limit, and add definitions. This amended notice corrects the height from ten to twenty (20) feet.

      Before this: Referred by the City Council at its Sep 10 meeting.

      Solar, zoning changesup to 10 kilowatts per turbine; height up to twenty (20) feet; 5 feet over the building height limit

      Planned motions after the hearing close it and refer the ordinance to the Planning Board for its recommendation.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      SUMMARY Ordinance relative to Zoning - Roof-Mounted wind Energy Systems Ordinance Section 1. Section 3.1 Table of Principal and Accessory is hereby amended by inserting a new use in section E. ACCESSORY USES … shall be considered those with a rated nameplate capacity less than or equal to 10 kilowatts per turbine, and a height up to twenty (20) feet.
