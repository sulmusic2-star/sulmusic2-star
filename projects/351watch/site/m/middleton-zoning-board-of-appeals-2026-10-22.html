    • 40B housing at 35 Village Road: hearing continued to Oct 29, no discussion Oct 22Postponed to Thu Oct 29

      The applicant wants a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build multi-family housing at 35 Village Road, plus a special permit and site plan approval to change parking requirements, shrink the landscape buffer and adjust lot lines for the project.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 24 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda, when it was likewise continued without discussion.

      Affordable housing35 Village Road

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Continued Public Hearings … 35 Village Road – Applicant is requesting a Comprehensive Permit for the creation of multi-family housing [No discussion planned] … PLEASE NOTE: The ongoing public hearings for 35 Village Rd have been continued to October 29, 2026 and will not be discussed at the October 22, 2026 meeting.

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
