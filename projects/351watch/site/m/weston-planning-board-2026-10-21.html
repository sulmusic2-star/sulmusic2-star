On the agenda

    • Rezone 28 acres at 300 Wellesley St for 215 age-restricted homes: Planning Board hearingHearing

      HYM Investment Group LLC asks to rezone a 28.251-acre part of the Regis College land at 300 Wellesley St (now a Residence A zone) to allow 215 units of age-restricted multifamily housing.

      Before this: Planning Board site inspection Sep 11; design charette and continued hearing Sep 16; continued hearing Oct 7. The Select Board discussed the Regis/HYM project Sep 29 and Oct 13.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Where

      Size
