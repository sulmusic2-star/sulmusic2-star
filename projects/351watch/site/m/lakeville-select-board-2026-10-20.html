Lakeville Select Board

When
Tue Oct 20, 5:45 pm
Where
Lakeville Police Station 323 Bedford Street, Lakeville
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • Battery storage (BESS) bylaw: Select Board vote to send it to the Planning BoardPossible vote

      The Select Board may vote to send the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Bylaw to the Planning Board, which would hold a hearing and report at the Special Town Meeting. The same agenda has votes to accept an article for, and to sign, the Special Town Meeting warrant for November 18, 2026.

      Before this: The Select Board read and commented on the bylaw Sep 15; the draft was on the Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 Planning Board agendas.

      Topics
      Battery storage

      If sent, the Planning Board would hold a hearing and report to the Special Town Meeting; the warrant on this agenda is for November 18, 2026.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Discussion and Possible Vote to send the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Bylaw to the Planning Board to Hold a Hearing and Provide a Report at the Special Town Meeting.

    Words on the agenda. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
