Marshfield Town Meeting

Mon Oct 19, 7 pm

Marshfield High School Auditorium

Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.

Add this meeting to my calendar

    • Buy 30.98 acres off Holly Road for open space, $1,183,000: Town Meeting vote (Article 6)Vote

      The town would spend $1,183,000 in Community Preservation money to buy 30.98 acres of vacant land off Holly Road and protect it forever. The warrant says more open space helps the town avoid mandatory 40B housing developments.

      Before this: The Select Board voted on purchase and sale agreements for land off Holly Road and 1 Proctor Street at its Sep 21 meeting.

      Affordable housingoff Holly Road, Marshfield30.98 acres; $1,183,000 ($1,125,000 land purchase, $58,000 acquisition and restriction costs)Chestnut Hill Estates, LLC

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 6 Will the Town vote to appropriate the sum of $58,000 in acquisition and restriction costs, and $1,125,000 in land purchase expense, for a total appropriation of $1,183,000 from CPC Undesignated reserves to enable the Town to acquire for water resource protection, open space, passive recreation, and/or conservation purposes under Mass. Gen. L. Chapter 44B, the Community Preservation Act

    • Brant Rock Village Overlay removal, Article 9: citizen petition, Town Meeting voteVote

      A citizen petition from Kathleen Creed and others would do away with the Brant Rock Village Overlay District, an extra layer of zoning rules for the Brant Rock village area, including removing its references and boundaries from the zoning maps.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s public hearing on the petition was on its Oct 5 agenda.

      Zoning changesBrant Rock Village Overlay DistrictKathleen Creed, et al.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Petition Article – Kathleen Creed … including removal of the Brant Rock Village Overlay District references and boundaries from our Zoning maps. Petition of Kathleen Creed, et al.

    Words on the warrant. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. Overlay district: a zoning layer with extra rules on top of the zone underneath.
