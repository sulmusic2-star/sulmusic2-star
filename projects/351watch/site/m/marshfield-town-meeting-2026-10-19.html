Marshfield Town Meeting

Mon Oct 19, 7 pm Marshfield High School Auditorium Open the warrant (Town Meeting’s agenda) (PDF)

    • Buy 30.98 acres off Holly Road for open space, $1,183,000: Town Meeting vote (Article 6)

      The town would spend $1,183,000 in Community Preservation money to buy 30.98 acres of vacant land off Holly Road and protect it forever. The warrant says more open space helps the town avoid mandatory 40B housing developments.

      Before this: The Select Board voted on purchase and sale agreements for land off Holly Road and 1 Proctor Street at its Sep 21 meeting.

      Affordable housingoff Holly Road, Marshfield30.98 acres; $1,183,000 ($1,125,000 land purchase, $58,000 acquisition and restriction costs)Chestnut Hill Estates, LLCVote

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 6 Will the Town vote to appropriate the sum of $58,000 in acquisition and restriction costs, and $1,125,000 in land purchase expense, for a total appropriation of $1,183,000 from CPC Undesignated reserves to enable the Town to acquire for water resource protection, open space, passive recreation, and/or conservation purposes under Mass. Gen. L. Chapter 44B, the Community Preservation Act
