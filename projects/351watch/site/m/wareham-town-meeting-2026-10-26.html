Wareham Town Meeting

Mon Oct 26, 7 pm Wareham High School Open the warrant (PDF)

    • Add more Main Street lots to the Wareham Village Reinvestment zone: vote (Article 15).

      Article 15 expands the Wareham Village Reinvestment Subdistrict (WV-1R) on the zoning map to take in the lots listed in Schedule A, mostly on Main Street plus 8 Chapel Street, 14 Ladner Lane and 5 Sawyer Street. It does not change the district's rules.

      Zoning changes8 Chapel Street, 14 Ladner Lane, 348 Main Street, 330 Main Street, 332 Main Street, 5 Sawyer Street, 304 Main Street, 329 Main Street, 335 Main Street, 337 Main Street, 341 Main Street, 355 Main Street, 355R Main Street, 361 Main Street, 367 Main Street, 379 Main Street, 411 Main StreetVote

      Zoning changes normally need a two-thirds vote, then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 15 — BYLAW AMENDMENT EXPANSION OF THE WAREHAM VILLAGE REINVESTMENT SUBDISTRICT (WV-1R) To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Map of the Town of Wareham by expanding the boundaries of the existing Wareham Village: Reinvestment Subdistrict (WV-1R) to include the lots identified in Schedule A and depicted on Exhibit A, each attached hereto and incorporated by reference.

    Open Town Meeting: legal voters of Wareham qualified to vote in Town affairs attend and vote.Warrant (PDF): Wareham Town Meeting, Mon Oct 26
