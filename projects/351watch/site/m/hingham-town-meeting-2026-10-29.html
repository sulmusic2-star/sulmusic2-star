    • South Elementary roof, Article 1: Town Meeting vote; built solar-ready (not a solar item)Vote

      Article 1 asks Town Meeting to approve up to $8,576,727 to replace the South Elementary School roof, with the state’s school building authority (MSBA) expected to pay back about $2,872,787. The roof framing would be reinforced so solar panels could go on it later: the MSBA grant requires a solar-ready area, and the town is not obligated to install panels.

      SolarSouth Elementary Schoolup to $8,576,727 (MSBA reimbursement estimated at $2,872,787; town share about $5,703,940)
