Heath Planning Board

When
Mon Nov 2, 7 pm
Where
Online meeting

Agenda
Open the agenda (PDF) (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Battery storage, large solar and data center bylaws: Planning Board discussionDiscussion

      The board works on new bylaws for battery energy storage systems (BESS) and large-scale solar, with guest Allison Gage from FRCOG, then on data center bylaws and changes to its special permit procedures and requirements, including the bylaw work and its schedule.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 7 Planning Board agenda, with a note that FRCOG money and assistance are available for new bylaws.

      Topics
      Solar, battery storage, data centers
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      4. Discuss battery energy storage systems (BESS) and large-scale solar bylaws. Guest - Allison Gage from FRCOG. 5. Discuss data center bylaws and updates to special permit procedures and requirements, including bylaw work and scheduling.

    Words on the agenda. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
