Franklin Conservation

When
Thu Oct 22, 7 pm
Where
Town Council Chambers, second floor of the Municipal Building, or on Zoom
Agenda

On the agenda

    • Friendly 40B LIP: Conservation Commission general business item

      A friendly 40B LIP is a general business item for the Conservation Commission: a town-supported affordable-housing project under the state’s local initiative program, where the comprehensive permit can override local zoning.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 Conservation Commission agendas.

      Topics
      Affordable housing
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      GENERAL BUSINESS 2.1 Friendly 40B Lip

    Words on the agenda. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning.
