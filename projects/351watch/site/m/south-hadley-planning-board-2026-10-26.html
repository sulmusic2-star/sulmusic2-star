South Hadley Planning Board

When
Mon Oct 26, 6:30 pm
Where
Multipurpose room, South Hadley Senior Center, 45 Dayton Street, or on Zoom
Public comment
Anyone interested or wishing to be heard should appear in person or join online at the time stated. After the hearing the Planning Board makes a recommendation; Town Meeting votes on the bylaw.
Agenda
Open the agenda

On the agenda

    • Ban data centers in every zone of South Hadley: Planning Board hearingHearing

      The change would add a definition of “data center” to the zoning bylaw (Section 255-10) and list “Data Center” in the use table as an industrial use that is prohibited in all zoning districts.

      Before this: The Selectboard discussed a data center moratorium Sep 22 (with a “Data Center Prohibition” document in its packet) and held a listening session on data centers Oct 6.

      Topics
      Data centers, zoning changes
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      to consider amendments to the Zoning Bylaw to prohibit the construction of data centers by amending Section 255-10 Terms defined and inserting a definition for "data center," and by amending Attachment 1 Use Regulations Schedule by adding a new use under Industrial Uses called "Data Center" and prohibiting it in all zoning districts.
