Hubbardston Town Meeting

Tue Oct 27, 7 pm Hubbardston Center School Open the warrant (Town Meeting’s agenda)

    • Zoning updates to match new state law on variances, grandfathering: vote (Article 11)

      Article 11 rewrites parts of the zoning bylaw to match recent changes to the state Zoning Act: variances would turn on "practical difficulty" instead of "substantial hardship", some additions to older nonconforming homes would be allowed without a special permit, a nonconforming use would be lost after four years of abandonment instead of two, and a permit's protection would run twenty-four months instead of six.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing Sep 17 on revising the zoning bylaw for consistency with the state budget bill's changes on entitlements, non-conformities and variances.

      Zoning changesVote

      Two-thirds vote required (the warrant says so), then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 11. ZONING BYLAW ARTICLES REVISIONS TO SECTIONS 2.32, 4.2, 4.9, 4.10, 4.12, 10.1, AND 10.4 TO CONFORM THE ZONING BYLAW TO AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING ACT, G.L. c. 40A (Two-thirds vote required) To see if the Town will vote to amend the Hubbardston Zoning Bylaws Sections 2.32, 4.2, 4.9, 4.10, 4.11, 4.12, 10.1, 10.4, and 17.9

    Warrant: Hubbardston Town Meeting, Tue Oct 27
