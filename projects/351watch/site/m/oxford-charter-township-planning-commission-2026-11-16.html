Data-center ordinance

Mon Nov 16, at 6:30 pm, at Oxford High School. The Planning Commission holds a public hearing. Anyone can go and speak. Also on the agenda, it holds a hearing on data-center rules and talks about battery storage rules.

Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak. After the hearing the Planning Commission makes a recommendation; the Township Board adopts the ordinance after the county planning commission reviews it.
