Sutton Town Meeting

When
Mon Oct 19, 7 pm
Where
The Middle/High School, 383 Boston Road
Who votes
Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.
Warrant
Open the Town Meeting warrant

On the warrant

    • Accessory dwelling units replace accessory apartments, Article 9: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Planning Board’s article would replace the zoning bylaw’s accessory apartment section with rules for accessory dwelling units (ADUs). A Protected Use ADU, allowed by right under state law, could be up to half the main home’s floor area or 900 square feet, whichever is smaller, with a Building Department permit. A Large Accessory Dwelling Unit (LADU) of up to 1,200 square feet would need a special permit. One ADU is allowed per lot, and either kind may be a short-term rental if the owner lives on the property. The Finance and Warrant Advisory Committee voted 7-0 and the Planning Board 5-0-0 to recommend it.

      Before this: The Planning Board had a minor adjustment to the proposed ADU article on its Sep 14 agenda.

    • Rezone parts of 430 and 434 Putnam Hill Road from R-1 to Village: vote (Article 12)Vote

      A citizen petition by Mitchell Cohen to change the zoning map so the Rural Residential (R-1) parts of 430 and 434 Putnam Hill Road become Village (V) zoning. A no vote leaves them R-1.

      Before this: Printed as Article 12 in the Fall 2026 Town Meeting warrant; the Select Board approved and signed the warrant Sep 22.
