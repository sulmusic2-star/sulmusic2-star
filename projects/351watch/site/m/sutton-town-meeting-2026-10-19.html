Sutton Town Meeting

Mon Oct 19, 7 pm High School Open the warrant

    • Rezone parts of 430 and 434 Putnam Hill Road from R-1 to Village: vote (Article 12).

      A citizen petition by Mitchell Cohen to change the zoning map so the Rural Residential (R-1) parts of 430 and 434 Putnam Hill Road become Village (V) zoning. A no vote leaves them R-1.

      Before this: Printed as Article 12 in the Fall 2026 Town Meeting warrant; the Select Board approved and signed the warrant Sep 22.

      Zoning changes430 Putnam Hill Road (M49-P226) and 434 Putnam Hill Road (M49-P224)Mitchell CohenVote

      Zoning changes normally need a two-thirds vote, then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Changes the zoning map for the Rural Residential (R-1) portions of 430 Putnam Hill Road (M49-P226) and 434 Putnam Hill Road (M49-P224).

    Warrant: Sutton Town Meeting, Mon Oct 19
