On the agenda

    • 95 cottage rentals at 27 Kimball Road: 40B hearing, request to continue to 11/19/26Asked to move

      MP Properties IV, LLC wants a Comprehensive Permit (the state’s Chapter 40B permit that lets an affordable-housing project override local zoning) to build a 95-unit multifamily cottage rental development of 1, 2 and 3 bedroom units on a 9.4 acre property at 27 Kimball Road, in the R-40 zoning district. 25% of the units would be deed-restricted for low or moderate income housing.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 24 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda.

      Topics
      Affordable housing
      Where
      27 Kimball Road, Amesbury, MA (Map 73 and Lot 19)
      Size
      95 units (1, 2 and 3 bedroom) on 9.4 acres; 25% deed-restricted for low or moderate income housing
      Applicant
      MP Properties IV, LLC

      The board decides at this meeting whether to postpone; until it does, the hearing is still on.

      A request to continue the hearing to the 11/19/26 ZBA meeting is on the agenda.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      2. 27 Kimball Road – Comprehensive Permit MP Properties IV, LLC has submitted an application for a Comprehensive Permit pursuant to M.G.L. Chapter 40B Section 20-23. The proposed project is to construct a 95-unit multifamily cottage rental development … 25% of the proposed housing units will be deed-restricted for low or moderate income housing. Request to Continue to the 11/19/26 ZBA Meeting
