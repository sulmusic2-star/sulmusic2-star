Southbridge Town Council

When
Mon Oct 19, 7 pm
Where
Robert G. MacKinnon Council Chambers
Public comment
Citizens Forum is item 8 on the agenda.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • 365-day moratorium on data centers, zoning Section 8.11: third reading and council voteVote

      Third reading and final council vote on a 365-day moratorium on permitting, building, expanding or operating any data center in town, a new Section 8.11 of the zoning bylaw; no data center application would be accepted and pending ones would be stayed. The council could extend it twice for up to 180 days each, and the pause is meant to give the town time to study water, grid, noise and siting standards.

      Before this: First reading Sep 21 and second reading Oct 5 at the Town Council; the Sep 16 Town Council and Planning Board agendas carried the notice of the proposed data center bylaw changes.

      Topics
      Data centers, moratoriums

      If adopted, the moratorium begins the first day after the council’s adoption of the bylaw.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Amendments to the Town of Southbridge Zoning Bylaw Section 8, Special Regulations by adding a new section 8.11 Temporary Moratorium for Data Centers. First Reading: September 21, 2026 Second Reading: October 5, 2026 Third Reading: October 19, 2026 Section 8, Special Regulations, is hereby amended by adding the following new section 8.11: 8.11 TEMPORARY MORATORIUM FOR DATA CENTERS

    • Data center defined in the zoning bylaw, Section 10.1: third reading and council voteVote

      Third reading and final council vote on adding a definition of Data Center to Section 10.1 of the zoning bylaw. The definition covers hyperscale, enterprise, co-location and edge data centers, server farms, modular or containerized data systems, and cryptocurrency or blockchain mining when that is the main use, and excludes ordinary server rooms that support an office, school, store or medical building.

      Before this: First reading Sep 21 and second reading Oct 5 at the Town Council; the Sep 16 Town Council and Planning Board agendas carried the notice of the proposed data center bylaw changes.

      Topics
      Data centers
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Amendments to the Town of Southbridge Zoning Bylaw Section 10.1 Definitions by adding the definition of Data Center. First Reading: September 21, 2026 Second Reading: October 5, 2026 Third Reading: October 19, 2026 Data Center: A facility or campus consisting of one or more buildings primarily used for housing, operating, and co-locating networked computer systems

    • Worcester Polytechnic research on the data center moratorium: council vote to partnerVote

      The Town Council votes whether to partner with Worcester Polytechnic Institute to host an Interactive Qualifying Project, a student research project, doing research for the Southbridge Planning Board on the data center moratorium.

      Before this: The Planning Board discussed participation in the Worcester Polytechnic Institute IQP program on Oct 7.

      Topics
      Data centers, moratoriums
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Vote to authorize partnering with Worcester Polytechnic Institute to host an Interactive Qualifying Project to do research on behalf of the Southbridge Planning Board regarding the data center moratorium.

    Words on the agenda. Moratorium: a temporary ban.
