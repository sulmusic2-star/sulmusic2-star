    Words on the agenda. Buffer Zone: the land next to a wetland (the first 100 feet under state law; towns set their own lines inside it), where work needs the Conservation Commission’s review. Request for Determination: asks the Conservation Commission whether the wetlands law applies to a piece of work. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning.
