Open the agenda (first seen Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • Battery storage plant proposed at 1606 Riverside Ave: hearingHearing

      Somerset Energy Storage, LLC wants zoning approval for a Planned Development under Section 6.10 of the zoning by-laws to build a commercial Battery Energy Storage System (B.E.S.S.) at 1606 Riverside Avenue, in the Industrial Zoning District and the Business Industrial Overlay District. The land is owned by Somerset Riverside 1606, LLC.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 17, Oct 1, Oct 15, Oct 29 and Nov 5 Zoning Board of Appeals agendas.

      Topics
      Battery storage
      Where
      1606 Riverside Avenue, Assessor’s Map B2, Lot 203, Somerset, MA
      Applicant

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      PUBLIC HEARINGS: Somerset Energy Storage, LLC for 1606 Riverside Ave., Somerset, MA: petition of Somerset Energy Storage, LLC; regarding the property owned by Somerset Riverside 1606, LLC, and located at 1606 Riverside Avenue, Assessor’s Map B2, Lot 203, for a Planned Development under Section 6.10 of the Town of Somerset Zoning By-laws

    Words on the agenda. Overlay district: a zoning layer with extra rules on top of the zone underneath. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
