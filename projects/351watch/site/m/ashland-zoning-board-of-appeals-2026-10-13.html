Ashland Zoning Board of Appeals, Tue Oct 13, 7 pm

  1. Tue Oct 13, 7 pm Ashland Town Hall, or join online

    • Affordable housing

      The Zoning Board of Appeals will hold a public hearing on an affordable housing permit (40B) for 116 units of housing by 55 West Union LLC at 55 West Union Street.

      Public hearing.

      Can I speak?

      Yes. Anyone can attend a public hearing and ask to speak. You can also send written comments to the board through the town clerk before the meeting. Ask the clerk about sign-up rules and time limits.
