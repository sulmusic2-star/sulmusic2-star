Ashland Zoning Board of Appeals

Tue Oct 13, 7 pm Ashland Town Hall, or join online Open the agenda

Public hearing, in person or by Zoom.

    • 116 apartments (29 affordable) at 55 West Union St: 40B hearing continues

      55 West Union LLC wants a comprehensive permit (the state's 40B affordable-housing permit) for one four-story elevator building with 116 rental units, 87 at market rate and 29 kept affordable forever for households at or below 80% of area median income, on 4.9 acres in the Commercial Highway district. The agenda's own list shows sessions since August 26, 2025, most marked 'Continued Public Hearing; Matter was not discussed'; the last with the applicant present was September 8, 2026.
