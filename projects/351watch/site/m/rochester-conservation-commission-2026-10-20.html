On the agenda

    • Solar array and battery storage at 0 Walnut Plain Road: hearing, meeting canceledCanceled

      Walnut Plain Road Solar 1, LLC filed a Notice of Intent (the wetlands permit application) for a ground mounted solar array with a battery energy storage system at 0 Walnut Plain Road, with work inside the 100-foot buffer zone to a Bordering Vegetated Wetland. The October 20 meeting is cancelled; the commission next meets November 3, 2026.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 and Oct 6 Conservation Commission agendas; the Planning Board’s special permit and site plan review for the project was on its Sep 22 and Oct 13 agendas.

      Topics

      Where

      Applicant

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      DEP SE 272-0668 A Notice of Intent filed by Walnut Plain Road Solar 1, LLC, 55 Technology Drive, Lowell, MA 01851 for property located at 0 Walnut Plain Road, Map 14, Lot 0001. The applicant is proposing work within the 100-foot buffer zone to Bordering Vegetated Wetland. The proposed development includes a ground mounted solar array system with associated battery energy storage system.
