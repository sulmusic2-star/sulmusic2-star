Salem City Council

Mon Oct 19, 6 pm City Council Chambers, or join online Open the agenda

    • Rooftop wind turbine ordinance; solar is only a neighboring line (not a solar item).

      The words "medium-scale ground mounted solar energy systems" appear only because the new wind line is inserted next to it in the dimensional-rules notes; nothing about solar changes.

      Before this: On Sep 10 the City Council's Committee on Ordinances, Licenses and Legal Affairs recommended referring the Roof-Mounted Wind Energy Systems ordinance to the Planning Board for a joint public hearing.

      Solaradditional allowance of 5 feet over the maximum height of the buildingHearing

      The draft record shows motions to close the hearing and refer the matter to the Planning Board for its recommendation.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Section 2. Section 4.1.2 Notes to Table of Dimensional Requirements is hereby amended by inserting the line “and roof-mounted wind energy systems” after “medium-scale ground mounted solar energy systems” in subsection 8

    • Small rooftop wind turbines allowed in every zone: joint council-Planning Board hearing.

      The ordinance would add roof-mounted wind energy systems as an allowed accessory use in every zoning district, define them (rated at 10 kilowatts or less per turbine), and let them rise 5 feet above a building's height limit.

      Before this: On Sep 10 the City Council's Committee on Ordinances, Licenses and Legal Affairs recommended referring the Roof-Mounted Wind Energy Systems ordinance to the Planning Board for a joint public hearing.

      Zoning changesrated nameplate capacity less than or equal to 10 kilowatts per turbine; additional allowance of 5 feet over the maximum height of the buildingHearing

      The draft record shows motions to close the hearing and refer the matter to the Planning Board for its recommendation.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      The public hearing is to provide interested parties with an opportunity to comment on these Zoning Amendments. The complete text of this zoning ordinance amendment is available for inspection below, in the back-up documentation, at the City Clerk’s office, 93 Washington Street, Salem or the Planning Department, City Hall Annex, 98 Washington Street, Salem (during normal working hours).

    In person is the main way to take part; Zoom and SATV are backups.Agenda: Salem City Council, Mon Oct 19
