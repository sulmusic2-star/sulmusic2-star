On the warrant

    • Data center rules, by size tier, in the zoning bylaw: Town Meeting vote (Article 19)Vote

      Article 19, a petition, would add a definition of “Data Center” to the zoning bylaw (section 240-3.2) and sort data centers into tiers by electrical demand (Tier I is the small, local or edge-computing kind). The rest of the article, including what each tier would allow, is in the full warrant.
