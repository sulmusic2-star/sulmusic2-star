Falmouth Town Meeting

Mon Oct 26 Choose Town Office Open the warrant

    • Data center rules, by size tier, in the zoning bylaw: Town Meeting vote (Article 19).

      Article 19, a petition, would add a definition of "Data Center" to the zoning bylaw (section 240-3.2) and sort data centers into tiers by electrical demand (Tier I is the small, local or edge-computing kind). The rest of the article, including what each tier would allow, is cut off in our copy.

      Data centers, zoning changesVote

      Zoning changes normally need a two-thirds vote, then Attorney General review.

      Quoted from the warrant:

      To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Bylaws as follows: 1. By inserting new definition for Data Center to section 240-3.2 to read as follows: Data Center: A facility, or portion thereof, primarily designed or used to house

    • Mentions data centers: “A Data Center may include, but is not limited to, server racks, data storage systems, cooling systems, power conditioning equipment, substations, uninterruptible”
