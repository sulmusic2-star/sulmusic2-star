Newport City Council

Wed Oct 14, 6:30 pm City Hall Open the agenda

Public hearing. (Citizens' Forum, sign in with the City Clerk, 3 minutes, cannot be used for agenda items.)

    • Newport zoning changes (permitted uses; section 17.65 rules): hearing, first reading

      Amendments to the zoning ordinance's 'Permitted Uses' (17.14.020) and to section 17.65's use regulations and dimensional requirements (17.65.020, 17.65.030), with the Planning Board's recommendation; our Oct 5 Planning Board record ties these to a new Waterfront Campus subdistrict in the Innovation Hub district and the rezoning of Plat 9, Lot 219 (item 2 on this agenda).

      Before this: Aug 26 City Council: 'Waterfront Campus- Innovation Hub Zoning Amendments' received and referred to the Planning Board. Oct 5 Planning Board: review and recommendation on the 'Application of the City of Newport' for the amendments, covering an approximately 8.5-acre parcel at Plat 9, Lot 219.
