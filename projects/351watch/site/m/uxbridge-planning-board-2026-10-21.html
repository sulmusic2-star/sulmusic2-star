On the agenda

    • Marijuana “social consumption” ban stays, with wording updates: Planning Board hearingHearing

      The warrant article clarifies that marijuana social consumption establishments (places where people use marijuana on site) stay prohibited in Uxbridge, and updates §400-23 of the zoning bylaws to match state law. The notice doesn’t print the changes.

      Before this: Printed as Article 18 in the posted Fall Town Meeting warrant; the Select Board and Finance Committee reviewed it at their Oct 6 joint meeting.

      Topics
      Zoning changes

      Town Meeting votes on the warrant article;

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      ARTICLE 21; CLARIFYING THE EXISTING PROHIBITION ON MARIJUANA SOCIAL CONSUMPTION ESTABLISHMENTS AND MAKING AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING BYLAWS CONSISTENT WITH STATE LAW To see if the Town will vote to amend Chapter 400, Zoning Bylaws, §400-23, Marijuana Establishments and Medical Marijuana Treatment as shown below; or take any action relative thereto.
