Easthampton Conservation Commission

When
Mon Oct 19, 6 pm
Where
Municipal Offices, 50 Payson Avenue, Basement Conference Room, or join online
Agenda
Open the agenda (PDF) (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Solar canopy at Lathrop Community, 100 Bassett Brook Drive: open permit compliance updateDiscussion

      Lathrop Community of Easthampton’s permit (MassDEP File#151-0338) for a solar canopy, a maintenance shed and a trash enclosure at 100 Bassett Brook Drive is on the commission’s list of active projects and open permits for a compliance update.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 and Sep 28 Conservation Commission agendas.

    • Beacon Solar large-scale solar project, 50 Florence Road: open permit compliance updateDiscussion

      Beacon Solar, LLC’s large-scale solar project at 50 Florence Road (MassDEP File # 151-314, Map 115, Lots 2 and 5) is on the commission’s list of active projects and open permits for a compliance update; the permit runs to 6/17/2027. The same lots are also under an enforcement order (EO-2025-001) against J. Gawle & Signal Energy.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 and Sep 28 Conservation Commission agendas.

    Words on the agenda. Assessors Map and Lot: the town’s own number for the piece of land. DEP File #: the state environmental agency’s number for the wetlands filing.
