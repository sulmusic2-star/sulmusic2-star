Taunton City Council

When
Tue Oct 20, 7 pm
Where
Chester R. Martin Municipal Council Chambers, 15 Summer Street
Public comment

Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • Data center rules (440-808) and other zoning changes: City Council hearingHearing

      The City of Taunton proposes zoning changes, dated September 8, 2026, that add data centers to the definitions and to the business and industrial use table and create a new section, 440-808, on data centers; the package also removes the Office District, rezones parcels, and changes rules on 3-family homes, inclusionary housing and landscaping. The Planning Board recommends the changes, and VMD Industrial III, LLC wrote about whether the proposed data center moratorium would apply to existing buildings.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a hearing on these changes on Oct 1; a City Planner request for a vote on a data center moratorium was on the Sep 15 City Council agenda.

      Topics
      Data centers, moratoriums
      Applicant
      City of Taunton
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      HEARING: On the petition submitted by the City of Taunton for the Proposed Changes to the City of Taunton Zoning Ordinance Dated September 8, 2026 … 440-201 Definitions — Add Data Center; 440 Attachment #2 -Table of Use Regulation Business and Industrial Regulations— Add Data Center; Add 440-808 — Data Centers;

    Words on the agenda. Moratorium: a temporary ban.
