    Words on the agenda. Notice of Intent: the application for a wetlands permit, filed with the Conservation Commission. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Assessors Map and Lot: the town’s own number for the piece of land. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system). Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
