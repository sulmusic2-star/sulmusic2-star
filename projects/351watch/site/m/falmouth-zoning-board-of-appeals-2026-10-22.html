Falmouth Zoning Board of Appeals

When
Thu Oct 22, 6 pm
Where
Select Board's Meeting Room, Town Hall, 59 Town Hall Square, Falmouth
Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • 12 homes in six duplexes, Broken Bow Lane: comprehensive permit hearing continuesHearing

      Broken Bow Residences, LLC wants a comprehensive permit (the state’s affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build twelve homeownership units in six duplex buildings on Broken Bow Lane in East Falmouth. Three of the units would be affordable.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 10 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda.

      Topics
      Affordable housing
      Where

      Size
      12 homeownership units in 6 duplex buildings; 3 affordable
      Applicant
      Broken Bow Residences, LLC
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Continuation(s) 6:00pm: #084-25 Broken Bow Residences, LLC, ‘0’ (lot 001A) Broken Bow Lane, East Falmouth – requesting a comprehensive permit to construct twelve (12) homeownership units within six (6) duplex buildings; three(3) units will be affordable (Murphy, Morse, Duffy, Finneran, Peterson)

    • 46 affordable units on Locustfield Road: comprehensive permit hearing continuesHearing

      Falmouth Housing Trust, Inc., Falmouth Housing Corporation and Affirmative Investments Inc. want a comprehensive permit (the state’s affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build forty-six units on Locustfield Road: forty in two connecting buildings and six in three duplex buildings. All of the units would be affordable.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 24 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda.

      Topics
      Affordable housing
      Where
      ‘0’ (lots 004 & 005) Locustfield Road, Falmouth
      Size
      46 units: 40 in 2 connecting buildings, 6 in 3 duplex buildings; all affordable
      Sponsor
      Falmouth Housing Trust, Inc., Falmouth Housing Corporation & Affirmative Investments Inc.
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      #042-26 Falmouth Housing Trust, Inc., Falmouth Housing Corporation & Affirmative Investments Inc., ‘0’ (lots 004 & 005) Locustfield Road, Falmouth – requesting a comprehensive permit to construct two (2) connecting buildings with forty (40) units plus three (3) duplex buildings with six (6) units totaling forty-six (46) units; all units will be affordable
