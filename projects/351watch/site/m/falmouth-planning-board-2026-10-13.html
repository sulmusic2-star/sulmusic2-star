Falmouth Planning Board

Tue Oct 13, 6:30 pm Town Hall Open the agenda

Public hearing; there is also a public comment period at the start. No time limit is stated.

    • Solar canopy over boat and RV storage at 709 and 737 Gifford St: hearing

      Brian Yergatian wants a special permit for relief from dimensional rules to build a solar carport (panels on a canopy) over the existing boat and RV storage area at 709 and 737 Gifford Street. A separate site plan review for the same canopy is on the same night’s agenda. Both applications were filed May 18, 2026; the site plan review was last before the board August 11, 2026.

      Before this: On Sep 22 the applicant asked to continue both applications to Oct 13.

      Solar709 and 737 Gifford StreetBrian YergatianHearing

      The board can vote or continue. The site plan review has a decision deadline of October 31, 2026.

      Applicant: Brian Yergatian —- Special Permit application to for relief from the dimensional regulations as applied to exempt uses regarding a proposed solar photovoltaic carport at 709 and 737 Gifford Street. Filed: May 18, 2026
