    • State-required zoning updates, incl. affordable-housing permits: Planning Board voteVote

      The board votes a recommendation to the Town Council on zoning changes required by laws the RI General Assembly passed in 2026: definitions, SAFE emergency units, unified review, variances and special use permits, modifications, parking, and Article 17 on how affordable housing comprehensive permit applications are reviewed.

      Affordable housing

      The recommendation goes to the Town Council, which decides on the ordinance.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Recommendation to the Town Council on proposed amendments to Town Code Chapter 152, zoning ordinance to address amendments required by legislation adopted in the 2026 session of the RI General Assembly, including Section 400 Definitions; adding Section 732 regarding Supportive and Functional Emergency (SAFE) units; Section 903 regarding unified review;

    Words on the agenda. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning.
