Middleton Planning Board

Wed Oct 14, 7 pm Middleton Municipal Complex Open the agenda

    • New parking, setback and development standards in Middleton zoning: hearing

      Zoning bylaw changes: new parking regulations, changes to the performance standards for new development, and revised setback regulations; this notice gives no detail.

      Before this: Our records also hold the Planning Board's fuller Oct 14 notice: right-sized parking counts, shared parking, electric vehicle ready spaces and pedestrian access in parking lots; accessory structures get the same setbacks as the primary structure and front setbacks can no longer be measured from the street centerline in R-1a, R-1b and RA; dark sky compliant light fixtures; and a continued hearing the same night on administrative edits and the special permit/site plan process. The ZBA's Oct 14 agenda carries the same list.

      Zoning changesHearing

      Full text: middletonma.gov/338/Planning-Board, or the Town Clerk and Planning Board offices at 105 South Main Street. Zoning changes need a two-thirds vote at Town Meeting; the date isn't stated.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Proposed amendments include new parking regulations; modifications to existing performance standards for new development; and revised setback regulations.
