Harvard Town Meeting

When
Tue Oct 20, 6 pm
Where
The Bromfield School Cronin Auditorium, 14 Massachusetts Ave
Who votes
Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.
Warrant
Open the Town Meeting warrant

On the warrant

    • Clean-energy bylaw for solar and battery storage, Article 5: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Planning Board asks Town Meeting to replace Harvard’s large-scale ground-mounted solar overlay district (§125-53) with a new Clean Energy Facilities bylaw: local siting standards and a consolidated permitting process for solar, and for the first time standards for battery energy storage systems (BESS). The Select Board unanimously recommends it; the Finance Committee took no position.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 Select Board and Sep 28 Planning Board agendas.

      Topics
      Solar, battery storage, clean-energy permit rules
      Sponsor
      Planning Board article
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 5: REPLACE CURRENT §125-53 WITH NEW CLEAN ENERGY FACILITIES BYLAW … 1. Replace §125-53 (Large-Scale Ground-Mounted Solar Photovoltaic Facilities Overlay District … The proposed bylaw establishes local siting standards and a consolidated permitting process for solar energy systems, and for the first time, includes standards for battery energy storage systems (BESS).

    • Inclusionary rule in the MBTA multifamily zone, Article 9: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Planning Board asks Town Meeting to amend §125-60, the MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District, to make clear how the inclusionary affordable housing requirement applies inside the district, with other corrections. The warrant says these refine an adopted district, not its core standards. The Select Board unanimously recommends it; the Finance Committee took no position.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 Select Board and Sep 28 Planning Board agendas.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Sponsor
      Planning Board article
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 9: REVISE §125-60 MBTA COMMUNITIES MULTI-FAMILY OVERLAY DISTRICT … Summary: This amendment clarifies how the inclusionary affordable housing requirement applies within the overlay district and makes other corrective and clarifying revisions. These are refinements to an already-adopted district, not a change to its core standards.

    Words on the warrant. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Decommissioning: taking a project down at the end of its life and restoring the land. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
