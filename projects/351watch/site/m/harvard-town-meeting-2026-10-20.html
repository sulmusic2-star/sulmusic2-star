    • New rules for solar farms and battery storage: Town Meeting vote (Article 5)Vote

      Article 5 replaces Harvard’s 2010 solar overlay district with a “Clean Energy Facilities” bylaw: local standards for where and how solar and, for the first time, battery storage can be built (public safety, scenic and natural resource protection, money set aside for decommissioning), plus one consolidated local permit, to fit the 2024 state permitting law and recent court rulings.

      Before this: The Planning Board listed the Clean Energy Facilities bylaw replacing §125-53 on its Sep 28 agenda; the Select Board reviewed the warrant article Sep 15.

      Solar, battery storage, clean-energy permit rulesPlanning Board article

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Insert a new §125-53 titled "Clean Energy Facilities," as set forth in the document on file with the Town Clerk, incorporating standards for Solar Photovoltaic Installations, Battery Energy Storage Systems, and Consolidated Local Permitting consistent with 225 CMR 29.00 and Chapter 239 of the Acts of 2024

    • Existing 40B permit wording in Harvard’s zoning bylaw (attachment text, not a project)

      This is existing bylaw text about comprehensive permits, printed in the attachments to the zoning articles (Articles 5-9). No 40B housing project is proposed.

      Affordable housingPlanning Board article

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Board of Appeals in accordance with the standards and procedures specified by law for comprehensive permits. [Added 3-27-1993 ATM by Art. 27]

    Words on the warrant. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. Overlay district: a zoning layer with extra rules on top of the zone underneath. Decommissioning: taking a project down at the end of its life and restoring the land. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
