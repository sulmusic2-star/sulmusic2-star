    • Battery storage at 600 Cow Pond Brook Road: hearing continuedPostponed

      The Planning Board continues its public hearing on a major site plan review for a battery energy storage system at 600 Cow Pond Brook Road, applied for by GELD and Lightshift Energy.

      Before this: The hearing opened on the Oct 8 Planning Board agenda; a construction contract for Cow Pond Brook was on the Oct 13 Select Board agenda.

      Battery storage600 Cow Pond Brook Road, Assessors Map 248, Parcel 41GELD & Lightshift Energy

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      7:00 PM Public Hearing continuation– Application for Major Site Plan Review, Battery Energy Storage System, 600 Cow Pond Brook Road, Assessors Map 248, Parcel 41 (GELD & Lightshift Energy)

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Assessors Map and Lot: the town’s own number for the piece of land. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
