Malden City Council

Tue Oct 13, 6 pm Malden City Hall Open the agenda

    • New zoning papers on hotels, supermarkets and restaurants: committee discussion

      Councillor Winslow will float drafting new papers for more use-table and dimensional changes to the zoning code (Title 12), possibly covering motel-hotel, supermarket and restaurant zoning, after discussions on zoning papers 335, 335A and 337. No formal votes are expected.

      Before this: Related papers: 327-26 (use regulations, Oct 6 committee agenda) and 335-26 (inclusionary zoning, Sep 15 and Oct 6 committee agendas).

      Zoning changesCouncillor WinslowDiscussion

      If new papers are drafted, they would go to the council; no date is given.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      As a result of ongoing discussions regarding zoning amendments in Papers 335, 335A, and 337, Councillor Winslow will introduce the option of drafting new papers to consider additional use regulation updates and/or dimensional controls necessary to Title 12. Subject matter may include, but may not be limited to, Motel-Hotel, Supermarket, and Restaurant zoning.

    Agenda: Malden City Council, Tue Oct 13
