On the agenda

    • Standalone battery storage system at 26 Pearl Street: wetlands hearing continuesHearing

      ZPB 2020-030, LLC, c/o Zero Point Development, Inc, wants Conservation Commission approval (an NOI, the wetlands permit application) to build a standalone energy storage system (ESS) at 26 Pearl Street, within the Riverfront Area and the 100-foot Buffer Zone to Bank and Isolated Vegetated Wetland. LEC Environmental Consultants, Inc. represents the applicant.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 23 and Oct 7 Conservation Commission agendas. The Planning Board’s hearing on the 26 Pearl St development plan was on its Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 agendas.

      7:00 PM C. Hearing NOI 26 Pearl Street Construction of a standalone ZPB 2020-030, LLC LEC Environmental energy storage system (ESS) c/o Zero Point Development, Inc Consultants, Inc. 105-1039 within Riverfront Area and 100- … foot Buffer Zone to Bank and Worcester, MA 01608 Worcester, MA 01605 Isolated Vegetated Wetland

    Words on the agenda. Notice of Intent: the application for a wetlands permit, filed with the Conservation Commission. Buffer Zone: the land next to a wetland (the first 100 feet under state law; towns set their own lines inside it), where work needs the Conservation Commission’s review. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
