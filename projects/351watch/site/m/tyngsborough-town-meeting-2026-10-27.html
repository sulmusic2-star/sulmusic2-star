Tyngsborough Town Meeting

Tue Oct 27, 7 pm Tyngsborough Elementary School Open the warrant

    • Ticket-style fines for bylaw violations, incl. zoning (Article 8): not a zoning change.

      Article 8 replaces the general bylaws' penalty article with a non-criminal disposition bylaw: town officers can issue written tickets instead of criminal charges for violations of town bylaws, the Zoning Bylaw and town regulations, with a fine schedule. It does not change any zoning rule.
