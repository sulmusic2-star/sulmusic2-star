Lunenburg Planning Board

When
Mon Oct 19, 6 pm
Where
Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA, or on Zoom
Watch
Channel 8 or YouTube
Public comment
On Zoom use the “raise hand” feature; by phone dial *9 to ask to speak. In person at Town Hall or on Zoom: phone users dial *9 to request to speak, computer and app users use the raise hand feature. After the hearing the Planning Board makes a recommendation; Town Meeting votes on the bylaw.

On the agenda

    • Zoning clean-up changes for the Nov 17 Town Meeting: Planning Board hearingHearing

      Six housekeeping changes: replace “development plan” wording with “site plan”, drop references to a retail district that no longer exists, rename the Land Use Director to Planning Director, delete the “two dwellings on one lot” section, change a common-driveway limit from 4 lots to 4 primary dwellings, and let the Planning Board set stormwater permit fees.

      Topics
      Zoning changes

      The changes go to the Special Town Meeting on November 17, 2026.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      consider the following housekeeping zoning amendments for the Special Town Meeting on November 17, 2026

    • Zoning amendments for Special Town Meeting: Planning Board hearingHearing

      Public hearing on zoning bylaw amendments the Planning Board is sending to the Special Town Meeting, where voters decide them. Votes may be taken at the meeting.
