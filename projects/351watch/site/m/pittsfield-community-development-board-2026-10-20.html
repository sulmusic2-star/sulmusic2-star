    • Temporary ban on data centers in Pittsfield: Community Development Board hearingHearing

      A petition to add a new zoning section 4.332, “Data Centers, Temporary Moratorium,” pausing data centers in Pittsfield for a time the notice doesn’t give, and to add a glossary definition.

      Before this: On Sep 15 the Community Development Board agreed to become the petitioner for the moratorium; the petition was also on the City Council’s Oct 13 agenda.

      Agenda wording

      a public hearing will be held on a petition to amend City Code Chapter 23, Article 23-2 Section 2.2 “Glossary,” and to create Article 23-4 Section 4.332 “Data Centers, Temporary Moratorium” to amend the Zoning Ordinance to impose a temporary moratorium on Data Centers in Pittsfield.

    • Temporary moratorium on data centers in Pittsfield: Community Development Board hearingHearing

      The Community Development Board itself petitions to add Section 4.332, Data Centers, Temporary Moratorium, to the city zoning code (Chapter 23, Article 23-4, Section 4), to temporarily stop data centers from being built in Pittsfield. A second hearing the same night covers the board’s petition to amend Section 4.313, Conversions.

      Before this: The same petition was on the Sep 15 Community Development Board agenda; the City Council had it on Oct 13.

      Data centers, moratoriums, zoning changesCommunity Development Board

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      ZONING AMENDMENTS 1, Public Hearing: Petition from the Community Development Board to amend City Code Chapter 23, Zoning, Article 23-4 Section 4 to create Section 4.332, Data Centers, Temporary Moratorium in order to temporarily prevent the development of data centers in Pittsfield.

    Words on the agenda. Moratorium: a temporary ban.
