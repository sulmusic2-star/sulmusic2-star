Bridgewater Town Council

When
Tue Oct 20, 7 pm
Where
BTV Studios, 80 Spring Street
Public comment
Citizen Open Forum (item D) and Citizen Comments (item O). The council does not discuss during public comment; the Town Manager may respond at a future meeting.
Agenda

On the agenda

    • High intensity data centers zoning ordinance (D-FY26-016): Town Council actionPossible vote

      Ordinance D-FY26-016 would amend Bridgewater’s zoning ordinance to address high intensity data centers. It is listed under legislation for action, so the council may vote on it.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 7 Planning Board agenda, a joint meeting with the Community and Economic Development Committee.

      Topics
      Data centers, zoning changes
