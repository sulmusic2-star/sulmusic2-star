On the agenda

    • 124-foot Verizon cell tower at 12 Tozer Road: hearing continues from Sep 23Hearing

      Cellco Partnership d/b/a Verizon Wireless and the Towers, LLC want a special permit plus height and setback variances for a wireless communications facility with a monopole 124 feet in height (including a 4’ lightning rod), set back approximately 52’ from the property line, at 12 Tozer Road in the IR zoning district.

      Before this: Also a continued public hearing on the Sep 23 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda.

      Cellco Partnership d/b/a Verizon Wireless and the Towers, LLC requests a Special Permit under §§300-42 (C)(1)(p) and 300-91 height and setback Variances from §300-25A to permit a wireless communications facility with a monopole 124 feet in height (including 4’lightning rod) and setback from the property line of approximately 52’. The property is located at 12 Tozer Road in the IR zoning district

    Words on the agenda. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions.
