Beverly Zoning Board of Appeals, Wed Oct 28, 7 pm

  1. Wed Oct 28, 7 pm

    • Cell towers

      The Zoning Board of Appeals will hold a public hearing on a special permit and setback variances for a 124-foot cell tower at 12 Tozer Road (continued from an earlier meeting).

      Public hearing.

      Can I speak?

      Yes. Anyone can attend a public hearing and ask to speak. You can also send written comments to the board through the town clerk before the meeting. Ask the clerk about sign-up rules and time limits.

      Beverly’s website lists the clerk’s office.

      CONTINUED PUBLIC HEARINGS Cellco Partnership d/b/a Verizon Wireless and the Towers, LLC requests a Special Permit under §§300-42 (C)(1)(p) and 300-91 height and setback Variances from §300-25A to permit a wireless communications facility with a monopole 124 feet in height (including 4'lightning rod) and setback from the property line of approximately 52'.
