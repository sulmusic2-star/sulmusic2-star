    Words on the agenda. Assessors Map and Lot: the town’s own number for the piece of land. 40R district: a smart-growth zoning district for denser housing with a share kept affordable, which the state pays the town to adopt. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Overlay district: a zoning layer with extra rules on top of the zone underneath. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
