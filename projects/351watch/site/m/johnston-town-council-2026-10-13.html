Johnston Town Council

Tue Oct 13 1478 Atwood Avenue Open the agenda

    • Rezoning two Shun Pike lots from residential to industrial: hearing

      Ordinance 2026-8 would change the zoning map for Assessor's Plat 43, Lots 264 and 265 on Shun Pike from R-40 (residential) to I-Industrial. It began as a petition by Bachar Sasa (2025-CP-10). The agenda doesn't say what would be built.

      Before this: Sep 14 Town Council agenda: petition 2025-CP-10 by Bachar Sasa for the same lots, referred to the Planning Board for an advisory opinion.

      Zoning changesAssessor’s Plat 43, Lots 264 & 265 on Shun PikeBachar SasaHearing

      The council may vote the same night ('Votes may be taken on any or all items'). Unfinished business after 10:30 pm carries to the fourth Monday.
