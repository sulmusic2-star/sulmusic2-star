South Kingstown Town Council

Tue Oct 13, 7:30 pm Town Council Chambers Open the agenda

Public comment period (30 minutes): speak once, up to five minutes, on agenda items. Public hearing. There is also a 30-minute public comment period: speak once, up to five minutes.

    • Town votes on a solar carport contract (up to $1,658,677.65) for its Highway Facility

      The council votes on hiring RISE Group, Inc. of Cranston to design and build a solar carport at the town's Highway Facility: $1,518,022 base, plus $61,671 for the domestic-content equipment alternate, plus a 5% contingency, for a total not to exceed $1,658,677.65.

      SolarHighway Facility$1,579,693 (contract); $1,658,677.65 (total)RISE Group, Inc.Vote

      If approved, the award still depends on contract talks, confirmed financing, the Town Solicitor's approval of the documents, and the contractor's bonds and insurance.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      A resolution authorizing the Town Manager to award a Design-Build contract to RISE Group, Inc., 1341 Elmwood Avenue, Cranston, Rhode Island 02910, for the design, permitting, procurement, construction, utility interconnection, commissioning and closeout of the Highway Facility Solar Carport Project

    • Old Tower Hill Road form-based zoning: hearing continues from Sep 14 and Sep 28

      The council proposes a new zoning section (610) with a 'form-based code' for the Old Tower Hill Road area: five new districts with rules aimed at a walkable, mixed-use neighborhood, plus when the rules apply and how projects get reviewed.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 (hearing opened) and Sep 28 (continued) Town Council agendas; the Planning Board followed up Oct 6. Resident emails for and against are in this agenda’s communications.

      Zoning changesOld Tower Hill Road areaHearing

      The hearing continues from Sep 14 and Sep 28. The council can vote after it closes; no date is given.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      A. A Public Hearing, continued from September 14 and September 28, 2026, to consider proposed amendments to the Zoning Ordinance, Appendix A: Article 6, New Section 610, to implement Old Tower Hill Road Form-Based Code (FBC) and establish five (5) Form-Based Districts, as shown on the attached Regulating Plan Map
