Lawrence City Council

When
Tue Oct 20, 7 pm
Where
City Council Chambers, Lawrence City Hall, 200 Common Street, or on Zoom
Watch
YouTube
Public comment
Public Participation comes near the start of the meeting. The meeting is hybrid: take part in person in the Council Chamber or remotely on Zoom.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • Highway Access 2 Overlay District (HA-2): hearing continued to first November meetingMoved

      6 Chickering LLC (managers Juan Yepez and Luis Yepez, represented by John T. Smolak, Esq.) proposes a zoning ordinance amendment creating a new overlay district (an extra layer of zoning rules over part of the city), the Highway Access 2 Overlay District (HA-2). The public hearing remains open and is continued until the council’s first meeting in November 2026.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 City Council agenda, and a public hearing on Oct 6 that remains open.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Applicant
      6 Chickering LLC (Juan Yepez & Luis Yepez, Managers)

      The public hearing remains open, continued until the council’s first meeting in November 2026.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      362/26 Proposed Zoning Ordinance Amendment to create a new zoning overlay district to be known as Highway Access 2 Overlay District (HA-2) – 6 Chickering LLC (Juan Yepez & Luis Yepez, Managers) c/o John T. Smolak, Esq. [10-06-2026, PUBLIC HEARING REMAINS OPEN – Continued until First Meeting in November 2026]

    • 20-year solar billing credit deals with 978 Solar Development: committee reportDiscussion

      20-year agreements with 978 Solar Development LLC (New Leaf Energy) for the city to receive electricity billing credits under net metering (NET Metering Electricity Offtake Agreements). The Budget and Finance Committee reports on them; Simon Pereira, Supervisor of Energy Projects, brought the item.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 6 City Council agenda.

      Topics
      Solar
      Applicant
      Simon Pereira, Supervisor of Energy Projects
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      409/26 NET Metering Electricity Offtake Agreements, 20-year Electricity Billing Credit Agreements with 978 Solar Development LLC (New Leaf Energy) – Simon Pereira, Supervisor of Energy Projects

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that.
