Weston Planning Board

When
Tue Oct 20, 5 pm
Where
Town Hall, Main Level Meeting Room, or on Zoom
Public comment
On Zoom, raise your hand to be recognized to speak. Anything you want to show on screen must go to the Town Planner at least one day before the meeting.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Age-restricted multifamily housing at 300 Wellesley (Regis College, HYM): zoning workshopDiscussion

      HYM, the applicant, and Regis College, the owner, propose a Zoning Bylaw Amendment to allow age restricted multifamily housing at 300 Wellesley. The Planning Board goes over the draft in a workshop with public comment; votes may be taken on any agenda item.

      Before this: Also a continued hearing on the Oct 7 Planning Board agenda and on its Sep 11 and Sep 16 agendas (the Sep 16 one a design charette for a 215 unit project); it was on the Sep 29 and Oct 13 Select Board agendas too.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Where

      Applicant
