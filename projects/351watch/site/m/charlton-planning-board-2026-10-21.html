On the agenda

    • 261 North Sturbridge Road solar farm: screening change and decommissioning bond

      Nextera Energy asks for a field change at the 261 North Sturbridge Road solar farm, altering the vegetative planting and screening that was approved. The board also has the facility’s decommissioning bond (the money set aside to remove the project at the end of its life) on its administrative list.

      Before this: The field change request was also on the Sep 16 Planning Board agenda.
