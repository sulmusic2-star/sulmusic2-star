    • New Battery storage model bylaw and clean energy moratorium: Planning Board reviewDiscussion

      The board reviews the state Department of Energy Resources (DOER) model zoning bylaw for allowing battery energy storage systems, written for the new consolidated local permitting process for small clean energy infrastructure facilities. It also checks the status of its request for legal services on a temporary moratorium on permitting small clean energy infrastructure.

      Before this: The DOER consolidated clean energy permitting was on the Sep 28 and Oct 5 Planning Board agendas.

      Battery storage, moratoriums, clean-energy permit rules

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Review and discuss Department of Energy Resources (DOER) Model Zoning Bylaw: Allowing Use of Battery Energy Storage Systems (in light of the consolidated local permitting process for Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facilities). Status of Request for Legal Services regarding temporary moratorium on small clean energy infrastructure permitting.

    • New Groundwater Protection District map reference, zoning 145-54(B): Water Department requestDiscussion

      The Water Department asks to change the zoning bylaw’s Groundwater Protection District section (§ 145-54(B)) so it refers to the Townsend Zoning Map dated October 2014 instead of the 1998 Groundwater Protection Overlay Map, as a MassDEP sanitary survey recommended for Zone II #473. Ryan Lapierre, the chief water treatment plant operator, brings suggested wording.

      Zoning changesRyan Lapierre, Townsend Water Department

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      6:30 PM - Discussion with Ryan Lapierre, Townsend Water Department – Proposed amendment to Zoning Bylaw § 145-54(B), Groundwater Protection District map reference. … The proposed revision would update the reference from the 1998 Groundwater Protection Overlay Map to the Townsend Zoning Map dated October 2014, in response to a MassDEP sanitary survey recommendation for Zone II #473.

    Words on the agenda. Moratorium: a temporary ban. Battery energy storage (BESS): large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid.
