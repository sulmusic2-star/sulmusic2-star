Hearing on an affordable housing project at 84 & 88 Andover Street

Thursday at 6:30 pm. It comes up on the Zoning Board of Appeals’s agenda, but nothing is set to be discussed or decided. The hearing on an affordable housing project at 84 & 88 Andover Street moves to Dec 3, because the applicant asked.

      CONTINUED HEARINGS: 84 & 88 ANDOVER STREET- TRAILSIDE ANDOVER DEVELOPMENT LLC-ZBA-26-29- Request to continue to December 3, 2026 Comprehensive Permit under M.G.L., Chapter Ch 40B to construct 40 ownership condominium homes.

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
