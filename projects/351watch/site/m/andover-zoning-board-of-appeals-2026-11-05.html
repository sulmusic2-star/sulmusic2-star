    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
