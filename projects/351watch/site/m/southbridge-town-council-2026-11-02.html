Temporary ban on new data centers (a moratorium)

Monday at 7 pm, at Robert G. MacKinnon Council Chambers. The Town Council talks about this. No vote is listed. Also on the agenda, it talks about a special permit and site plans for a data center.

When

Where
Robert G. MacKinnon Council Chambers
Public comment

    Words on the agenda. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting. Variance: permission to depart from a zoning rule because the lot’s shape, size or ground makes the rule a hardship. Moratorium: a temporary ban.
