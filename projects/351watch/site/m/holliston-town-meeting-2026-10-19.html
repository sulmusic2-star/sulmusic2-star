Holliston Town Meeting

When
Mon Oct 19, 7 pm
Where
Holliston High School Auditorium, 370 Hollis Street, Holliston, MA
Who votes
Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.
Warrant
Open the Town Meeting warrant

On the warrant

    • Buy the fire station’s solar array, Article 13: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 13 asks Town Meeting to move $421,500 from Article 22 of the May 2024 Town Meeting to buy the solar panel array on the Fire Department headquarters at 59 Central Street, now owned by a third party, and repair the roof. The Finance Committee unanimously recommends the reallocation for the roof repair and solar array purchase.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 28 Select Board agenda, as the Central Fire Solar Array Purchase.

    • Groundwater Protection District zoning change, Article 17: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 17 asks Town Meeting to amend Section V-L of the Zoning By-Laws, the Groundwater Protection District rules. The Select Board, the Planning Board and Envisioning Future Holliston sponsor it.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s public hearing was on its Oct 8 agenda and continued Oct 14; also on the Sep 10 Planning Board and Sep 14 Select Board agendas.
