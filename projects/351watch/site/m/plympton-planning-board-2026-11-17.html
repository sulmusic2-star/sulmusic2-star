    • Parallel Products ground-mounted solar, 87 County Road: hearing continues Nov 17Hearing

      Parallel Products Solar Energy, LLC wants site plan approval under zoning bylaw Section 300-6.10 to build a large-scale ground-mounted solar energy system at 87 County Road (Assessors M-B-L 4-2-11). The public hearing continues from an earlier session. The plan and application can be seen at the Town Clerk’s office during business hours.

      CONTINUED PUBLIC HEARING-PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE … 87 County Road- To consider the Site Plan approval Application of application of Parallel Products Solar Energy, LLC pursuant to the Plympton Zoning Bylaw Section 300-6.10 for the construction of a large scale ground mounted solar energy system on property located at 87 County Road, Assessors M-B-L: 4-2-11.

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Site plan review: the board’s check of a project’s layout, parking, drainage and lighting.
