Lancaster Planning Board

Tue Oct 13, 7 pm Nashaway Meeting Room, or join online Open the agenda

'Public comment will be received.' In person or by Zoom.

    • Zoning for a new neighborhood at 220 Old Common Rd (state school site): hearing continues

      The board continues its hearing on a draft zoning amendment and subdivision rules for a mixed-use development on the state-owned Lancaster Industrial School for Girls site at 220 Old Common Road.

      Before this: Sep 28 Planning Board: hearing notices for the zoning amendment (a new zoning district for the site, for mixed use and single- and multifamily housing through the state's State Land for Homes Initiative) and the subdivision rules (new Article VI).

      Zoning changes220 Old Common RoadHearing

      The hearing continues; the board's next meetings are Oct 26 and Nov 9.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      III. Continued Public Hearing: Lancaster Industrial School for Girls (DCAMM) Zoning & Subdivision Rules & Regulations Amendments: The draft zoning amendment and subdivision rules and regulations for the proposed mixed-use development at the state-owned site at 220 Old Common Road will be heard and considered by the Board. Public comment will be received.
