Cortlandt Town Board

When

Where

Public comment

Agenda
Open the agenda (PDF) (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • 2.3 MW solar project, Lexington Ave and Route 202: Town Board vote on 1-year extensionExtension

      Cortlandt CSG LLC, an affiliate of Bullrock Renewables, asks the Town Board to extend for one year its special permit and site development plan approval for a solar facility on Lexington Avenue at Route 202. The approved project, under Planning Board Resolution 8-22, is an approximately 2.3 MW solar energy system with about 5,300 panels on a parcel of about 33 acres west of Lexington Avenue and north of Crompond Road, and would remove about 2,000 regulated trees.
