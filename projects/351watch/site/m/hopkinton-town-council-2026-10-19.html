Hopkinton Town Council

When
Mon Oct 19, 6:45 pm
Where
Town Hall, 1 Town House Road, Hopkinton, RI 02833, or on Zoom
Watch
YouTube
Public comment
Public comment on agenda items is at the Chair’s discretion. On Zoom, use the raise hand function or dial *9, then *6 to unmute; state your name and address before speaking. Being in person at Town Hall is the most reliable way to take part.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Data centers banned in every zoning district: Town Council hearing on the ordinanceHearing

      Councilors Geary and Burns propose amending the zoning ordinance to define a data center and make data centers a prohibited use in every zoning district, with a new purpose section (5.8.1) and a fix to a numbering error in Section 5.6. Council discussion and public comment follow, then a motion to continue the hearing or close it and either set a later date for adopting the ordinance or vote on adoption now.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 21 Town Council agenda; the Planning Board’s Oct 7 agenda had a possible vote on reconsidering its earlier vote on the data center advisory.

      Topics
      Data centers
      Applicant
      Councilors Geary and Burns

      After the hearing the council votes to continue it, or to close it and either set a later date for the adoption vote or vote on adoption.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      1. PROPOSED ORDINANCE RE: DATA CENTERS Open a hearing on an amendment to the Code of Ordinances, Section 2. – Definitions, to add a definition for a Data Center; revise Section 5. – District use regulations, to show that data centers are a prohibited use in all zoning districts; add Section 5.8.1. Purpose – which explains that this amendment is to address data centers as a distinct land use
