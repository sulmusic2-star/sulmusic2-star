Swansea Conservation Commission

Tue Oct 13, 6 pm Swansea Police Station Open the agenda (PDF)

Public hearing; no rules stated.

    • Solar farm with batteries off Old Warren Rd: wetlands hearing (amended filing)

      Old Warren Rd Development LLC (Pedro Rodri uez, as the agenda spells it) filed an amended wetlands permit request for a ground-mounted solar farm with battery storage at 0 Old Warren Rd; the same night, Black Swan Development Corp. (Rory Walker) seeks a wetlands permit for Phase 2 of a solar facility at 440 Old Warren Rd.

      Before this: Sep 30: state wetlands filing (NOI) by Old Warren Road Development II, LLC for a 4.9 MW-AC battery energy storage system, gravel driveway and stormwater system at '0 Old Warren Road (lot 2)', described as 'phase 2 of 072-1889' (in our records; it's unclear which of the two Old Warren Rd items it matches).

      The Commission rules only on wetlands, not on whether the project is allowed. Next meeting Nov 9.
