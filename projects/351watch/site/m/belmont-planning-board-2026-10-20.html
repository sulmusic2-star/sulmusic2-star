Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Zoning bylaw amendment for changes to MGL c. 40A (the state zoning law), Case #26-23Hearing

      A continued public hearing on Case #26-23, a zoning bylaw amendment responding to changes in the state zoning law (MGL c. 40A). It would amend Section 1.5 (nonconforming uses and structures), Section 6.11 (nonconforming historic accessory buildings), Section 6D (single- and two-family dwellings in the General Residence District) and Section 7 (administration).
