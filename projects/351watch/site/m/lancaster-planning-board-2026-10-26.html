Lancaster Planning Board

Mon Oct 26, 7 pm Nashaway Meeting Room, or join online Open the agenda

Public hearing: anyone can speak; written comments go to the town clerk before the meeting.

    • Synthetic soccer fields at 70 McGovern; "Solar District" is only the zoning overlay

      Benters, LLC asks for a stormwater permit and site plan review to build new synthetic soccer fields and expand an existing parking lot at 70 McGovern. The lot lies in a Solar District overlay, but the notice proposes no solar.

      Solar70 McGovern, Lancaster, MABenters, LLC

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Request for a Stormwater Management Permit pursuant to §170 (Stormwater Management) and Site Plan Review pursuant to §220-34 relative to the construction of new synthetic soccer fields with an expansion of an existing parking facility. The property is located at 70 McGovern, Lancaster, MA, in the EZA Retail Subdistrict, Flexible Design 1 District and is overlayed by the Solar District

    Agenda: Lancaster Planning Board, Mon Oct 26
