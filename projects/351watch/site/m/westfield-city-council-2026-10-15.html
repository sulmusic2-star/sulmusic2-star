Westfield City Council

Thu Oct 15, 6 pm Council Chambers Open the agenda

Public hearing; no rules stated.

    • New zoning rules for clean-energy projects, including battery storage: hearing

      The City Council proposes zoning rules for clean energy systems, including battery storage (BESS); the notice doesn't say what they require. Full petition: City Clerk's Office or cityofwestfield.org/applications.

      Before this: Sep 17 City Council: motion of Councilors Fanion and Mello to amend the zoning ordinance to regulate clean energy systems, including BESS, referred to the Planning Board with a hearing ordered. Oct 6 Planning Board: the petition on its agenda.

      Battery storageCity CouncilHearing

      The council votes after the hearing, possibly at a later meeting; the notice doesn't say when.

      To be held at 6:00 PM in Council Chambers, City Hall, 59 Court St., Westfield, MA on October 15, 2026, by the City Council to consider a petition of the City Council to adopt zoning regulations for clean energy systems, including BESS. Access the full petition at the City Clerk’s Office (City Hall) or at: www.cityofwestfield.org/applications
