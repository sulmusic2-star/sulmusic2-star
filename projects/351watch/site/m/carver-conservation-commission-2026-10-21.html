Carver Conservation Commission

When
Wed Oct 21, 7 pm
Where
Meeting Room #1, Carver Town Hall, 108 Main Street
Watch
Channel 15 or YouTube
Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak.
Agenda
Open the agenda (PDF) (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • Solar canopies with battery storage, 235 Main St (Parallel Products): wetlands hearingHearing

      Parallel Products Solar Energy LLC wants a wetlands permit (a Notice of Intent) to build solar canopies with battery energy storage, plus site work, in an existing parking area at 235 Main Street, within the 65-foot and 100-foot buffer zones to a bordering vegetated wetland.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s Oct 13 agenda had the project’s special permit hearing, where the applicant asked for a continuance.

      Topics
      Solar, battery storage
      Where
      235 Main Street, Carver, MA; Assessors Map 87, Lots 2-1A, 2-4A, 2-5A, 2-6A, 2-7A, and 2-34D
      Applicant
      Parallel Products Solar Energy LLC
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      235 Main Street Notice of Intent (NOI) … to hear the Notice of Intent submitted by Parallel Products Solar Energy LLC to construct solar canopies with battery energy storage and associated site work in an existing parking area within the 65-foot and 100-foot Buffer Zone to Bordering Vegetated Wetland (BVW).

    • Federal Pond Solar, 104 Tremont St: wetlands hearing continued to Nov 4Moved to Wed Nov 4

      Federal Pond Solar, LLC wants a wetlands permit (a Notice of Intent under the state Wetlands Protection Act and the Carver Wetlands Protection Bylaw) to build a dual-use agriculture and solar array and ground-mounted solar arrays with coupled battery storage at 104 Tremont Street, with site work and interconnection in and near wetlands by a cranberry bog.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 2 and Oct 7 Conservation Commission agendas; the Planning Board’s Oct 13 agenda had the project’s special permit hearing, where the applicant asked for a continuance.

      Topics
      Solar, battery storage
      Where
      104 Tremont Street, Carver, MA; Assessors Map 123, Lot 13
      Applicant
      Federal Pond Solar, LLC
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      104 Tremont Street CONTINUED PUBLIC HEARINGS Notice of Intent (NOI) DEP File # SE126-710 … to hear the Notice of Intent submitted by Federal Pond Solar, LLC, to construct a dual-use agriculture/solar array and ground-mounted solar arrays with coupled battery storage … CONTINUED TO NOVEMBER 4, 2026.

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Dual-use: solar panels raised high enough that farming goes on underneath. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. DEP File #: the state environmental agency’s number for the wetlands filing. Buffer Zone: the land next to a wetland (the first 100 feet under state law; towns set their own lines inside it), where work needs the Conservation Commission’s review. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
