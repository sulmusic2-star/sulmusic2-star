    • New 300-unit 40B at Deerpath Rd and Heather Hill Rd, Bournedale: 25% affordable, hearingHearing

      Olivia Grace, LLC wants a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) for a 300-unit multifamily development, 25% of it affordable, with a community building, garages, recreation areas, parking and utilities at Deerpath Road and Heather Hill Road in Bournedale. The applicant also asks for waivers from parts of the zoning bylaw and other town bylaws and regulations.

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Permit Number: 2025-SP10 Applicant Name: Olivia Grace, LLC Project Address: Deerpath Rd & Heather hill Rd, Bournedale MA, 02532 Proposed Project: The applicant is seeking a Comprehensive Permit under M.G.L. c. 40B, ss. 20-23 … The applicant is proposing a project that consists of a 300-unit multifamily (25% affordable) residential development

    Words on the agenda. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
