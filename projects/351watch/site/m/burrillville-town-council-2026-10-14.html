Burrillville Town Council

Wed Oct 14, 7 pm Burrillville Town Building, 105 Harrisville Main Street, Harrisville Open the agenda

Public Comment is item 2. Speakers state their names and addresses.

    • Vote: resident petition to ban data centers in Burrillville's zoning (item 26-320)

      Roberta Lacey petitions the Town Council to amend Chapter 30 (Zoning) of the town ordinances to prohibit data centers; the council votes on what to do with the petition.

      Data centersRoberta LaceyVote

      Supporting documents are in the Town Clerk's office and at burrillville.org/town-council/pages/town-council-agenda-packet-items.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      26-320 Consider and act on a petition from Roberta Lacey for an amendment to the Burrillville General Ordinances, Chapter 30 Zoning to prohibit data centers; or take any other action thereon.
