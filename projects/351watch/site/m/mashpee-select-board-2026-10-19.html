    • Harwich’s push for Cape Cod Commission review of 40B projects: draft support letterDiscussion

      The Select Board reviews a draft letter supporting the Town of Harwich’s request for state legislation that would let the Cape Cod Commission decide, case by case, whether to review Chapter 40B comprehensive permit projects (the state’s affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) as developments of regional impact (a DRI referral).

      Before this: Harwich’s letter to the Barnstable County commissioners was on the Oct 5 Select Board agenda.

      Affordable housingTown of Harwich

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      4. Review of Draft Letter of Support for Town of Harwich Request to Pursue Legislation Authorizing Cape Cod Commission Discretionary DRI Referral of Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit Projects

    Words on the agenda. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning.
