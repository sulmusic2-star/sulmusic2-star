Ipswich Town Meeting

When
Tue Oct 20, 7 pm
Where
IPSWICH MIDDLE SCHOOL/HIGH SCHOOL, 134 High Street
Who votes
Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.
Warrant
Open the Town Meeting warrant

On the warrant

    • Mixed-use buildings: shops must fill 25% of the ground floor, up front: vote (Article 5)Vote

      Article 5 changes the definition of “Use, Residential-Mixed” so the non-residential use must take up at least 25% of the building’s ground floor and sit in the front of the building, facing the sidewalk or street.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Size
      no less than twenty-five percent (25%) of the building’s ground floor
      Sponsor
      Planning Board article

      Requires a Two-Thirds Majority Vote (the warrant says so), then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      To see if the Town will vote to amend the Ipswich Protective Zoning Bylaw by revising Section III, Definitions, to update the definition of “Use, Residential Mixed” as set forth below; or take any action relative thereto.

    • ADU rules (Article 6) and business-district size rules (Article 7): Town Meeting votesVote

      Article 6 replaces Ipswich’s accessory apartment, converted-accessory-building and detached unit rules with one “Protected Use Accessory Dwelling Unit” section to match state law (one such unit per lot, no bigger than half the main home’s floor area or 900 square feet). Article 7 changes dimensional and density rules for business districts; its text is in the full warrant.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Size
      not larger in Gross Floor Area than ½ the Gross Floor Area of the Principal Dwelling or 900 square feet, whichever is smaller
      Sponsor
      Planning Board & Building Department article
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      6 Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Bylaw Amendments – Ipswich Protective Zoning Bylaw Planning Board & Building Department 7 Amend Dimensional & Density Regulations for Business Districts – Ipswich Protective Zoning Bylaw Planning Board

    • New definition of “structure” in the zoning bylaw: Town Meeting vote (Article 4)Vote

      Article 4 rewrites the zoning bylaw’s definition of “structure” to mean anything built or placed on the ground for support or shelter and meant to be permanent (or anchored to something permanent), so permit reviews are clearer and easier to defend.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Sponsor
      Planning Board article

      Requires a Two-Thirds Majority Vote (the warrant says so), then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      To see if the Town will vote to amend the Ipswich Protective Zoning Bylaw by revising Section III, Definitions, to update the definition of “Structure” as set forth below; or take any action relative thereto.
