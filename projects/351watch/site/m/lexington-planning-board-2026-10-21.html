Lexington Planning Board

Wed Oct 21, 6 pm Online meeting Open the agenda

    • Cancelled: Oct 21 hearing on new rules for Special Residential Developments (Article 7)

      The Planning Board cancelled its Oct 21 remote hearing on Article 7, which would change Section 6.9 of the zoning bylaw on Special Residential Developments (site coverage limits, a landscaped buffer around the edge, protection of historic buildings, changed gross floor area limits, wider open space standards).

      Before this: The Planning Board reviewed amendments to Special Residential Developments, Section 6.9, in a work session Oct 7.
