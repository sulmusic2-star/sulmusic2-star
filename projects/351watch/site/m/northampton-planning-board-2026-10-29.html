Northampton Planning Board

Thu Oct 29, 7 pm City Council Chambers Open the agenda

Public hearing: anyone can speak; written comments go to the town clerk before the meeting.

    • 365-day ban on new data centers: joint council committee and Planning Board hearing

      The ordinance would define data centers and impose a 365 day temporary moratorium on establishing one, by changing zoning sections 350-2.0 and 350-5.2.

      Before this: The City Council referred ordinance 26.125, Data Center Moratorium, Sep 3 and gave a resolution to form a select committee to study data centers its second reading Sep 17.

      Data centers, moratoriums, zoning changesHearing

      By City Council rules, the Committee on Legislative Matters considers the ordinance before the full City Council votes.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Proposed zoning ordinance amendments to establish definition of data centers and to impose a 365 day temporary moratorium on establishment of a data center by amending 350-2.0 and 350- 5.2.
