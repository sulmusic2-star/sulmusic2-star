Marcellus Town Board

When
Wed Oct 21, 6:30 pm
Where
Town Hall
Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak.
Agenda
Open the agenda (PDF) (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • 12-month moratorium on data centers and crypto mining, Local Law E: vote to enactVote

      After the public hearing, the Town Board has a resolution to enact Local Law E of 2026, the twelve-month moratorium. The draft resolution calls the law a Type II action under SEQR, which ends environmental review, and says it went to the Onondaga County Planning Board for review.

      Before this: Introduced at the Sep 16 Town Board meeting; also on the Oct 7 Town Board agenda.

      Topics
      Battery storage, data centers, moratoriums
      Where
      All real property within the Town of Marcellus
      Sponsor
      Town Board of the Town of Marcellus

      The law takes effect immediately upon passage and lasts twelve months, unless renewed or ended sooner by Town Board resolution.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      New Business A. Local Law NO. E of 2026 Twelve (12) Month Moratorium on Commercial Cryptocurrency Mining Operations, Data Mining, Data Processing Centers, and Data Storage Facilities Within the Town of Marcellus a. Enactment for Local Law No. E of 2026 … WHEREAS, the enactment of Local Law No. E-2026 has previously been determined to be Type II action under SEQR

    • 12-month moratorium on data centers and crypto mining, Local Law E: hearingHearing

      Local Law E of 2026 would stop the development, construction or use of new commercial cryptocurrency mining operations, data mining, data processing centers and data storage facilities anywhere in the Town of Marcellus for twelve months. The law exempts projects already approved or on Town-owned property, but they could not be expanded while it lasts.

      Before this: Introduced at the Sep 16 Town Board meeting; also on the Oct 7 Town Board agenda.

      Topics
      Data centers, moratoriums
      Where
      All real property within the Town of Marcellus
      Sponsor
      Town Board of the Town of Marcellus

      An enactment vote on Local Law E is on the same agenda, under New Business.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Public Hearing: Local Law E of 2026 Twelve (12) Month Moratorium on Commercial Cryptocurrency Mining Operations, Data Mining, Data Processing Centers, and Data Storage Facilities Within the Town of Marcellus

    Words on the agenda. Moratorium: a temporary ban. Battery energy storage: large batteries that store electricity and feed it back to the grid (a battery energy storage system).
