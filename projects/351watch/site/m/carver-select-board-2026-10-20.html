    • Special Town Meeting warrant, solar credits and 40R district included: Select Board votePossible vote

      The Select Board may vote on which articles to approve for the Special Town Meeting warrant and on its recommendations. The list includes buying solar credits, a 40R smart growth district (presented at 6:40PM), payment in lieu of tax agreements, unpaid bills, more money for FY 2027 health insurance, moving town-owned fiber lines, compensated absences, revolving funds, updating transfers to the middle high school stabilization fund, and Conservation Commission alternate members.

    • 40R smart growth district: presentation and warrant votePossible vote

      A presentation on the 40R article (a smart growth zoning district, state-backed zoning for denser housing) at 6:40PM, then at 6:50PM a discussion and possible vote to put the Special Town Meeting articles on the warrant with the board’s recommendation, including the 40R Smart growth district. This is zoning under 40R, not a comprehensive permit project.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 6 Select Board agenda (an update on revised 40R language and the same warrant vote) and the Oct 13 Planning Board agenda (a vote to recommend the Chapter 40R Smart Growth Overlay Zoning District).

      Solar, affordable housing

      Agenda wording

      6:40PM Presentation on 40R article 6:50PM Discussion and possible vote to approve Special Town Meeting articles to the warrant and recommendation: Unpaid bills, Supplemental appropriation for FY 2027 health insurance … 40R Smart growth district

    Words on the agenda. Comprehensive permit: the state’s 40B permit: one permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals for housing with affordable units, which can override local zoning. 40R district: a smart-growth zoning district for denser housing with a share kept affordable, which the state pays the town to adopt. PILOT: a payment in lieu of taxes: a yearly payment the owner and the town agree on instead of property tax.
