Agenda

On the agenda

    • Sign Bylaw zoning amendment: Select Board sends it to Planning Board for a hearingVote

      The Select Board would accept a proposed amendment to the zoning bylaw’s Sign Bylaw and send it to the Planning Board for a public hearing. At the same meeting it votes to approve the Special Town Meeting warrant and the Special Town Election warrant.

      Before this: The Planning Board had the Sign By-Law changes on its Sep 14 and Sep 28 agendas, the latter with a public forum, and on Oct 13 a possible vote to forward the article to the Select Board.

      Topics
      Zoning changes

      If accepted, the amendment goes to the Planning Board for a public hearing.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      4. Action Items: a) Accept and Send to Planning Board the Proposed Zoning Bylaw Amendment to Sign Bylaw for Public Hearing b) Vote to Approve Special Town Meeting Warrant and Special Town Election Warrant and send to Constable for Public Posting
