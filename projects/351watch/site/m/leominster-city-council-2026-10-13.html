Leominster City Council

Tue Oct 13, 6 pm Mahan City Council Chambers Open the agenda

    • New utility pole to power a solar battery project at 526 Willard St (pole only)

      National Grid and Verizon ask the council for permission to put a new 45ft pole (P56-50) on Willard St, about 520 feet southwest of Bainbridge St & Jytek Rd, to provide power for a solar battery project at 526 Willard St. The pole petition is before the Public Service Committee; the battery project itself is not on the agenda.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 (first time on the agenda) and Sep 28 (public hearing, 5:50 pm) City Council agendas.

      Solar, battery storage526 Willard StMassachusetts Electric Company d/b/a National Grid and Verizon New England, Inc.

      The petition had a public hearing Sep 28 at 5:50 pm and is marked 'Further Time' in the Public Service Committee.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      National Grid to install one new 45ft H | Mid Span Pole, numbered P56-50, on Willard St to provide power for solar battery project at 526 Willard St.

    Public forum at 6:55 pm on items on the agenda: state your name and address, 2 minutes each. The council does not respond.Agenda: Leominster City Council, Tue Oct 13
