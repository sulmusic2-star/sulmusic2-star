Norton Town Meeting

Mon Oct 19 Norton High School Open the warrant

    • Industrial zone: taller buildings, more lot coverage. Town Meeting vote (Article 10).

      Article 10 changes the zoning table so buildings in the Industrial (I) district can be taller and cover more of the lot. The warrant's table is garbled in our copy; the Planning Board's earlier agenda (see history) described it as raising the height to 65 feet and lot coverage to 50%.

      Before this: On the Sep 15 Planning Board agenda ("increase the height of buildings in Industrial to 65 feet and the lot coverage to 50%") and in the Select Board's Sep 17 warrant review as Article 10.

      Zoning changesVote

      Zoning changes normally need a two-thirds vote, then Attorney General review.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 10 To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Bylaw, Chapter 175, §§ 175-6.2, Table of Dimensional Requirements, to revise the maximum building height and lot coverage requirements for the Industrial (I) Zoning District

    • Solar-setback footnote in Article 10's zoning table (not a solar item).

      The solar sentence is an existing footnote in the zoning table that points to the solar setback rules in Article XXII; Article 10 changes only Industrial-zone height and lot coverage.

      Solar

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      For setbacks applicable to large-scale, ground-mounted solar photovoltaic power generation installations, see Article XXII, § 175-22.3.

    • Cell-tower height footnote in Article 10's zoning table (not a tower item).

      The 125-foot wireless facility line is an existing footnote in the zoning table; Article 10 changes only Industrial-zone height and lot coverage.

      Cell towers

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Maximum height in feet for wireless communication facilities is 125 feet.

    Warrant: Norton Town Meeting, Mon Oct 19
