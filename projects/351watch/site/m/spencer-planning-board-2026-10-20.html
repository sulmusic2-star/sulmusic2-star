    • Data center moratorium through Nov 5, 2027 (Zoning Bylaw 4.8.12): discussion and actionVote

      A newly proposed zoning bylaw section, Article 4.8.12, would put a temporary moratorium on data centers in Spencer, in effect through November 5, 2027. The Planning Board has it listed for discussion and action, so the board may act on it at this meeting.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 Planning Board agenda, as a discussion of a data center moratorium and zoning bylaw; the Board of Selectmen’s Sep 28 agenda had the referral of the proposed amendment to the Planning Board for a double hearing.
