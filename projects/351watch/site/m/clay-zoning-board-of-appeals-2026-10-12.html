Mon Oct 12 Open the agenda (PDF)

    • Battery storage gear at 4664 Wetzel Rd needs setback waivers: hearing

      Todd Fritzen and Wetzel Storage LLC ask for four variances to put battery energy storage equipment much closer to the lot lines than the industrial zone allows: 10.3 feet from Wetzel Road instead of 200, 20 feet from Maltlage Drive instead of 50, 20 feet on the side instead of 25, and 10.3 feet instead of 115 under the highway overlay rule.

      Battery storage · 4664 Wetzel Road, Liverpool, NY, 13090 · Todd Fritzen / Wetzel Storage LLCHearing

      The board can grant, deny or set conditions. The county planning board report was requested Sep 24 and had not come back when the agenda was printed.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      The requested variances include a reduction in the front yard setback along Wetzel Road from 200 feet to 10.3 feet, a reduction in the front yard setback along Maltlage Drive from 50 feet to 20 feet, a reduction in the side yard setback from 25 feet to 20 feet, and a reduction in Highway Overlay Type C setback from 115 feet to 10.3 feet for the development of BESS equipment.

    Public hearing: anyone can speak; written comments go to the town clerk before the meeting.Agenda (PDF): Clay Zoning Board of Appeals, Mon Oct 12
