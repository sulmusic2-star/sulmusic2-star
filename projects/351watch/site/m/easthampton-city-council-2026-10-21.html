Easthampton City Council

When
Wed Oct 21, 6 pm
Where
50 Payson Ave., 2nd floor meeting area, or join online
Public comment

On the agenda

    • Sign rules in the Zoning Ordinance, Sec. 10.0: pending in the Ordinance Committee

      The council’s Ordinance Committee is reviewing and revising the Zoning Ordinance’s sign rules, as a Zoning Ordinance Amendment to Sec. 10.0, Signs, alongside a General Ordinance Amendment to Ch. 8, Article V, Signs. Both were referred 8-5-26 with an action deadline of 11-3-26.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 16 and Oct 7 City Council agendas.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Agenda wording
