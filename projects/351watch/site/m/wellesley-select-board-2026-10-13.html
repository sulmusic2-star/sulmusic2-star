Wellesley Select Board

Tue Oct 13, 6:30 pm Town Hall Open the agenda

Citizen Speak: email sel@wellesleyma.gov before the meeting to be added to the speaker list; give name and address; 3 minutes per speaker, 15 minutes total.

    • Vote: Select Board sets Article 7 wording (open meeting bylaws) for the Nov Town Meeting

      Article 7 would amend Articles 2, 2A and 3 of Wellesley's Town Bylaw; the Select Board votes whether to approve the wording proposed by the Town Bylaw Review Committee, whose chair Mr. Largess reports that evening. This is a general bylaw, not zoning.

      Before this: Also on the Select Board's Sep 29 and Oct 6 agendas. The Special Town Meeting warrant for this November strikes Articles 2 (Open Meetings), 2A (Meetings in Executive Session) and 3 (Minutes) and combines them into a new Article 2.

      Zoning changesTown Bylaw Review CommitteeVote

      Special Town Meeting, November 2026. The Bylaw Review Committee meets on this Oct 13 before the Select Board.

      Discuss and Vote Article 7, Town Bylaw Amendments to Article 2, 2A, and 3• The Bylaw Review Committee will be taking this matter up at their meeting on October 13th and Mr. Largess will report to the Board on Tuesday evening.
