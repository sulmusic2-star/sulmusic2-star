Plymouth Town Meeting

Sat Oct 17 Open the warrant (PDF)

    • Votes on an article proposing a temporary ban on new data centers (a moratorium).

      Data centers, moratoriums, zoning changesVote

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      …ium from October 17, 2026, until October 16, 2027 on the application, permitting, construction, expansion, or conversion of any Data Center to provide the Plymouth Planning Board with sufficient time to research and understand this complex use so that reasonable regulations may be developed for consideration at the Fall 2027 Special Town Meeting, all by inserting the underlined as documented on file with the Town Clerk, "Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Data Centers Zoning", or take any other action relative thereto.

    • Votes on a zoning change: Industrial Zoning Update.

      Zoning changesVote

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Light Industrial Building Height Overlay District (LIBHOD), exempting mechanical equipment from the height measurement calculation in the Light Industrial District within the Dimensional Table, and eliminating the 0.25 maximum Floor Area Ratio requirement for Light Industry and Renewable/Alternate Energy Manufacturing uses in the Use Table, all by inserting the underlined and deleting the strikethrough as documented on file with the Town Clerk, "Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Industrial Zoning Update", or take any other action relative thereto.

    • Votes on a zoning change: Senior Housing.

      Zoning changesVote

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      …replacing the word "Elderly" with "Senior", modifying the definition of "Elderly Household" in §201-3, modifying the parking table in §203-7 related to certain senior housing uses, and prohibiting the existing Elderly Housing use in the General Commercial, Arterial Commercial, and Mixed Commerce Zoning Districts as listed in the Use Table, all by inserting the underlined and deleting the strikethrough as documented on file with the Town Clerk, "Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Senior Housing", or take any other action relative thereto.
