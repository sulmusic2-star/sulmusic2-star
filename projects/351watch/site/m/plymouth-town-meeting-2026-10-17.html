Plymouth Town Meeting

Sat Oct 17, 8 am

Plymouth North High School at 41 Obery Street

Elected Town Meeting members vote. Other registered Plymouth voters may speak with 48 hours’ notice to, and permission from, the Town Moderator, and a request to the Town Clerk.

    • One-year ban on new data centers: Town Meeting vote (Article 16)Vote

      No new data-center applications, permits, construction, expansion or conversions from October 17, 2026 to October 16, 2027, to give the Planning Board time to write rules for the Fall 2027 Special Town Meeting.

      Before this: The Planning Board discussed a data center moratorium article Aug 26 and held a public hearing on it Sep 23; the Select Board discussed Article 16 on Sep 22.

      Data centers, moratoriums, zoning changesPlanning Board article

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Bylaw, §201-3, Definitions, by adding a definition for “Data Center”, and by creating a new section, §207-12, Temporary Moratorium on Data Centers, which would enact a one-year temporary moratorium from October 17, 2026, until October 16, 2027 on the application, permitting, construction, expansion, or conversion of any Data Center

    • Senior housing zoning update; no “Elderly Housing” in commercial zones: vote (Article 14)Vote

      Article 14 swaps “Elderly” for “Senior” throughout the zoning bylaw, changes the “Elderly Household” definition and the parking rules for some senior housing, and stops allowing the existing Elderly Housing use in the General Commercial, Arterial Commercial and Mixed Commerce zones.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing on the senior housing article Aug 26; the Select Board took a 4-1 vote on Article 14 at its Sep 22 meeting.

      Zoning changesPlanning Board article

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 14: Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Senior Housing To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Bylaw by replacing the word “Elderly” with “Senior”, modifying the definition of “Elderly Household” in §201-3, modifying the parking table in §203-7 related to certain senior housing uses

    • Looser light-industrial building rules: height, setbacks, floor area: vote (Article 15)Vote

      Article 15 reduces setback and special permit requirements in the Light Industrial Building Height Overlay District (LIBHOD), leaves mechanical equipment out of height measurements in the Light Industrial District, and drops the 0.25 maximum floor area ratio for Light Industry and Renewable/Alternate Energy Manufacturing uses.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing on the industrial zoning updates Aug 26; the Select Board voted on Article 15 at its Sep 22 meeting (one member abstained).

      Zoning changesPlanning Board article

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      ARTICLE 15: Zoning Bylaw Amendment – Industrial Zoning Update To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning Bylaw by reducing setback and Special Permit requirements in the Light Industrial Building Height Overlay District (LIBHOD), exempting mechanical equipment from the height measurement calculation in the Light Industrial District within the Dimensional Table

    Words on the warrant. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions. Moratorium: a temporary ban.

From the Town Meeting warrant on plymouth-ma.gov. Read Oct 10; the town’s listing checked Oct 17, 9:15 am.
