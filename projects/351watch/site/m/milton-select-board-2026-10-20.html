      Discussion, update and possible approval of the warrant for the October 19, 2026 Special Town Meeting. Article 5 would amend Chapter 275-3.25 of the zoning bylaws, the MBTA Communities Multi-family Overlay District, on its affordability requirements.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 22, Oct 6, Oct 13 and Oct 19 Select Board agendas.
