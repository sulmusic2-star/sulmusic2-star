Tue Oct 13, 6:45 pm Town Hall, or join online Open the agenda

Open Forum at 7:02 PM (no rules stated).

    • Select Board reviews Town Meeting article on new clean-energy facility zoning (8:10 pm)

      For its Town Meeting recommendation, the Select Board reviews a Planning Board zoning article creating Section 6.6, 'Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facilities (SCEIF)', and changing the general accessory uses, signs, large-scale solar review and definitions sections; the agenda doesn't say what the new section requires.

      Before this: Planning Board public hearing on the article (PB 2612 ZNG) Sep 14 and Oct 5; its notice says the Planning Board sponsors it and that Section 6.6 sets a permit process and standards for installing, maintaining and decommissioning facilities such as battery storage, to be consistent with the MA 2024 Clean Energy Act. Select Board reviewed the warrant Sep 22 (listing it as Article 1); Conservation Commission discussed the article Sep 9. The Oct 26 Special Town Meeting notice: Monday October 26, 2026, 7:00 PM, Westford Academy Gymnasium.
