North Attleborough Town Council

Wed Oct 14, 7 pm Town Hall Open the agenda

'Resident And Community Comment' is item IV (no rules stated).

    • Offices allowed by right in downtown C-7.5 zone: Town Council measure 2027-024

      Measure 2027-024 would change the bylaw to allow professional and business offices and services (medical, legal, insurance, real estate and similar) by right in the C-7.5 Commercial District (Downtown); it is under New Business and the agenda doesn't say whether a vote is taken.

      Zoning changes

      Paper copies of measures: Town Manager's Office, Town Hall, Monday through Thursday 8:00AM-4:30PM, Friday 8:00AM -12:00PM.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      a. Measure 2027-024- Bylaw Amendment to allow “Miscellaneous professional and business offices and services, including but not limited to medical, legal, insurance, real estate offices, and other professional services” by right in the C-7.5 Commercial District (Downtown)

    Agenda: North Attleborough Town Council, Wed Oct 14
