    • A special permit for Hyperion Systems at 299 River Road: hearingHearing

      Topics

      Where
      299 River Road
      Permits

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Jake Marley of Hyperion Systems, LLC has applied for a special permit to install a 400kW ac (590.40kW dc) dual-use, elevated, large-scale, ground-mounted solar array within land in agricultural use at 299 River Road Whately and owned by The Chang Family Trust, pursuant to Article 171-33 under the Whately Zoning Ordinance.

    Words on the agenda. Dual-use: solar panels raised high enough that farming goes on underneath. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Special permit: permission the zoning bylaw lets a board grant for a use it allows only with conditions.
