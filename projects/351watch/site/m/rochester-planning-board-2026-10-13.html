Rochester Planning Board

Tue Oct 13, 7 pm Town Hall, or join online Open the agenda

Public hearing; no rules stated.

    • Solar farm with battery storage at 0 High Street: hearing continues from Sep 8

      High Street Solar 1, LLC and High Street Solar 2, LLC want a special permit and site plan approval for a ground-mounted solar farm with a battery energy storage system at 0 High Street, in the Residential-Agricultural district; the hearing is continued from September 8, 2026, and the land is owned by Ritter, C., LLC, Hawes, P., LLC and Hawes, M., LLC.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 22 Planning Board agenda. Conservation Commission wetlands filing (DEP SE 272-0665) heard Sep 15, continued from an earlier session.

      Solar, battery storage0 High StreetHigh Street Solar 1, LLC & High Street Solar 2, LLCHearing

      The board can vote or continue. The same night, under board business, it signs site plans and a mylar for 'High Street Solar Site A'.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      (continued from September 8, 2026) A Site Plan Review and Special Permit application filed by High Street Solar 1, LLC & High Street Solar 2, LLC … The applicant is proposing the installation and operation of a ground-mounted solar energy system with battery energy storage system (BESS) and associated site work. The property is located entirely within the Residential-Agricultural (RE/AG) District.

    • Walnut Plain Road solar farm hearing: request to move it to Oct 27

      Walnut Plain Road Solar 1, LLC wants a special permit and site plan approval for a large ground-mounted solar array at 0 Walnut Plain Road, land owned by BKK Holding, LLC in the Residential-Agricultural district; the applicant asked to continue the hearing to Oct 27.

      Before this: Sep 22 Planning Board: the applicant asked to continue to Oct 13. Conservation Commission wetlands filing (DEP SE 272-0668): continued at the applicant's request Sep 15 (to Oct 6) and Oct 6 (to Oct 20).

      Solar0 Walnut Plain RoadWalnut Plain Road Solar 1, LLCAsks to postpone to Tue Oct 27

      If the board agrees, there will be no discussion Oct 13; the hearing moves to Tue Oct 27, 7:00 p.m., Town Hall.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      (requested to continue until October 27, 2026) A Special Permit and Site Plan Review application filed by Walnut Plain Road Solar 1, LLC … The applicant is proposing the installation and operation of a ground mounted large scale photovoltaic solar array system and associated site work.
