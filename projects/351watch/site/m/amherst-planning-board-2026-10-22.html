Amherst Planning Board

When
Thu Oct 22, 6:30 pm
Where
Online meeting
Public comment
Join by Zoom or telephone; no in-person attendance by the public. After the hearing the Planning Board makes a recommendation; the City Council votes on the ordinance.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 17)

On the agenda

    • Ban on data centers in the zoning bylaw: joint Planning Board hearingHearing

      Zoning changes that would prohibit data centers, by amending Section 3.3 (Use Classifications and Standards) and Article 12 (Definitions) of the Zoning Bylaw. The Planning Board and the Town Council’s Community Resources Committee hear it together.

      Before this: The Town Council referred these changes on its Oct 5 agenda.

      Topics
      Data centers, zoning changes
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      2. Joint Public Hearing With Community Resources Committee – Zoning Amendment 7:00 PM - Amendments to Prohibit Data Centers: Section 3.3 Use Classifications and Standards, and Article 12 Definitions To consider amending the following sections of the Zoning Bylaw: Section 3.3 – Use Classifications and Standards and Article 12 Definitions.

    • Accessory dwelling unit size, half-stories and attics: joint zoning hearingHearing

      Zoning changes on how big an accessory dwelling unit can be (Section 5.0112, Gross Floor Area), on half-stories (Section 6.18, Minimum or Maximum Floors), and on the definition of attic (Section 12 Definitions). The Planning Board and the Town Council’s Community Resources Committee hear it together.

      Before this: The Town Council referred these changes on its Sep 28 agenda.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      1. Joint Public Hearing With Community Resources Committee – Zoning Amendment 6:35 PM - Amendments to Accessory Dwelling Unit Floor Area, Half-Stories, and Attics To consider amending the following sections of the Zoning Bylaw: Section 5.0112- Gross Floor Area, Section 6.18 – Minimum or Maximum Floors, and, Section 12 Definitions (attic).
