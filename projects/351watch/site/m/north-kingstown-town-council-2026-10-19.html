On the agenda

    • Solar panels on the Public Safety Complex: council discussion after variance winDiscussion

      The Town Council asked for a discussion of solar panels on the Public Safety Complex, after the town won its appeal to the State Building Board for a variance exempting the building from a solar panel requirement.

    Words on the agenda. Variance: permission to depart from a zoning rule because the lot’s shape, size or ground makes the rule a hardship.
