Seekonk Zoning Board of Appeals

When
Mon Oct 19, 6 pm
Where
Seekonk Human Services Community Meeting Room, 540 Arcade Avenue
Public comment
Public hearing: anyone can attend and ask to speak.
Agenda
Open the agenda (posted Oct 16)

On the agenda

    • 36 homes, 10 affordable, at 95 Sagamore Road: 40B hearing continues, traffic studyHearing

      Heritage Seekonk Development Inc. of Rehoboth wants a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build 18 buildings with 36 multi-family homes, 10 of them affordable, on about 30.30 acres at 95 Sagamore Road in an R-2 zone. The hearing, continued from April 13, May 4, June 1, July 6, August 3 and October 5, is to include the traffic study.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda. The Conservation Commission’s Sep 14 and Oct 5 agendas carried the 95 Sagamore Road wetlands filing, and the Planning Board discussed the 40B application on Oct 13.

      Topics

      1. 2026-10 Heritage Seekonk Development Inc., 7 Park Street #1, Rehoboth, MA, owner/petitioner, requesting a Comprehensive Permit pursuant to Massachusetts General Law Chapter 40B, and the Town of Seekonk, MA Zoning Board of Appeals Comprehensive Permit Regulations to allow for the construction of 18 (eighteen) buildings with a total of 36 (thirty-six) multi-family residential units

    Words on the agenda. Continued: put off to a later meeting; a continuance is the request to do that. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
