Auburn Town Meeting

When
Tue Oct 27
Where
Town Office
Who votes
Representative Town Meeting: elected members vote. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.
Warrant
Open the Town Meeting warrant (PDF)

On the warrant

    • One-year data-center moratorium, Article 20: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Town Manager asks Town Meeting to pause building permits for one year for Electronic Data Storage projects (data centers, use line 3.2.6.17 in the Table of Principal Uses), so the town can study and adopt more rules for them in a proposed new section 3.4 of the Auburn Zoning Bylaw.

      Topics
      Data centers, moratoriums
      Applicant
      Town Manager
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      Vote to enact a One-Year Moratorium on the issuance of building permits for Electronic Data Storage projects as currently listed in line 3.2.6.17 in the Table of Principal Uses and the associated Description of Use in order to allow for the Town to study and adopt additional regulations pertaining to such project per the proposed new section 3.4 of the Auburn Zoning Bylaw below. (Town Manager)

    • Inclusionary zoning, new Section 14, Article 14: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 14 would add a new Section 14, Inclusionary Zoning (rules on including affordable homes in new housing), to the Auburn Zoning Bylaws. The Assistant Town Manager/Economic Development Coordinator sponsors it. The Select Board recommends approval; the Finance Committee defers to the petitioner.

      Topics
      Zoning changes
      Applicant
      Assistant Town Manager/Economic Development Coordinator
      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the warrant:

      14 Vote to amend the Auburn Zoning Bylaws by adding a new Section 14 (Inclusionary Zoning). (Assistant Town Manager/Economic Development Coordinator) Recommend approval Defer to Petitioner

    Words on the warrant. Moratorium: a temporary ban. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit.
