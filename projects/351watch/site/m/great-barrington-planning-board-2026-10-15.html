Great Barrington Planning Board

Thu Oct 15, 6 pm Fire Station, or join online Open the agenda (PDF)

    • No more cap on hotel and motel rooms: Planning Board hearing.

      The change would delete Section 7.10.2 of the zoning bylaw, ending the maximum number of rental rooms allowed in hotels and motels.

      Zoning changesHearing

      After closing the hearing, the board votes whether to recommend the change to Town Meeting.

      Agenda wording

      Quoted from the agenda:

      Amend Section 7.10, Hotels and Motels, by deleting Section 7.10.2, Room Limits, in its entirety, including Sections 7.10.2.1 and 7.10.2.2, thereby eliminating the maximum number of rental rooms applicable to hotels and motels.

    Public hearing: anyone can speak; written comments go to the town clerk before the meeting.Agenda (PDF): Great Barrington Planning Board, Thu Oct 15
