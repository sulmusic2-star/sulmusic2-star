    Words on the agenda. Ground-mounted: panels on frames in a field, not on a roof. Decommissioning: taking a project down at the end of its life and restoring the land. Applicant: the person or company asking for the permit. By right: allowed under the zoning rules as they stand, with no special permit or hearing.
