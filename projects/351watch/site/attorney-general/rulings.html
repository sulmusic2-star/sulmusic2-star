15 of 15 moratoriums disapproved since Tracer Lane II; 105 of 107 zoning bylaws approved in whole or in part; 129 decisions in 94 towns, Jan 6, 2022 to Oct 7, 2026. Each decision letter links to the Attorney General’s public website; court rulings and city ordinances are included for context and labeled as such.
