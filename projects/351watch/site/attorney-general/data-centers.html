A Massachusetts town can pause data centers: state law protects solar and battery storage, not data centers. The Attorney General approved the moratoriums in Shutesbury (case 12047, Jan 20, 2026), Templeton (case 12294, Sep 22, 2026) and Gill (case 12422, Sep 24, 2026). A moratorium runs from the date the Attorney General approves it, or from the town vote where the bylaw says so, to the end date the bylaw names. Dates come from the warrant, the decision letter and the bylaw; a date we could not find in those reads "Not found".
