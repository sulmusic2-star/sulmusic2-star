    Town Meeting Marshfield High School Auditorium Warrant (PDF): Marshfield Town Meeting, Mon Oct 19

    • Town Meeting: Buy 30.98 acres off Holly Road for open space, $1,183,000: Town Meeting vote (Article 6)Vote

      The town would spend $1,183,000 in Community Preservation money to buy 30.98 acres of vacant land off Holly Road and protect it forever. The warrant says more open space helps the town avoid mandatory 40B housing developments.

      Before this: The Select Board voted on purchase and sale agreements for land off Holly Road and 1 Proctor Street at its Sep 21 meeting.

    • Brant Rock Village Overlay removal, Article 9: citizen petition, Town Meeting voteVote

      A citizen petition from Kathleen Creed and others would do away with the Brant Rock Village Overlay District, an extra layer of zoning rules for the Brant Rock village area, including removing its references and boundaries from the zoning maps.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s public hearing on the petition was on its Oct 5 agenda.
