SuttonWorcester County Consolidated permit
Battery storage and solar		 In effect Oct 1, 2026Applications accepted

Sutton said it would begin accepting consolidated local permit applications for small clean-energy facilities, including solar and battery storage, on Oct. 1, 2026, with the Town Manager serving as Local Government Representative. As of its July 2, 2026 notice the town had not decided whether to adopt a local permitting bylaw or rely on DOER's model rules (latest found).
