Bylaw rulings and town actions (1)

  • Consolidated permit, battery storage and solar. In effect, Oct 1, 2026.

    Sutton said it would begin accepting consolidated local permit applications for small clean-energy facilities, including solar and battery storage, on Oct 1, 2026, with the Town Manager serving as Local Government Representative. As of its July 2, 2026 notice the town had not decided whether to adopt a local permitting bylaw or rely on the state energy department’s model rules.
