Tue Oct 20

  1. 6 pm

    Town Meeting The Bromfield School Cronin Auditorium, 14 Massachusetts Ave Warrant: Harvard Town Meeting, Tue Oct 20

    • Town Meeting: Clean-energy bylaw for solar and battery storage, Article 5: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Planning Board asks Town Meeting to replace Harvard’s large-scale ground-mounted solar overlay district (§125-53) with a new Clean Energy Facilities bylaw: local siting standards and a consolidated permitting process for solar, and for the first time standards for battery energy storage systems (BESS). The Select Board unanimously recommends it; the Finance Committee took no position.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 Select Board and Sep 28 Planning Board agendas.

    • Inclusionary rule in the MBTA multifamily zone, Article 9: Town Meeting voteVote

      The Planning Board asks Town Meeting to amend §125-60, the MBTA Communities Multi-Family Overlay District, to make clear how the inclusionary affordable housing requirement applies inside the district, with other corrections. The warrant says these refine an adopted district, not its core standards. The Select Board unanimously recommends it; the Finance Committee took no position.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 15 Select Board and Sep 28 Planning Board agendas.

    Warrant: Harvard Town Meeting, Tue Oct 20

Tue Sep 15

  1. 7 pm

    Select Board Online meeting Agenda: Harvard Select Board, Tue Sep 15

    • Select Board: New rules for solar farms and battery storage in Harvard’s draft Town Meeting warrant

      A draft Town Meeting article would set local siting rules and a single permit process for solar projects and, for the first time, rules for battery storage, to fit the state rules (225 CMR 29.00) and the 2024 state clean-energy law. The Select Board is reviewing the draft warrant (item 8, 7:50), not voting on the bylaw.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s hearing on this article was under way Sep 14 (per its Sep 28 agenda minutes).

    • Solar credit deals of up to 20 years in Harvard’s draft warrant (not a project)

      A draft warrant article would let the town enter net metering credit purchase agreements with the owner of a solar facility for up to 20 years and sign the related papers. A town power-credit contract, not a construction project. The Select Board is reviewing the draft warrant.

    • Solar credit deal in Harvard’s draft Town Meeting warrant (not a project)

      A draft warrant article would let the town sign a 30-year lease and net metering credit purchase agreement with the owner of a solar facility; any agreement over three years needs a Town Meeting vote. A town power-credit contract, not a construction project. The Select Board is reviewing the draft warrant.

    Agenda: Harvard Select Board, Tue Sep 15
