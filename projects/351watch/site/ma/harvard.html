Battery storage and solar		 Proposed Aug 17, 2026Planning Board hearing (scheduled)

A Planning Board draft would replace Harvard's large-scale solar overlay district with a town-wide Clean Energy Facilities bylaw that regulates BESS for the first time and adds a consolidated local permit option. The first Planning Board hearing was scheduled for Aug. 17, 2026 (latest found).
