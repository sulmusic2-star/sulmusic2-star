    • Selectboard: New state permitting process for small clean energy projects: planning director briefingDiscussion

      Maureen Pollock, the planning director, briefs the Selectboard on the state’s new consolidated local permitting process for small clean energy projects (225 CMR 29.00). Under the 2024 Climate Act, towns must decide a complete application within 12 months with all local permits reviewed together; if no decision is issued the project is approved by default, and appeals go to the state Energy Facilities Siting Board.
