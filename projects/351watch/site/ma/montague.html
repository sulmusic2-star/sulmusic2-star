Mon Oct 19

  1. New state permitting process for small clean energy projects: planning director briefing

    Selectboard, 6:25 pm, online

    The Selectboard took this up. Also on the agenda, it took up new state permit process for small clean energy projects: Selectboard briefing.

    Read the Oct 19 agenda (PDF): Montague Selectboard

    Details

    Speaking: Public comment period at 6:00 PM; individuals are limited to two minutes each. Public comment period at 6:00 PM, early in the meeting: two minutes per person, strictly timed.

    • Selectboard: New state permitting process for small clean energy projects: planning director briefingDiscussion

      Maureen Pollock, the planning director, briefs the Selectboard on the state’s new consolidated local permitting process for small clean energy projects (225 CMR 29.00). Under the 2024 Climate Act, towns must decide a complete application within 12 months with all local permits reviewed together; if no decision is issued the project is approved by default, and appeals go to the state Energy Facilities Siting Board.

    • New state permit process for small clean energy projects: Selectboard briefingDiscussion

      Planning Director Maureen Pollock briefs the Selectboard on the consolidated local permitting process that state rules (225 CMR 29.00, under the 2024 Climate Act) now require for small clean energy projects: one application covering every local land-use and environmental permit, decided within 12 months or approved by default. Towns had to take part starting October 1, 2026, and the memo says the town should name a Local Government Representative to receive applications and check that they are complete.
