    • Planning Board: Zoning clean-up changes for the Nov 17 Town Meeting: Planning Board hearingHearing

      Six housekeeping changes: replace “development plan” wording with “site plan”, drop references to a retail district that no longer exists, rename the Land Use Director to Planning Director, delete the “two dwellings on one lot” section, change a common-driveway limit from 4 lots to 4 primary dwellings, and let the Planning Board set stormwater permit fees.

    • Zoning amendments for Special Town Meeting: Planning Board hearingHearing

      Public hearing on zoning bylaw amendments the Planning Board is sending to the Special Town Meeting, where voters decide them. Votes may be taken at the meeting.

What the state has said about Lunenburg’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Feb 14, 2025). Article 24 (May 4, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Lunenburg regulated battery storage both as part of solar facilities and as a standalone use. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11488

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Apr 24, 2024). Article 15 (Nov 14, 2023 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Lunenburg allowed solar parking canopies by special permit in its Commercial and Office Park/Industrial districts. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11276
