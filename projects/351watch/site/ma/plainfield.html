Bylaw rulings and town actions (1)

  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Withdrawn, Nov 12, 2025.

    The Planning Board proposed a temporary moratorium on special permit applications for solar arrays and battery storage (Article 3), but at the Nov 12, 2025 Special Town Meeting the Select Board took no action and no vote was held. A Select Board member said the article would not have been legally binding.
