Attorney General reviews of town bylaws (3)

  • Zoning bylaw, battery storage. Adopted, Jun 2, 2026.

    Annual Town Meeting voters in June 2026 passed Article 24, a standalone BESS zoning section based on DOER's model bylaw that bans mid- and large-scale systems in the Aquifer Protection District and within 300 feet of drinking-water or agricultural wells. It follows a Nov. 12, 2025 amendment capping BESS at 250 kWh in the aquifer district.

    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately voters pass town meeting articles (June 4, 2026)
    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately voters to consider new BESS bylaw at Town Meeting (May 29, 2026)
    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately special town meeting (Nov. 13, 2025)

  • Attorney General: approved, Jun 5, 2026. Article 6 (Nov. 12, 2025 STM), zoning regulation.

    Whately barred BESS over 250 kWh of aggregate capacity in its Aquifer Protection District and added BESS definitions. The AG approved it without comment; the letter does not describe the article, so its content comes from news coverage. Decision letter, case 12144

  • Attorney General: approved, Oct 4, 2023. Article 24 (May 23, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Whately required solar utility connections to be underground where feasible. The AG approved it. Decision letter, case 11126
