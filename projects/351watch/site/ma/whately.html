Bylaw rulings and town actions (3)

  • Zoning bylaw, battery storage. Adopted, Jun 2, 2026.

    Annual Town Meeting voters in June 2026 passed Article 24, a standalone battery storage zoning section based on the state energy department’s model bylaw that bans mid- and large-scale systems in the Aquifer Protection District and within 300 feet of drinking-water or agricultural wells. It follows a Nov. 12, 2025 amendment capping battery storage at 250 kWh in the aquifer district.

    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately voters pass town meeting articles (June 4, 2026)
    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately voters to consider new BESS bylaw at Town Meeting (May 29, 2026)
    • Greenfield Recorder: Whately special town meeting (Nov. 13, 2025)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Jun 5, 2026). Article 6 (Nov. 12, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Whately barred battery storage over 250 kWh of aggregate capacity in its Aquifer Protection District and added battery storage definitions. The Attorney General approved it without comment; the letter does not describe the article, so its content comes from news coverage. Decision letter, case 12144

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Oct 4, 2023). Article 24 (May 23, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Whately required solar utility connections to be underground where feasible. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11126
