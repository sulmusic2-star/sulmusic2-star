What the state has said about Gill’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Sep 24, 2026). Article 7 (May 4, 2026 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Gill's Article 7 imposed a moratorium on battery storage and data centers until May 3, 2027. The Attorney General disapproved the battery storage part but approved the data-center part, since data centers are not a protected use. Decision letter, case 12422

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Sep 24, 2026). Article 7 (May 4, 2026 Annual Town Meeting), data-center part, moratorium.

    The same letter approved the data-center half of Gill's Article 7: a moratorium on data centers until May 3, 2027 stands, because data centers aren't a protected use. The battery-storage half was struck. Decision letter, case 12422
