Earlier meetings, back to September

Wed Oct 21

  1. Accessory dwelling units bylaw, Article 8: Planning Board review and possible re-vote

    Planning Board, 5:30 pm, Town Hall Hearing Room 1146, or join online

    The Planning Board took this up. Also on the agenda: short-term rentals stay in zoning bylaw, Articles 6 and 7: sunset moves to 2027 or 2029, and 1 more.

    Read the Oct 21 agenda (PDF): Yarmouth Planning Board

    Details

    No public hearing is listed. The agenda doesn’t say whether you can comment; the town clerk would know.

    • Planning Board: Accessory dwelling units bylaw, Article 8: Planning Board review and possible re-voteVote

      Article 8 for the November 16 Special Town Meeting would amend Yarmouth’s Accessory Apartment zoning bylaw to match changes required by the Affordable Homes Act (Section 8 of Chapter 150 of the Acts of 2024) and the state’s rules for Protected Use Accessory Dwelling Units (760 CMR 71.00), keeping as many existing provisions as the rules allow. The Planning Board reviews Town Counsel’s input, may make minor amendments, and may re-vote its recommendation.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 23 and Oct 7 Planning Board agendas and the Sep 29 and Oct 6 Select Board agendas.

    • Short-term rentals stay in zoning bylaw, Articles 6 and 7: sunset moves to 2027 or 2029Discussion

      Article 6 would keep short-term rentals in the zoning bylaw while the new General Bylaw Chapter 110 takes over on January 1, 2028: it changes the use table to “no” for the MU and MOD districts, bars them in units built under the ROAD overlay zoning, and moves the Section 418 sunset to December 31, 2027. It would be withdrawn if the General Bylaw fails. Article 7 is the fallback if the other articles fail: it extends the sunset from November 30, 2026 to December 31, 2029. Each needs a 2/3 vote at Town Meeting.

      Before this: The draft articles were on the Sep 23 and Oct 7 Planning Board agendas; the Select Board had the Special Town Meeting warrant on Sep 29 and Oct 6.

    • Short-term rental and accessory unit articles for Nov 16 Town Meeting: final reviewDiscussion

      The board goes over Town Counsel’s comments and may make minor changes to four draft articles for the Special Town Meeting: a new General Bylaw Chapter 110 regulating short-term rentals (Article 5), two zoning amendments keeping short-term rentals in the zoning bylaw while extending their sunset clause (Articles 6 and 7), and changes to the accessory apartment zoning bylaw to match the state’s Affordable Homes Act (Article 8). It may re-vote its recommendations on the warrant articles as needed.

      Before this: The draft articles were on the Sep 23 and Oct 7 Planning Board agendas; the Select Board had the Special Town Meeting warrant on Sep 29 and Oct 6.
