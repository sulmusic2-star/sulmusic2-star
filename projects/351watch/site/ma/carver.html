Attorney General reviews of town bylaws (3)

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Nov 13, 2023. Article 27 (Apr. 11, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Carver's large-scale solar amendments were approved except a cap of five acres of deforestation per lot and herbicide/pesticide rules. Decision letter, case 10941

  • Attorney General: approved, Nov 10, 2023. Articles 28 and 29 (Apr. 11, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Carver added Tier 1-3 BESS requirements and listed where each tier is allowed by right, by special permit or prohibited. The AG approved it. Decision letter, case 10941

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Nov 14, 2022. Articles 26 and 38 (Apr. 12, 2022 ATM), moratorium.

    Carver adopted a one-year ban on new or expanded large-scale solar (Art. 26) and an 11.5-month ban on battery storage (Art. 38). The AG disapproved both because they were not grounded in articulated health, safety or welfare concerns. Decision letter, case 10526
