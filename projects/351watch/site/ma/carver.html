Attorney General decisions

The Attorney General's rulings on Carver’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. Statewide record by type of bylaw.

DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Nov 13, 2023 CarverArticle 27 (Apr. 11, 2023 ATM) Zoning regulation
solar		 Approved in part

Carver's large-scale solar amendments were approved except a cap of five acres of deforestation per lot and herbicide/pesticide rules.

Decision letter, case 10941
Nov 10, 2023 CarverArticles 28 and 29 (Apr. 11, 2023 ATM) Zoning regulation
BESS		 Approved

Carver added Tier 1-3 BESS requirements and listed where each tier is allowed by right, by special permit or prohibited. The AG approved it.

Decision letter, case 10941
Nov 14, 2022 CarverArticles 26 and 38 (Apr. 12, 2022 ATM) Moratorium
both		 Disapproved

Carver adopted a one-year ban on new or expanded large-scale solar (Art. 26) and an 11.5-month ban on battery storage (Art. 38). The AG disapproved both because they were not grounded in articulated health, safety or welfare concerns.
