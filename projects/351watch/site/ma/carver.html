Bylaw rulings and town actions (3)

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Nov 13, 2023). Article 27 (Apr. 11, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Carver's large-scale solar amendments were approved except a cap of five acres of deforestation per lot and herbicide/pesticide rules. Decision letter, case 10941 (solar letter)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Nov 10, 2023). Articles 28 and 29 (Apr. 11, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Carver added Tier 1-3 battery storage requirements and listed where each tier is allowed by right, by special permit or prohibited. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 10941 (battery storage systems letter)

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Nov 14, 2022). Articles 26 and 38 (Apr. 12, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Carver adopted a one-year ban on new or expanded large-scale solar (Art. 26) and an 11.5-month ban on battery storage (Art. 38). The Attorney General disapproved both because they were not grounded in articulated health, safety or welfare concerns. Decision letter, case 10526
