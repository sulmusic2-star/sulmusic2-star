Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Attorney General: approved, Aug 13, 2026. Article 22 (May 11, 2026 ATM), consolidated permit bylaw.

    Medway adopted a Consolidated Local Permit section for small clean energy facilities to conform to the 2024 Climate Act and 225 CMR 29.00. The AG approved it; it is the only decision tagged Small Clean Energy Facilities in the portal as of Oct. 10, 2026. Decision letter, case 12251

  • Attorney General: approved, Feb 10, 2026. Article 10 (Nov. 12, 2025 STM), zoning regulation.

    Medway required commercial BESS operators to submit third-party safety certification before operating. The AG approved it, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act. Decision letter, case 12085

  • Attorney General: approved, Aug 7, 2023. Article 26 (May 8, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Medway amended its ground-mounted solar definition and vegetation-control rules. The AG approved them, with comments. Decision letter, case 10925

  • Attorney General: approved in part, May 17, 2023. Articles 15, 16 and 18 (Nov. 14, 2022 STM), zoning regulation.

    Medway's BESS and solar amendments were approved except Schedule of Uses text that banned Tier 2 BESS in every district but one. Decision letter, case 10779

  • Attorney General: approved, May 17, 2022. Article 10 (Nov. 15, 2021 STM), moratorium.

    Medway's time-limited moratorium on BESS construction in its Energy Resource district was approved because it was limited in time and geographic scope and served a planning purpose. This is the only clean-energy moratorium the AG approved in 2022-2026, and it came two weeks before the SJC's Tracer Lane II ruling. Decision letter, case 10409
