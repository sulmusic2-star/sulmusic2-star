Bylaw rulings and town actions (5)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Aug 13, 2026). Article 22 (May 11, 2026 Annual Town Meeting), consolidated permit bylaw.

    Medway adopted a Consolidated Local Permit section for small clean energy facilities to conform to the 2024 Climate Act and 225 CMR 29.00. The Attorney General approved it; it is the only decision tagged Small Clean Energy Facilities in the portal as of Oct 10, 2026. Decision letter, case 12251

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Feb 10, 2026). Article 10 (Nov 12, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Medway required commercial battery storage operators to submit third-party safety certification before operating. The Attorney General approved it, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act. Decision letter, case 12085

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Aug 7, 2023). Article 26 (May 8, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Medway amended its ground-mounted solar definition and vegetation-control rules. The Attorney General approved them, with comments. Decision letter, case 10925

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated May 17, 2023). Articles 15, 16 and 18 (Nov 14, 2022 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Medway's battery storage and solar amendments were approved except Schedule of Uses text that banned Tier 2 battery storage in every district but one. Decision letter, case 10779

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated May 17, 2022). Article 10 (Nov 15, 2021 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Medway's time-limited moratorium on battery storage construction in its Energy Resource district was approved because it was limited in time and geographic scope and served a planning purpose. This is the only clean-energy moratorium the Attorney General approved in 2022-2026, and it came two weeks before the Supreme Judicial Court's Tracer Lane II ruling. Decision letter, case 10409
