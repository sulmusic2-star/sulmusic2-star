Attorney General decisions

Decisions on Medway’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. What survives review statewide.

DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Aug 13, 2026 MedwayArticle 22 (May 11, 2026 ATM) Consolidated permit bylaw
both		 Approved

Medway adopted a Consolidated Local Permit section for small clean energy facilities to conform to the 2024 Climate Act and 225 CMR 29.00. The AG approved it; it is the only decision tagged Small Clean Energy Facilities in the portal as of Oct. 10, 2026.

Decision letter, case 12251
Feb 10, 2026 MedwayArticle 10 (Nov. 12, 2025 STM) Zoning regulation
BESS		 Approved

Medway required commercial BESS operators to submit third-party safety certification before operating. The AG approved it, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act.

Decision letter, case 12085
Aug 7, 2023 MedwayArticle 26 (May 8, 2023 ATM) Zoning regulation
solar		 Approved

Medway amended its ground-mounted solar definition and vegetation-control rules. The AG approved them, with comments.

Decision letter, case 10925
May 17, 2023 MedwayArticles 15, 16 and 18 (Nov. 14, 2022 STM) Zoning regulation
both		 Approved in part

Medway's BESS and solar amendments were approved except Schedule of Uses text that banned Tier 2 BESS in every district but one.

Decision letter, case 10779
May 17, 2022 MedwayArticle 10 (Nov. 15, 2021 STM) Moratorium
BESS		 Approved

Medway's time-limited moratorium on BESS construction in its Energy Resource district was approved because it was limited in time and geographic scope and served a planning purpose. This is the only clean-energy moratorium the AG approved in 2022-2026, and it came two weeks before the SJC's Tracer Lane II ruling.
