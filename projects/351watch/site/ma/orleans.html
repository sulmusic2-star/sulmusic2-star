    • Zoning Board of Appeals: Affordable homes at 66 & 76 Route 6A: 40B hearing continues (Case 2026-06)Hearing

      Housing Assistance Corporation and Habitat for Humanity Cape Cod seek a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build affordable rental and homeownership housing, in phases, at 66 & 76 Route 6A in the Village Center (VC) district. They ask for waivers from local zoning, subdivision rules and town fees, among them building height, units in a single structure, lot area, setbacks and parking.
