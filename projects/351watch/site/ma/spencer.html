Bylaws and state rulings (4)

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Aug 20, 2024. Articles 27, 28 and 29 (May 2, 2024 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Spencer's replacement solar and BESS regulations were approved except rules on herbicides, pesticides and fertilizers at agrivoltaic installations. Decision letter, case 11401

  • Attorney General: no action (moot), Jul 19, 2024. Articles 14 and 15 (Nov. 9, 2023 STM), moratorium.

    Spencer's temporary moratoria on large ground-mounted solar and on energy systems as a principal use were never ruled on. The AG took no action because the town adopted solar and storage zoning in May 2024, which ended the moratoria by their own terms. Decision letter, case 11260

  • Attorney General: approved in part, May 30, 2023. Article 10 (Nov. 10, 2022 STM), zoning regulation.

    Spencer's solar amendments were approved except a ban on energy storage systems as a principal use in every district and a cap of 25 large-scale solar facilities town-wide. Decision letter, case 10804

  • Attorney General: approved, Feb 28, 2022. Article 8 (Nov. 17, 2021 STM), zoning regulation.

    Amendments to Spencer's solar PV bylaw on decommissioning surety, setbacks and visual mitigation were approved. The AG cautioned the town to apply them consistently with s. 3. Decision letter, case 10436
