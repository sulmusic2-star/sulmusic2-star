Spencer's replacement solar and BESS regulations were approved except rules on herbicides, pesticides and fertilizers at agrivoltaic installations.

Decision letter, case 11401
Jul 19, 2024 SpencerArticles 14 and 15 (Nov. 9, 2023 STM) Moratorium
both		 No action (moot)

Spencer's temporary moratoria on large ground-mounted solar and on energy systems as a principal use were never ruled on. The AG took no action because the town adopted solar and storage zoning in May 2024, which ended the moratoria by their own terms.
