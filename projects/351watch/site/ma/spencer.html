What the state has said about Spencer’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Aug 20, 2024). Articles 27, 28 and 29 (May 2, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Spencer's replacement solar and battery storage regulations were approved except rules on herbicides, pesticides and fertilizers at agrivoltaic installations. Decision letter, case 11401

  • Attorney General: no action (moot) (letter dated Jul 19, 2024). Articles 14 and 15 (Nov 9, 2023 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Spencer's temporary moratoriums on large ground-mounted solar and on energy systems as a principal use were never ruled on. The Attorney General took no action because the town adopted solar and storage zoning in May 2024, which ended the moratoriums by their own terms. Decision letter, case 11260

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated May 30, 2023). Article 10 (Nov 10, 2022 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Spencer's solar amendments were approved except a ban on energy storage systems as a principal use in every district and a cap of 25 large-scale solar facilities town-wide. Decision letter, case 10804

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Feb 28, 2022). Article 8 (Nov 17, 2021 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Amendments to Spencer's solar PV bylaw on decommissioning surety, setbacks and visual mitigation were approved. The Attorney General cautioned the town to apply them consistently with § 3. Decision letter, case 10436
