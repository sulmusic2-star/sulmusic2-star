Bylaw rulings and town actions (4)

  • Zoning bylaw, battery storage and solar. Adopted, Oct 1, 2026.

    On Oct 1, 2026, Special Town Meeting voters unanimously approved Article 4, a new Clean Energy zoning section with revised solar and wind rules and the town's first battery storage rules, based largely on the state energy department’s model framework. As a town zoning bylaw it is subject to Attorney General review.

    • Town of Lee: Oct. 1, 2026 Special Town Meeting warrant
    • Berkshire Edge: Lee voters approve all articles at Special Town Meeting (Oct. 2, 2026)

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Aug 31, 2026). Article 26 (May 14, 2026 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Lee adopted a moratorium on commercial battery storage through Nov 14, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it for lacking justification and conflicting with the consolidated permit requirement starting Oct 1, 2026. Decision letter, case 12338
