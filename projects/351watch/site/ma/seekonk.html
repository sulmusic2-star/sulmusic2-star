  1. 36 homes, 10 affordable, at 95 Sagamore Road: 40B hearing continues, traffic study

    Zoning Board of Appeals, 6 pm, Seekonk Human Services Community Meeting Room, 540 Arcade Avenue

    The Zoning Board of Appeals took this up.

    Read the Oct 19 agenda: Seekonk Zoning Board of Appeals

    Details

    • Zoning Board of Appeals: 36 homes, 10 affordable, at 95 Sagamore Road: 40B hearing continues, traffic studyHearing

      Heritage Seekonk Development Inc. of Rehoboth wants a comprehensive permit (the state’s 40B affordable-housing permit that can override local zoning) to build 18 buildings with 36 multi-family homes, 10 of them affordable, on about 30.30 acres at 95 Sagamore Road in an R-2 zone. The hearing, continued from April 13, May 4, June 1, July 6, August 3 and October 5, is to include the traffic study.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 Zoning Board of Appeals agenda. The Conservation Commission’s Sep 14 and Oct 5 agendas carried the 95 Sagamore Road wetlands filing, and the Planning Board discussed the 40B application on Oct 13.
