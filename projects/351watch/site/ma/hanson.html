Bylaw rulings and town actions (4)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Aug 31, 2026). Article 34 (May 4, 2026 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Hanson made further battery storage amendments. The Attorney General approved them. Decision letter, case 12322

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Feb 24, 2025). Article 12 (Oct 7, 2024 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    After its 2023 moratorium was struck, Hanson allowed Tier I battery storage by right everywhere and Tier II by right with site plan review in three of six districts. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11578

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Mar 7, 2024). Article 34 (May 1, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Hanson's battery storage article imposed a two-year town-wide moratorium on Tier 1 battery storage and allowed Tier 2 only in an overlay district that was never mapped. The Attorney General disapproved the whole article. Decision letter, case 10916 (battery storage systems letter)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Nov 6, 2023). Article 35 (May 1, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Hanson allowed small and medium ground-mounted solar as of right with site plan review. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 10916 (letter dated Nov 6, 2023)
