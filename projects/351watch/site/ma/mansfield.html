  1. 7 pm

    Planning Board Town Hall Meeting Room 3, or join online Agenda: Mansfield Planning Board, Wed Oct 28

    • Planning Board: 55-and-older housing, B4 district and other zoning amendments: Planning Board placeholderDiscussion

      A standing placeholder for zoning bylaw amendments the Planning Board is working on: residential facilities for residents 55 years of age or older (the Clemmey proposal), two B4 Zoning District changes (the commercial component and multifamily housing by special permit), a permitting clarification for the North Main Street Overlay District, and new or amended uses under Section 230-3.5 such as coworking offices, pet services, food incubators and makerspaces. Also on the list: Parkway/Transit Oriented Development, scenic road and inclusionary zoning ideas from individual members, and the board’s own rules and fees.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 23 Planning Board agenda.
