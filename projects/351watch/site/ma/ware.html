Bylaws and state rulings (2)

  • Attorney General: approved, Dec 11, 2023. Article 20 (May 8, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    After its 2022 moratorium was struck, Ware adopted a BESS zoning section with definitions, an aquifer-district rule and use-table entries. The AG approved it. Decision letter, case 11085

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Mar 15, 2023. Article 22 (May 9, 2022 ATM), moratorium.

    Ware adopted a roughly 13-month moratorium on battery storage even though it had regulated BESS since 2020. The AG disapproved it as unsupported by health or safety evidence and not adopted for a legitimate planning purpose. Decision letter, case 10725
