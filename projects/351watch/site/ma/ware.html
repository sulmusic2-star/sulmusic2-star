Bylaw rulings and town actions (2)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Dec 11, 2023). Article 20 (May 8, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    After its 2022 moratorium was struck, Ware adopted a battery storage zoning section with definitions, an aquifer-district rule and use-table entries. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11085

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Mar 15, 2023). Article 22 (May 9, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Ware adopted a roughly 13-month moratorium on battery storage even though it had regulated battery storage since 2020. The Attorney General disapproved it as unsupported by health or safety evidence and not adopted for a legitimate planning purpose. Decision letter, case 10725
