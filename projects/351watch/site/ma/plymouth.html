Sat Oct 17

  1. 8 am

    Town Meeting Plymouth North High School at 41 Obery Street Warrant (PDF): Plymouth Town Meeting, Sat Oct 17

    Speaking: Elected Town Meeting members vote. Other registered Plymouth voters may speak with 48 hours’ notice to, and permission from, the Town Moderator, and a request to the Town Clerk.

    • Town Meeting: One-year moratorium on new data centers: Town Meeting vote (Article 16)Vote

      No new data-center applications, permits, construction, expansion or conversions from October 17, 2026 to October 16, 2027, to give the Planning Board time to write rules for the Fall 2027 Special Town Meeting.

      Before this: The Planning Board discussed a data center moratorium article Aug 26 and held a public hearing on it Sep 23; the Select Board discussed Article 16 on Sep 22.

    • Senior housing zoning update; no “Elderly Housing” in commercial zones: vote (Article 14)Vote

      Article 14 swaps “Elderly” for “Senior” throughout the zoning bylaw, changes the “Elderly Household” definition and the parking rules for some senior housing, and stops allowing the existing Elderly Housing use in the General Commercial, Arterial Commercial and Mixed Commerce zones.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing on the senior housing article Aug 26; the Select Board took a 4-1 vote on Article 14 at its Sep 22 meeting.

    • Looser light-industrial building rules: height, setbacks, floor area: vote (Article 15)Vote

      Article 15 reduces setback and special permit requirements in the Light Industrial Building Height Overlay District (LIBHOD), leaves mechanical equipment out of height measurements in the Light Industrial District, and drops the 0.25 maximum floor area ratio for Light Industry and Renewable/Alternate Energy Manufacturing uses.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing on the industrial zoning updates Aug 26; the Select Board voted on Article 15 at its Sep 22 meeting (one member abstained).

    Warrant (PDF): Plymouth Town Meeting, Sat Oct 17
