Attorney General reviews of town bylaws (2)

  • Moratorium, battery storage. Expired, Sep 30, 2026.

    Zoning ordinance Section 5-31 (dated Feb. 26, 2026) made battery energy storage systems a prohibited use in all districts as an interim restriction, covering standalone systems and accessory systems of 250 kWh or more. By its terms it ran through Sept. 30, 2026 or until superseding zoning is adopted; no extension had been found as of Oct. 10, 2026, so it appears to have lapsed.

    • City of Westfield: Zoning Ordinance Section 5-31, Interim Restriction for BESS
    • Westfield Planning Board minutes, Feb. 3, 2026 (hearing on interim restriction)
    • Westfield City Council hearing notice, Feb. 5, 2026

  • Attorney General: adopted (city; no ag review), Feb 26, 2026. Zoning Ordinance Section 5-31, Interim Restriction for BESS, moratorium.

    Westfield made BESS a prohibited use in all districts as an interim restriction through Sept. 30, 2026. As a city ordinance it never went to the AG, and the moratoria the AG strikes for towns have no counterpart review for cities. Source
