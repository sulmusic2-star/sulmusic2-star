Tracked local actions

WestfieldHampden County Moratorium
Battery storage		 Expired Sep 30, 2026Set to expire

Zoning ordinance Section 5-31 (dated Feb. 26, 2026) made battery energy storage systems a prohibited use in all districts as an interim restriction, covering standalone systems and accessory systems of 250 kWh or more. By its terms it ran through Sept. 30, 2026 or until superseding zoning is adopted; no extension had been found as of Oct. 10, 2026, so it appears to have lapsed.
