Bylaw rulings and town actions (2)

  • Moratorium, battery storage. Expired, Sep 30, 2026.

    Zoning ordinance Section 5-31 (dated Feb 26, 2026) made battery energy storage systems a prohibited use in all districts as an interim restriction, covering standalone systems and accessory systems of 250 kWh or more. By its terms it ran through Sep 30, 2026 or until superseding zoning is adopted; no extension had been found as of Oct 10, 2026, so it appears to have lapsed.

    • City of Westfield: Zoning Ordinance Section 5-31, Interim Restriction for BESS
    • Westfield Planning Board minutes, Feb. 3, 2026 (hearing on interim restriction)
    • Westfield City Council hearing notice, Feb. 5, 2026

  • City council: adopted (city; no attorney general review) (decided Feb 26, 2026). Zoning Ordinance Section 5-31, Interim Restriction for battery storage, moratorium.

    Westfield made battery storage a prohibited use in all districts as an interim restriction through Sep 30, 2026. As a city ordinance it never went to the Attorney General, and the moratoriums the Attorney General strikes for towns have no counterpart review for cities. Source
