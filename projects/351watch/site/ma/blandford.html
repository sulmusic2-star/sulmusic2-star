What the state has said about Blandford’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Mar 11, 2026). Article 1 (Nov 17, 2025 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Blandford adopted a moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and battery storage through May 31, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it, noting that it had approved a Blandford solar moratorium in 2019 but has disapproved such moratoriums since Tracer Lane II. Decision letter, case 12153

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Jun 26, 2024). Articles 6 and 7 (Mar 18, 2024 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Blandford adopted a new battery storage section and added Tier 1-3 battery storage to its Schedule of Uses. The Attorney General approved both; a related Article 5 was put on procedural hold. Decision letter, case 11317

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated May 29, 2024). Article 25 (June 6, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Blandford's new Schedule of Uses sorts ground-mounted solar into small, medium and large categories with permit paths. The Attorney General approved it, with § 3 comments. Decision letter, case 11165
