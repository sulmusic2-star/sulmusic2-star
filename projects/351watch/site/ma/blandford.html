BlandfordHampden County Moratorium
Battery storage and solar		 Disapproved by AG Mar 11, 2026AG decision

The Nov. 17, 2025 Special Town Meeting adopted Article 1, a moratorium on large-scale ground-mounted solar and battery storage through May 31, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it on March 11, 2026 (Case #12153), finding no articulated health, safety or welfare justification.

Blandford adopted a moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and BESS through May 31, 2026. The AG disapproved it, noting that it had approved a Blandford solar moratorium in 2019 but has disapproved such moratoria since Tracer Lane II.
