  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Disapproved by AG, Mar 11, 2026.

    The Nov. 17, 2025 Special Town Meeting adopted Article 1, a moratorium on large-scale ground-mounted solar and battery storage through May 31, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it on March 11, 2026 (Case #12153), finding no articulated health, safety or welfare justification.

    • Attorney General decision, Blandford Case #12153 (Mar. 11, 2026)
    • Foley Hoag: Massachusetts AGO rejects yet another municipal solar and battery storage moratorium (Mar. 2026)
    • State Impact Center: AG disapproved Blandford moratorium

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Mar 11, 2026. Article 1 (Nov. 17, 2025 STM), moratorium.

    Blandford adopted a moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and BESS through May 31, 2026. The AG disapproved it, noting that it had approved a Blandford solar moratorium in 2019 but has disapproved such moratoria since Tracer Lane II. Decision letter, case 12153

  • Attorney General: approved, Jun 26, 2024. Articles 6 and 7 (Mar. 18, 2024 STM), zoning regulation.

    Blandford adopted a new BESS section and added Tier 1-3 BESS to its Schedule of Uses. The AG approved both; a related Article 5 was put on procedural hold. Decision letter, case 11317

  • Attorney General: approved, May 29, 2024. Article 25 (June 6, 2022 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Blandford's new Schedule of Uses sorts ground-mounted solar into small, medium and large categories with permit paths. The AG approved it, with s. 3 comments. Decision letter, case 11165
