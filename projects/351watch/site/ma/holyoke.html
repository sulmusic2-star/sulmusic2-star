Tue Oct 20

  1. 6:30 pm

    Select Board Holyoke City Hall, 536 Dwight St, or on Zoom Agenda: Holyoke Select Board, Tue Oct 20

    • Select Board: Ad-hoc committee to study data centers in Holyoke: City Council voteVote

      The Ordinance Committee recommends adopting, as amended, an order for the City Council President to set up by ordinance a temporary ad-hoc committee, ending by June 30, 2027, to study what commercial data center development could mean for Holyoke (power and water demand, environment, tax revenue, zoning, public safety) and recommend zoning and permitting rules. The members would come from Holyoke Gas and Electric, the water works or Water Commission, the Massachusetts Green High-Performing Computing Center, the Planning Board, the Conservation Commission, the Office of Planning and Development, a resident and the City Council.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 6 City Council agenda, in the Ordinance Committee’s pending items.
