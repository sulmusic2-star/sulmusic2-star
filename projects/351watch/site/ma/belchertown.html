What the state has said about Belchertown’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Nov 22, 2024). Article 28 (May 13, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Belchertown added a battery storage section with siting, design, monitoring and removal standards. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11517

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Nov 14, 2022). Article 22 (May 9, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Belchertown's new commercial solar bylaw was approved except a ban on herbicides, rodenticides and other pesticides, which is preempted. Decision letter, case 10613
