What the state has said about Leyden’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Jan 30, 2026). Article 9 (July 16, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Leyden allowed commercial or industrial-scale battery facilities by special permit. The Attorney General approved it but flagged unclear wording and its earlier disapproval of Leyden's standalone-battery storage ban. Decision letter, case 12006

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Apr 16, 2024). Article 9 (Mar 20, 2023 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Leyden's large-scale solar section was approved except prohibitions on battery energy storage facilities (effectively a standalone-battery storage ban) and a herbicide ban. Decision letter, case 10919
