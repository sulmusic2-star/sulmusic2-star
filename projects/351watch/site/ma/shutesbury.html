Attorney General decisions

The Attorney General's rulings on Shutesbury’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. Statewide record by type of bylaw.

DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Nov 25, 2024 ShutesburyArticle 26 (Apr. 27, 2024 ATM) General bylaw (licensing)
BESS		 Disapproved

Shutesbury's general bylaw required a license from a new seven-member Energy Storage System Licensing Board to operate any non-residential storage system. It was disapproved because it regulated land use and should have been adopted as zoning.

Decision letter, case 11425
Nov 16, 2023 ShutesburyArticle 3 (Jan. 19, 2023 STM) Zoning regulation
both		 Disapproved

Shutesbury's new solar bylaw, which also banned energy storage as a principal use in all districts, was disapproved in full. The Planning Board notice was defective, and limiting large solar to one per planning sector violated s. 3 and the uniformity rule.

Decision letter, case 10856
