Attorney General reviews of town bylaws (2)

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Nov 25, 2024. Article 26 (Apr. 27, 2024 ATM), general bylaw (licensing).

    Shutesbury's general bylaw required a license from a new seven-member Energy Storage System Licensing Board to operate any non-residential storage system. It was disapproved because it regulated land use and should have been adopted as zoning. Decision letter, case 11425

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Nov 16, 2023. Article 3 (Jan. 19, 2023 STM), zoning regulation.

    Shutesbury's new solar bylaw, which also banned energy storage as a principal use in all districts, was disapproved in full. The Planning Board notice was defective, and limiting large solar to one per planning sector violated s. 3 and the uniformity rule. Decision letter, case 10856
