Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Zoning bylaw, battery storage. Proposed, Dec 30, 2025.

    A Dec. 30, 2025 draft BESS zoning amendment prepared for the March 2026 Annual Town Meeting would allow BESS by special permit in certain business and planned-development districts and prohibit them in roughly 90% of town. The Town Meeting outcome was not found (latest found).

    • Town of Duxbury: proposed BESS zoning bylaw amendment, draft for warrant (Dec. 30, 2025)

  • Attorney General: town denial annulled, Jun 23, 2025. Duxbury Energy Storage, LLC v. Town of Duxbury ZBA, Land Ct. 23 MISC 000643 (LER), court ruling.

    The Land Court held that a standalone battery facility not connected to any solar is a structure that facilitates the collection of solar energy, so it is protected by s. 3, and annulled the town's denial. The AG has cited it in BESS decisions since September 2025 (Rochester, Case #11532). Source
