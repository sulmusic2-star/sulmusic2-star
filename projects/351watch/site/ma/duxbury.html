Attorney General decisions

Decisions on Duxbury’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. What survives review statewide.

DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Jun 23, 2025 DuxburyDuxbury Energy Storage, LLC v. Town of Duxbury ZBA, Land Ct. 23 MISC 000643 (LER) Court ruling
BESS		 Town denial annulled

The Land Court held that a standalone battery facility not connected to any solar is a structure that facilitates the collection of solar energy, so it is protected by s. 3, and annulled the town's denial. The AG has cited it in BESS decisions since September 2025 (Rochester, Case #11532).
