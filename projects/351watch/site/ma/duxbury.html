  • Attorney General: town denial annulled, Jun 23, 2025. Duxbury Energy Storage, LLC v. Town of Duxbury ZBA, Land Ct. 23 MISC 000643 (LER), court ruling.

    The Land Court held that a standalone battery facility not connected to any solar is a structure that facilitates the collection of solar energy, so it is protected by s. 3, and annulled the town's denial. The AG has cited it in BESS decisions since September 2025 (Rochester, Case #11532). Source
