The last two meetings

Thu Oct 22

  1. Ban on data centers in the zoning bylaw

    Planning Board, 6:30 pm, online

    The Planning Board held a public hearing. Zoning changes that would prohibit data centers, by amending Section 3.3 (Use Classifications and Standards) and Article 12 (Definitions) of the Zoning Bylaw. The Planning Board and the Town Council’s Community Resources Committee hear it together. Also on the agenda, it held a hearing on accessory dwelling unit size, half-stories and attics.

    Read the Oct 22 agenda: Amherst Planning Board

    Details

    • Planning Board: Ban on data centers in the zoning bylaw: joint Planning Board hearingHearing

      Zoning changes that would prohibit data centers, by amending Section 3.3 (Use Classifications and Standards) and Article 12 (Definitions) of the Zoning Bylaw. The Planning Board and the Town Council’s Community Resources Committee hear it together.

      Before this: The Town Council referred these changes on its Oct 5 agenda.

    • Accessory dwelling unit size, half-stories and attics: joint zoning hearingHearing

      Zoning changes on how big an accessory dwelling unit can be (Section 5.0112, Gross Floor Area), on half-stories (Section 6.18, Minimum or Maximum Floors), and on the definition of attic (Section 12 Definitions). The Planning Board and the Town Council’s Community Resources Committee hear it together.

      Before this: The Town Council referred these changes on its Sep 28 agenda.
