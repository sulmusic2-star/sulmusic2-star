Follow North Attleborough’s meetings

Coming up

Mon Oct 19

  1. 6:30 pm

    Town Council Town Hall, JoAnn Cathcart Conference Room, 43 South Washington St. Agenda: North Attleborough Town Council, Mon Oct 19

    • Town Council: Offices by right in downtown C-7.5 district, Measure 2027-024: By-Law Sub-CommitteeDiscussion

      Measure 2027-024 would change the zoning bylaw to allow professional and business offices and services (medical, legal, insurance, real estate and other professional offices) by right in the C-7.5 Commercial District (Downtown). It is new business for the Town Council’s By-Law Sub-Committee.

      Before this: Also new business on the Oct 14 Town Council agenda.

    Agenda: North Attleborough Town Council, Mon Oct 19
