The last two meetings

Mon Oct 19

  1. Small roof-mounted wind turbines allowed in every zoning district

    Planning Board, 6 pm, City Council Chambers, City Hall, 93 Washington Street, 2nd floor, or on ZoomAgenda (PDF): Salem Planning Board, Oct 19

    The Planning Board held a public hearing.

    Details

    Speaking: The hearing is for interested parties to comment on the zoning amendments. In person is the primary way to take part; a Zoom webinar may be provided as a secondary way.

    • Planning Board: Small roof-mounted wind turbines allowed in every zoning district: joint public hearingHearing

      Salem would amend its zoning ordinance to allow roof-mounted wind energy systems (up to 10 kilowatts per turbine and up to twenty (20) feet tall) as an accessory use in every zoning district, let them rise 5 feet above a building’s height limit, and add definitions. This amended notice corrects the height from ten to twenty (20) feet.

      Before this: Referred by the City Council at its Sep 10 meeting.

  2. Small rooftop wind turbines allowed in every zone: joint council-Planning Board hearing

    City Council, 6 pm, City Council Chambers, City Hall, 93 Washington Street, 2nd floor, or on ZoomAgenda (PDF): Salem City Council, Oct 19

    The City Council took this up.

    Details

    • City Council: Small rooftop wind turbines allowed in every zone: joint council-Planning Board hearingHearing

      The ordinance would add roof-mounted wind energy systems as an allowed accessory use in every zoning district, define them (rated at 10 kilowatts or less per turbine), and let them rise 5 feet above a building’s height limit.

      Before this: On Sep 10 the City Council’s Committee on Ordinances, Licenses and Legal Affairs recommended referring the Roof-Mounted Wind Energy Systems ordinance to the Planning Board for a joint public hearing.
