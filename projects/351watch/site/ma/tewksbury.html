Bylaws and state rulings (2)

  • Attorney General: approved, Nov 17, 2025. Article 30 (May 5, 2025 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Tewksbury defined BESS and allowed it by special permit in its two industrial districts and Office-Research district. The AG approved it. Decision letter, case 11792

  • Attorney General: approved, Oct 28, 2022. Article 29 (May 2, 2022 ATM; zoning recodification), zoning regulation.

    Tewksbury's recodified zoning includes a large-scale solar section that cuts the minimum lot from 20 to 5 acres and allows such solar by special permit in all districts. The AG approved it, noting the Table of Uses had not been updated to match; unrelated text was disapproved. Decision letter, case 10508
