Bylaw rulings and town actions (2)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Nov 17, 2025). Article 30 (May 5, 2025 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Tewksbury defined battery storage and allowed it by special permit in its two industrial districts and Office-Research district. The Attorney General approved it. Decision letter, case 11792

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Oct 28, 2022). Article 29 (May 2, 2022 Annual Town Meeting; zoning recodification), zoning regulation.

    Tewksbury's recodified zoning includes a large-scale solar section that cuts the minimum lot from 20 to 5 acres and allows such solar by special permit in all districts. The Attorney General approved it, noting the Table of Uses had not been updated to match; unrelated text was disapproved. Decision letter, case 10508
