  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Oct 28, 2022). Article 29 (May 2, 2022 Annual Town Meeting; zoning recodification), zoning regulation.

    Tewksbury's recodified zoning includes a large-scale solar section that cuts the minimum lot from 20 to 5 acres and allows such solar by special permit in all districts.
