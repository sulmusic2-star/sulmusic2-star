What the state has said about Rochester’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Sep 10, 2025). Article 20 (May 13, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Rochester's new battery storage chapter was approved except its noise limits, which the Attorney General found to be an unreasonable regulation. Decision letter, case 11532

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Sep 10, 2025). Article 13 (Jan 27, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Rochester's follow-up amendments to its battery storage chapter, including special permit rules in water-related districts, were approved. Decision letter, case 11715

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Apr 26, 2023). Article 23 (May 23, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Rochester added year-round screening and buffer requirements for large-scale solar. The Attorney General approved them but warned they cannot be used to deny otherwise compliant projects. Decision letter, case 10844
