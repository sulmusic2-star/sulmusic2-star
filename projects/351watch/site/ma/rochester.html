Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Sep 10, 2025. Article 20 (May 13, 2024 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Rochester's new BESS chapter was approved except its noise limits, which the AG found to be an unreasonable regulation. Decision letter, case 11532

  • Attorney General: approved, Sep 10, 2025. Article 13 (Jan. 27, 2025 STM), zoning regulation.

    Rochester's follow-up amendments to its BESS chapter, including special permit rules in water-related districts, were approved. Decision letter, case 11715

  • Attorney General: approved, Apr 26, 2023. Article 23 (May 23, 2022 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Rochester added year-round screening and buffer requirements for large-scale solar. The AG approved them but warned they cannot be used to deny otherwise compliant projects. Decision letter, case 10844
