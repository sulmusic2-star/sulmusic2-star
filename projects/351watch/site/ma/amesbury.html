      MP Properties IV, LLC wants a Comprehensive Permit (the state’s Chapter 40B permit that lets an affordable-housing project override local zoning) to build a 95-unit multifamily cottage rental development of 1, 2 and 3 bedroom units on a 9.4 acre property at 27 Kimball Road, in the R-40 zoning district. 25% of the units would be deed-restricted for low or moderate income housing.
