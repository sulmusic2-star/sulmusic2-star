PetershamWorcester County Zoning bylaw
Battery storage and solar		 Approved by AG Aug 13, 2026AG decision

Articles 3 to 8 of the Dec. 8, 2025 Special Town Meeting revised the town's solar and BESS zoning. The Attorney General approved them on Aug. 13, 2026 (Case #12175), except a rule barring BESS from being visible from public roads and a requirement that owners pay for emergency-responder equipment and training.

Jul 14, 2026 PetershamSunpin Energy Services, LLC v. ZBA of Petersham, 497 Mass. 794 (SJC-13860) Court ruling
solar		 Town denial vacated

The SJC held that a town must provide reasonable opportunities for solar and may deny a special permit for a protected solar project only when necessary to protect public health, safety or welfare. The AG has cited it in every solar and BESS decision since August 2026.
