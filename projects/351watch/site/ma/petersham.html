    Petersham's revised solar and battery storage zoning was approved except a rule that battery storage not be visible from public roads and a requirement that owners pay for emergency-responder equipment and training. Decision letter, case 12175

  • Court ruling: town denial vacated (decided Jul 14, 2026). Sunpin Energy Services, LLC v. Zoning Board of Appeals of Petersham, 497 Mass. 794 (Supreme Judicial Court-13860), court ruling.

    The Supreme Judicial Court held that a town must provide reasonable opportunities for solar and may deny a special permit for a protected solar project only when necessary to protect public health, safety or welfare. The Attorney General has cited it in every solar and battery storage decision since August 2026. Source
