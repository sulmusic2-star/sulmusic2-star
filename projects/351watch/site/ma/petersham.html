What the state has said about Petersham’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Aug 13, 2026). Articles 3-8 (Dec 8, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Petersham's revised solar and battery storage zoning was approved except a rule that battery storage not be visible from public roads and a requirement that owners pay for emergency-responder equipment and training. Decision letter, case 12175

  • Court ruling: ruled for the developer; sent back to the board (decided Jul 14, 2026). Sunpin Energy Services, LLC v. Zoning Board of Appeals of Petersham, 497 Mass. 794 (Supreme Judicial Court-13860), court ruling.

    The Supreme Judicial Court held that a town must give solar reasonable opportunities and may deny a special permit for a protected solar project only when necessary to protect public health, safety or welfare. It vacated the Land Court judgment that had upheld Petersham’s denial and sent the matter back to the zoning board to decide under that test. The Appeals Court (105 Mass. App. Ct. 641, July 9, 2025) had ordered the permit issued. Source
