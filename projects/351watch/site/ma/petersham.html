  • Court ruling: town denial vacated (decided Jul 14, 2026). Sunpin Energy Services, LLC v. Zoning Board of Appeals of Petersham, 497 Mass. 794 (Supreme Judicial Court-13860), court ruling.

    The Supreme Judicial Court held that a town must provide reasonable opportunities for solar and may deny a special permit for a protected solar project only when necessary to protect public health, safety or welfare.
