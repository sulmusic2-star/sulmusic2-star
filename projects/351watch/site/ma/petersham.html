  • Attorney General: approved in part, Aug 13, 2026. Articles 3-8 (Dec. 8, 2025 STM), zoning regulation.

    Petersham's revised solar and BESS zoning was approved except a rule that BESS not be visible from public roads and a requirement that owners pay for emergency-responder equipment and training. Decision letter, case 12175

  • Attorney General: town denial vacated, Jul 14, 2026. Sunpin Energy Services, LLC v. ZBA of Petersham, 497 Mass. 794 (SJC-13860), court ruling.

    The SJC held that a town must provide reasonable opportunities for solar and may deny a special permit for a protected solar project only when necessary to protect public health, safety or welfare. The AG has cited it in every solar and BESS decision since August 2026. Source
