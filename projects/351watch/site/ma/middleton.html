    • Planning Board: New parking, setback and development standards in Middleton zoning: hearingHearing

      Zoning bylaw changes: new parking regulations, changes to the performance standards for new development, and revised setback regulations; this notice gives no detail.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s fuller Oct 14 notice lists: right-sized parking counts, shared parking, electric vehicle ready spaces and pedestrian access in parking lots; accessory structures get the same setbacks as the primary structure and front setbacks can no longer be measured from the street centerline in R-1a, R-1b and RA; dark sky compliant light fixtures; and a continued hearing the same night on administrative edits and the special permit/site plan process.
