Coming up

  1. Planning Board Thu Oct 22, 6:30 pm Lakeville Police Station

    • New rules for battery storage plants: Planning Board hearing.

      A new zoning section (§270-12) would regulate battery energy storage systems: permit rules by tier (Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3), a noise study and noise limits, commissioning and decommissioning plans, a decommissioning fund, proof of insurance, safety, what happens on ownership change or abandonment, site plan applications and special permit criteria.

      Before this: A BESS draft was on the Planning Board's Sep 10 and Sep 24 agendas; the Select Board read and commented on the BESS bylaw Sep 15; the Planning Board met with Town Counsel on edits Oct 8.

      Battery storageHearing

    • Two new zoning bylaws, battery storage and open-space subdivisions: hearing.

      One hearing on two new zoning sections: §270-12, rules for battery energy storage systems (see the battery storage brief), and §270-13, Open Space and Residential Development (OSRD), which would let subdivisions use smaller lots in return for permanently protected open space, with a special permit, a five-step design process, and subsidized and senior housing parts.

      Before this: The Select Board voted Oct 6 on sending the OSRD bylaw to the Planning Board; the BESS draft was on Planning Board agendas Sep 10, Sep 24 and Oct 8 and before the Select Board Sep 15.

    Public hearing: anyone can speak; written comments go to the town clerk before the meeting.Agenda: Lakeville Planning Board, Thu Oct 22
