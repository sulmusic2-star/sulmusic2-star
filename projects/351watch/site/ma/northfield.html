      The board continues its hearing and votes on the draft moratorium (temporary ban) on data storage centers, and on changes to the accessory dwelling unit section 200-9.1. A zoning change like this is adopted by Town Meeting; the Planning Board’s vote is its recommendation, and no Town Meeting date is listed.
