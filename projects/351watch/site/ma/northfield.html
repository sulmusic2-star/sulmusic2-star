  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Disapproved by AG, Oct 30, 2024.

    The May 6, 2024 Annual Town Meeting adopted a moratorium on principal-use BESS (Article 26) and one on large-scale ground-mounted solar including agrivoltaics (Article 27), both running to August 2025. The Attorney General disapproved both moratoria on Oct. 30, 2024 (Case #11346) but approved a new BESS definition.
