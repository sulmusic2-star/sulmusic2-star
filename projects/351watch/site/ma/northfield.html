What the state has said about Northfield’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Oct 30, 2024). Articles 26 and 27 (May 6, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Northfield adopted moratoriums on principal-use battery storage and on large ground-mounted solar, including agrivoltaics, through August 2025. The Attorney General disapproved both moratoriums (it approved a new battery storage definition in Art. 26). Decision letter, case 11346
