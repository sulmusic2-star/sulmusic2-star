Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Disapproved by AG, Oct 30, 2024.

    The May 6, 2024 Annual Town Meeting adopted a moratorium on principal-use BESS (Article 26) and one on large-scale ground-mounted solar including agrivoltaics (Article 27), both running to August 2025. The Attorney General disapproved both moratoria on Oct. 30, 2024 (Case #11346) but approved a new BESS definition.

    • Attorney General decision, Northfield Case #11346 (Oct. 30, 2024)
    • Same Northfield AG decision, copy posted by Town of Worthington

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Oct 30, 2024. Articles 26 and 27 (May 6, 2024 ATM), moratorium.

    Northfield adopted moratoria on principal-use BESS and on large ground-mounted solar, including agrivoltaics, through August 2025. The AG disapproved both moratoria (it approved a new BESS definition in Art. 26). Decision letter, case 11346
