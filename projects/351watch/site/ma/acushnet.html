Bylaw rulings and town actions (2)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Feb 28, 2025). Article 28 (June 17, 2024 Annual Town Meeting), general bylaw.

    Acushnet's new general Stormwater Management bylaw specifically requires large-scale ground-mounted solar to obtain a stormwater permit. The Attorney General approved it, cautioning the town to apply the solar provisions consistently with § 3. Unlike Wendell's and Shutesbury's licensing bylaws, it regulates a stormwater impact rather than land use. Decision letter, case 11638

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Oct 20, 2023). Article 12 (Oct. 17, 2022 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Acushnet replaced its large-scale ground-mounted solar article, and the Attorney General approved it. The letter notes that a 180-day solar moratorium voted in spring 2022 was never submitted for Attorney General review and so never took lawful effect. Decision letter, case 10853
