Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Attorney General: approved, Feb 28, 2025. Article 28 (June 17, 2024 ATM), general bylaw.

    Acushnet's new general Stormwater Management bylaw specifically requires large-scale ground-mounted solar to obtain a stormwater permit. The AG approved it, cautioning the town to apply the solar provisions consistently with s. 3. Unlike Wendell's and Shutesbury's licensing bylaws, it regulates a stormwater impact rather than land use. Decision letter, case 11638

  • Attorney General: approved, Oct 20, 2023. Article 12 (Oct. 17, 2022 STM), zoning regulation.

    Acushnet replaced its large-scale ground-mounted solar article, and the AG approved it. The letter notes that a 180-day solar moratorium voted in spring 2022 was never submitted for AG review and so never took lawful effect. Decision letter, case 10853
