  1. Comprehensive permit (affordable housing) at 0 & 194 Lowland St: Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) deliberations

    Zoning Board of Appeals, 6 pm, online

    The Zoning Board of Appeals took this up.

    Read the Oct 21 agenda: Holliston Zoning Board of Appeals

    Details

    • Zoning Board of Appeals: Comprehensive permit (affordable housing) at 0 & 194 Lowland St: ZBA deliberationsDiscussion

      MP Properties IV, LLC wants a comprehensive permit (the state permit for developments with affordable housing that can override local zoning) for land owned by DCAB, LLC at 0 & 194 Lowland Street. The Zoning Board of Appeals deliberates on it, talking the application through among its members.

      Before this: The public hearing was continued on the Sep 24 and Oct 15 Zoning Board of Appeals agendas; revised plans and the updated waiver list were on the Sep 15 Conservation Commission agenda.

Mon Oct 19

  1. Buy the fire station’s solar array, Article 13

    Town Meeting, 7 pm, Holliston High School Auditorium, 370 Hollis Street

    Town Meeting voted on this. Also on the warrant, it voted on Groundwater Protection District zoning change, Article 17.

    Read the Town Meeting warrant: Holliston Town Meeting

    Details

    • Town Meeting: Buy the fire station’s solar array, Article 13: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 13 asks Town Meeting to move $421,500 from Article 22 of the May 2024 Town Meeting to buy the solar panel array on the Fire Department headquarters at 59 Central Street, now owned by a third party, and repair the roof. The Finance Committee unanimously recommends the reallocation for the roof repair and solar array purchase.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 28 Select Board agenda, as the Central Fire Solar Array Purchase.

    • Groundwater Protection District zoning change, Article 17: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 17 asks Town Meeting to amend Section V-L of the Zoning By-Laws, the Groundwater Protection District rules. The Select Board, the Planning Board and Envisioning Future Holliston sponsor it.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s public hearing was on its Oct 8 agenda and continued Oct 14; also on the Sep 10 Planning Board and Sep 14 Select Board agendas.
