  1. 40 homes, 10 affordable, at 24 Bancroft Street (Woodland Ridge 40B)

    Zoning Board of Appeals, 7 pm

    The Zoning Board of Appeals’ hearing continues. Speak for or against in writing before the hearing, or in person at the hearing (yourself or through an agent or attorney). In person only.

    Read the Nov 18 agenda: Pepperell Zoning Board of Appeals

    Details

    • Zoning Board of Appeals: 40 homes, 10 affordable, at 24 Bancroft Street (Woodland Ridge 40B): hearing continuesHearing

      Woodland Ridge, LLC (through attorney Melissa Robbins of Farrell, & Robbins, P.C.) wants a 40B comprehensive permit for 40 single-family homes for sale on 45.5 acres between Bancroft Street and Orion Road: 32 two-bedroom and 8 three-bedroom homes, with 10 of them (25%) affordable to households at or below 80% of area median income.

      Four zoning articles for the Saturday, November 7 Town Meeting: new zoning district definitions (Section 2150), a new definitions section (Section 10000), a new Table of Principal Uses (Appendix A), and much smaller minimums in the Urban Residential district (lot area 40,000 square feet to 10,000 square feet, frontage 150 feet to 50 feet, front, side and rear setbacks to 10, 5 and 10 feet).
