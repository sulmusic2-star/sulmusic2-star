  • Attorney General: solar protection enforced, Jun 2, 2022. Tracer Lane II Realty, LLC v. City of Waltham, 489 Mass. 775 (SJC-13195), court ruling.

    The SJC held that Waltham's ban on large-scale solar in all but 1-2% of the city was impermissible, and that s. 3 claims require balancing the town's interest against the burden on solar. Every AG solar or BESS decision since mid-2022 relies on it. Source
