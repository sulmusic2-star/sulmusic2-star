Bylaw rulings and town actions (1)

  • Court ruling: solar protection enforced (decided Jun 2, 2022). Tracer Lane II Realty, LLC v. City of Waltham, 489 Mass. 775 (Supreme Judicial Court-13195), court ruling.

    The Supreme Judicial Court held that Waltham's ban on large-scale solar in all but 1-2% of the city was impermissible, and that § 3 claims require balancing the town's interest against the burden on solar. Every Attorney General solar or battery storage decision since mid-2022 relies on it. Source
