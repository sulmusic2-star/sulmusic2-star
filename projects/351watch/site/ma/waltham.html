DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Jun 2, 2022 Waltham (city)Tracer Lane II Realty, LLC v. City of Waltham, 489 Mass. 775 (SJC-13195) Court ruling
solar		 Solar protection enforced

The SJC held that Waltham's ban on large-scale solar in all but 1-2% of the city was impermissible, and that s. 3 claims require balancing the town's interest against the burden on solar. Every AG solar or BESS decision since mid-2022 relies on it.
