Bylaw rulings and town actions (1)

  • Court ruling: battery storage protected as solar (decided May 9, 2023). NextSun Energy LLC v. Fernandes, No. 19 MISC 000230 (RBF) (Mass. Land Ct. May 9, 2023), court ruling.

    The Land Court held that a battery energy storage system built with a solar project in Norton is a structure that facilitates the collection of solar energy, so it has the § 3 solar protections (decision May 9, 2023; amended judgment entered June 23, 2023). The Attorney General has cited it in solar and battery-storage decisions since September 2023. Source
