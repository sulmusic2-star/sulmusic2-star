Mon Oct 26

  1. Ban data centers in every zone of South Hadley

    Planning Board, 6:30 pm, Multipurpose room, South Hadley Senior Center, 45 Dayton Street, or on Zoom

    The Planning Board held a public hearing. The change would add a definition of “data center” to the zoning bylaw (Section 255-10) and list “Data Center” in the use table as an industrial use that is prohibited in all zoning districts. Also on the agenda, it held a hearing on a data-center bylaw and talked about a zoning change.
