Mon Oct 26

  1. 6:30 pm

    Planning Board Multipurpose room, South Hadley Senior Center, 45 Dayton Street, or on Zoom Agenda: South Hadley Planning Board, Mon Oct 26

    Speaking: Anyone interested or wishing to be heard should appear in person or join online at the time stated.

    • Planning Board: Ban data centers in every zone of South Hadley: Planning Board hearingHearing

      The change would add a definition of “data center” to the zoning bylaw (Section 255-10) and list “Data Center” in the use table as an industrial use that is prohibited in all zoning districts.

      Before this: The Selectboard discussed a data center moratorium Sep 22 (with a “Data Center Prohibition” document in its packet) and held a listening session on data centers Oct 6.

    Agenda: South Hadley Planning Board, Mon Oct 26
