3 items at 1 meeting coming up, mostly zoning changes and data centers. Next: the Town Meeting, Tue Oct 27.

Coming up

Tue Oct 27

  1. No time listed

    Town Meeting Warrant: Uxbridge Town Meeting, Tue Oct 27

    • Town Meeting: Data-center moratorium, Article 16: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 16 would set a temporary moratorium on applying for and installing data centers. It would add a new zoning section, Section 400-24 “Moratorium on Data Centers”, under Article VI, and a new definition in Article X, Definitions.

      Before this: On the Sep 23 Planning Board agenda for a public hearing, and on the Oct 6 Select Board agenda.

    • Nonconforming uses, retreat lots, Board of Appeals rules, Article 17: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 17 would amend three zoning bylaw sections: Section 400-12 on nonconforming uses and structures (older uses and buildings that don’t meet today’s rules), Section 400-32 on retreat lots, and Section 400-49 on the Board of Appeals.

      Before this: On the Sep 23 Planning Board agenda for a public hearing, and on the Oct 6 Select Board agenda.

    • Clarify ban on marijuana social-consumption businesses, Article 18: Town Meeting voteVote

      Article 18 would amend the zoning bylaw’s marijuana section, §400-23, to clarify the town’s existing prohibition on marijuana social consumption establishments (businesses where customers use marijuana on site) and bring the zoning bylaws in line with state law.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 6 Select Board agenda and the Oct 21 Planning Board agenda (public hearing).

    Warrant: Uxbridge Town Meeting, Tue Oct 27
