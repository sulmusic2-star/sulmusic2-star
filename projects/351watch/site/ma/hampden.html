HampdenHampden County Moratorium
Battery storage		 Disapproved by AG Mar 11, 2025AG decision

Town Meeting adopted a six-month moratorium on standalone battery storage permits (Article 18, Nov. 2024 Special Town Meeting). The Attorney General disapproved it on Mar. 11, 2025; the town later adopted battery-storage zoning that the AG approved on Feb. 9, 2026.
