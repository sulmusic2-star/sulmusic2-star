Bylaw rulings and town actions (2)

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Dec 4, 2023). Article 28 (May 13, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Pelham's rewritten solar bylaw was approved except carbon-sequestration mitigation, a cap on installations in the overlay district, a pesticide ban, a rule barring solar-sited storage from handling off-site energy, and a requirement that storage owners pay for emergency-responder training. Decision letter, case 11057 (solar letter)

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Dec 4, 2023). Articles 29 and 30 (May 13, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning prohibition.

    The Attorney General approved Pelham's new energy storage system definition (Art. 29) but disapproved Art. 30, which prohibited ESS as a principal use in every district. Decision letter, case 11057 (battery storage systems letter)
