  1. Select Board Tue Oct 20, 7 pm Dartmouth High School

    • Protect 29 acres at 970 Tucker Road as an alternative to a 40B: vote (Article 1).

      Article 1 spends $1,000,000 of Community Preservation money on a conservation restriction over about 29 acres at 970 Tucker Road (the Paskamansett Valley Conservation Project). A board recommendation in the packet says this gives "a reasonable alternative" to a proposed 40B housing development called the Residences at Hawthorn.

      Before this: The Conservation Commission discussed the Paskamansett Valley conservation restriction Sep 1, and the Select Board attended a CPC meeting on the Paskamansett Valley Project Sep 8.

      Affordable housing970 Tucker Road$1,000,000; approximate 29-acre portionCommunity Preservation CommitteeVote

    • Repeal of the sidewalk snow-removal bylaw (Article 15): a general bylaw, not zoning.

      Article 15 would delete Section 315-6 of the general bylaws, which requires snow to be removed from sidewalks. It is a general bylaw change, not a zoning change.

    • A new town job in the personnel bylaw (Article 14): not a zoning item.

      Article 14 amends the personnel bylaw to add a new position. It is not a zoning change. The article text is cut off in our copy.

      Zoning changesBoard of Parks & Recreation

    • Dartmouth Fall Town Meeting warrant index: no zoning articles listed.

      This is the warrant's index. The articles it names here (fines for health and public works rules, a new personnel position, sidewalk snow removal, animal fines) are general bylaw changes, not zoning.
