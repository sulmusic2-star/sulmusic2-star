Coming up

Show

Tue Oct 27

  1. 7 pm

    Planning Board Select Board Room, Winchester Town Hall, 71 Mt. Vernon Street Agenda: Winchester Planning Board, Tue Oct 27

    Speaking: All interested persons will be given an opportunity to be heard.

    • Planning Board: Rezone 114 Church Street from institutional to apartment zoning: hearing (Article 37)Hearing

      A citizen petition to change 114 Church Street from the ConservancyInstitutional District (SCI) to Apartment House Residential District B (RB-20). A similar petition, Article 38, would move 18 Raymond Place from SCI to the General Resident District (RB-6.5).

    • Minimum lot width in the RDA zone cut from 120 to 100 feet: hearing (Article 4)Hearing

      Article 4 would let a conforming house on a nonconforming lot be rebuilt, extended or changed when the problem is lot width, and would cut the minimum lot width in the RDA District from 120 feet to 100 feet. Article 5 (design review) and Article 6 (permit paperwork), also by the Planning Board, are on the same hearing.

      Before this: The Select Board heard a presentation of the Planning Board’s warrant articles Oct 5.

    • Rezone part of The Ledges Condominium, 7 Wainwright Road: hearing (Article 40)Hearing

      A citizen petition to move part of The Ledges Condominium at 7 Wainwright Road from the Planned Residential District (PRD) to Residential District A (RDA-20). A related Article 39 would release part of the condo’s unimproved common open space, which a 1985 Zoning Board of Appeals decision says needs a Town Meeting majority before it can be developed. Article 38 (18 Raymond Place, SCI to RB-6.5) is also on this hearing.

    Agenda: Winchester Planning Board, Tue Oct 27
