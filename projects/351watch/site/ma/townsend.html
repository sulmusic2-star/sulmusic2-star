Mon Oct 19

  1. 6:30 pm

    Planning Board Selectboard Chambers in Townsend Town Hall, 272 Main Street, Townsend, or on Zoom

    • Planning Board: Battery storage model bylaw and clean energy moratorium: Planning Board reviewDiscussion

      The board reviews the state Department of Energy Resources (DOER) model zoning bylaw for allowing battery energy storage systems, written for the new consolidated local permitting process for small clean energy infrastructure facilities. It also checks the status of its request for legal services on a temporary moratorium on permitting small clean energy infrastructure.

      Before this: The DOER consolidated clean energy permitting was on the Sep 28 and Oct 5 Planning Board agendas.

    • Groundwater Protection District map reference, zoning 145-54(B): Water Department requestDiscussion

      The Water Department asks to change the zoning bylaw’s Groundwater Protection District section (§ 145-54(B)) so it refers to the Townsend Zoning Map dated October 2014 instead of the 1998 Groundwater Protection Overlay Map, as a MassDEP sanitary survey recommended for Zone II #473. Ryan Lapierre, the chief water treatment plant operator, brings suggested wording.

      First time on an agenda

    • Mentions battery storage and data centers: “MRPC (Katherine Waugh) survey for Planning Board input on local zoning and permitting approaches for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), data centers, and tiny homes.”

      First time on an agenda
