Coming up

  1. Town Meeting Tue Oct 20, 7 pm Ipswich Middle School

    • Mixed-use buildings: shops must fill 25% of the ground floor, up front: vote (Article 5)

      Article 5 changes the definition of "Use, Residential-Mixed" so the non-residential use must take up at least 25% of the building's ground floor and sit in the front of the building, facing the sidewalk or street.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Zoning changesno less than twenty-five percent (25%) of the building’s ground floorPlanning Board articleVote

    • ADU rules (Article 6) and business-district size rules (Article 7): Town Meeting votes

      Article 6 replaces Ipswich's accessory apartment, converted-accessory-building and detached unit rules with one "Protected Use Accessory Dwelling Unit" section to match state law (one such unit per lot, no bigger than half the main home's floor area or 900 square feet). Article 7 changes dimensional and density rules for business districts; its text is not in our copy.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Zoning changesnot larger in Gross Floor Area than ½ the Gross Floor Area of the Principal Dwelling or 900 square feet, whichever is smallerPlanning Board & Building Department articleVote

    • New definition of "structure" in the zoning bylaw: Town Meeting vote (Article 4)

      Article 4 rewrites the zoning bylaw's definition of "structure" to mean anything built or placed on the ground for support or shelter and meant to be permanent (or anchored to something permanent), so permit reviews are clearer and easier to defend.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

      Zoning changesPlanning Board articleVote
