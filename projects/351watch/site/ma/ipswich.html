  1. 7 pm

    Town Meeting IPSWICH MIDDLE SCHOOL/HIGH SCHOOL, 134 High Street

    • Town Meeting: Mixed-use buildings: shops must fill 25% of the ground floor, up front: vote (Article 5)Vote

      Article 5 changes the definition of “Use, Residential-Mixed” so the non-residential use must take up at least 25% of the building’s ground floor and sit in the front of the building, facing the sidewalk or street.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

    • ADU rules (Article 6) and business-district size rules (Article 7): Town Meeting votesVote

      Article 6 replaces Ipswich’s accessory apartment, converted-accessory-building and detached unit rules with one “Protected Use Accessory Dwelling Unit” section to match state law (one such unit per lot, no bigger than half the main home’s floor area or 900 square feet). Article 7 changes dimensional and density rules for business districts; its text is in the full warrant.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.

    • New definition of “structure” in the zoning bylaw: Town Meeting vote (Article 4)Vote

      Article 4 rewrites the zoning bylaw’s definition of “structure” to mean anything built or placed on the ground for support or shelter and meant to be permanent (or anchored to something permanent), so permit reviews are clearer and easier to defend.

      Before this: The Select Board held a public hearing on the whole Oct 20 Special Town Meeting warrant and voted its recommendations Sep 14.
