  1. Oct 21 hearing on new rules for Special Residential Developments (Article 7)

    Planning Board, 6 pm, online

    The Planning Board took this up. Also on the agenda, it took up Special Residential Development rules, Section 6.9: Planning Board work session.

    Read the Oct 21 agenda: Lexington Planning Board

    Details

    No public hearing is listed. The agenda doesn’t say whether you can comment; the town clerk would know.

    • Planning Board: Cancelled: Oct 21 hearing on new rules for Special Residential Developments (Article 7)

      The Planning Board cancelled its Oct 21 remote hearing on Article 7, which would change Section 6.9 of the zoning bylaw on Special Residential Developments (site coverage limits, a landscaped buffer around the edge, protection of historic buildings, changed gross floor area limits, wider open space standards).

      Before this: The Planning Board reviewed amendments to Special Residential Developments, Section 6.9, in a work session Oct 7.

    • Special Residential Development rules, Section 6.9: Planning Board work sessionDiscussion

      Planning Board members talk through proposed changes to the zoning rules for Special Residential Developments (Section 6.9), aiming to submit them to Annual Town Meeting. Other possible future changes named: variances under the 2026 changes to the Zoning Act, accessory apartments, clean energy siting and permitting, and bicycle parking.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 7 Planning Board agenda. A public hearing on the Section 6.9 changes (Article 7) set for Oct 21 was cancelled at the board’s request.
