Bylaw rulings and town actions (1)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Jan 4, 2023). Article 18 (June 27, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), zoning prohibition.

    Oakham capped battery storage at half the capacity of an associated large solar installation and prohibited battery storage not attached to a permitted large-scale solar site. The Attorney General approved it; within weeks (Wendell, Mar 2023) the office began disapproving similar standalone-battery storage bans. Decision letter, case 10697 (the letter’s RE line reads Case # 10690)
