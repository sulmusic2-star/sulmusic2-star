    • Town Meeting: Zoning updates to match new state law on variances, grandfathering: vote (Article 11)Vote

      Article 11 rewrites parts of the zoning bylaw to match recent changes to the state Zoning Act: variances would turn on “practical difficulty” instead of “substantial hardship”, some additions to older nonconforming homes would be allowed without a special permit, a nonconforming use would be lost after four years of abandonment instead of two, and a permit’s protection would run twenty-four months instead of six.

      Before this: The Planning Board held a public hearing Sep 17 on revising the zoning bylaw for consistency with the state budget bill’s changes on entitlements, non-conformities and variances.

What the state has said about Hubbardston’s bylaws

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Dec 23, 2025). Article 26 (June 2, 2025 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Hubbardston's second battery storage bylaw, adopted after its near-total ban was struck in 2023, was approved. Decision letter, case 11973

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Dec 19, 2023). Article 23 (June 7, 2022 Annual Town Meeting), zoning prohibition.

    Hubbardston's Grid-Scale battery storage article imposed what the Attorney General called an almost complete ban on grid-scale battery storage, with no record evidence of need. The Attorney General disapproved it in its entirety. Decision letter, case 10663

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Dec 19, 2023). Articles 23 and 24 (June 6, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    The Attorney General approved Hubbardston's use-table clean-ups except references to the disapproved grid-scale battery storage article and a pesticide/herbicide ban at solar sites. Decision letter, case 11103
