Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Attorney General: approved, Dec 23, 2025. Article 26 (June 2, 2025 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Hubbardston's second BESS bylaw, adopted after its near-total ban was struck in 2023, was approved. Decision letter, case 11973

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Dec 19, 2023. Article 23 (June 7, 2022 ATM), zoning prohibition.

    Hubbardston's Grid-Scale BESS article imposed what the AG called an almost complete ban on grid-scale battery storage, with no record evidence of need. The AG disapproved it in its entirety. Decision letter, case 10663

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Dec 19, 2023. Articles 23 and 24 (June 6, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    The AG approved Hubbardston's use-table clean-ups except references to the disapproved grid-scale BESS article and a pesticide/herbicide ban at solar sites. Decision letter, case 11103
