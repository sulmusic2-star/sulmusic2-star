  2. Town Meeting Mon Oct 19, 7:30 pm Milton High School Auditorium at 25 Gile Road

    Town Meeting: the town’s voters decide. Ask the town clerk how others may speak.

    • MBTA zone: at least 15% affordable homes in bigger projects, made clear: vote (Article 5)Vote

      Article 5 deletes words from the MBTA Communities Multi-family Overlay District rules (Chapter 275, Section 3.25, subparagraph P(3)). The Warrant Committee says the change makes clear that the larger developments must include at least 15% affordable housing, and it supports the article.

      Before this: Listed as Article 5 (affordability requirements) on the Select Board’s Sep 15, Sep 22, Oct 6 and Oct 13 agendas during warrant review.
