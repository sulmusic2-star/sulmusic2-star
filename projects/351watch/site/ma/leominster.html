Battery storage		 Adopted Sep 28, 2026City Council vote

The City Council voted 8 to 0 to pause new battery storage site development, alongside a data center moratorium, effective until July 31, 2027. Two battery projects already underway, on Lancaster Street and Willard Street, are not paused.
