Tracked local actions

TownActionStatusDateWhat happened
HadleyHampshire County Zoning bylaw
Battery storage		 Approved by AG Nov 12, 2024AG decision

Article 23 of the May 2, 2024 Annual Town Meeting added energy storage definitions and a new Large Energy Storage Systems section to the zoning bylaw. The Attorney General approved it on Nov. 12, 2024 (Case #11355), except a ban on earth removal to facilitate a storage installation, which was disapproved.

  • Attorney General decision, Hadley Case #11355 (Nov. 12, 2024), copy posted by Town of Worthington

Aug 14, 2025 HadleyArticle 12 (May 1, 2025 ATM) Zoning regulation
solar		 Approved

After the AG struck its 2024 ban on earth removal for energy storage, Hadley limited earth removal to what is incidental and necessary to build a solar system. The AG approved this narrower rule.

Decision letter, case 11787

BESS		 Approved in part

Hadley's energy storage definitions and Large Energy Storage Systems section were approved except a ban on earth removal to facilitate an ESS installation.
