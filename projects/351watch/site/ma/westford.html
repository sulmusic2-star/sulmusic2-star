    • New zoning rules for small clean-energy sites like battery storage: hearing continues

      The Planning Board proposes a new zoning section (6.6) with a permit process and standards for building, maintaining and removing small clean-energy facilities such as battery storage, to fit the 2024 state clean energy law. It would also delete the old large-scale solar review sections.
