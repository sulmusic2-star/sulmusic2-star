    • Select Board reviews Town Meeting article on new clean-energy facility zoning (8:10 pm)Discussion

      For its Town Meeting recommendation, the Select Board reviews a Planning Board zoning article creating Section 6.6, ‘Small Clean Energy Infrastructure Facilities (SCEIF)’, and changing the general accessory uses, signs, large-scale solar review and definitions sections; the agenda doesn’t say what the new section requires.

Mon Oct 5

  1. 7 pm

    Planning Board Town Hall, Meeting Room 201, 55 Main Street Agenda: Westford Planning Board, Mon Oct 5

    Speaking: Public hearing. If you cannot attend, email written comments to planningboardstaff@westfordma.gov by 4:00 pm on the meeting day.

    • Planning Board: New zoning rules for small clean-energy sites like battery storage: hearing continuesHearing

      The Planning Board proposes a new zoning section (6.6) with a permit process and standards for building, maintaining and removing small clean-energy facilities such as battery storage, to fit the 2024 state clean energy law. It would also delete the old large-scale solar review sections.

      Before this: Sep 14 Planning Board hearing; Sep 9 Conservation Commission discussion of the amendment; Sep 22 and Oct 13 Select Board review of the warrant article (Planning Board Article 1 of the Special Town Meeting warrant).

    Agenda: Westford Planning Board, Mon Oct 5
