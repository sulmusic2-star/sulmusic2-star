    Sturbridge adopted a moratorium on standalone battery storage of 250 kWh or more through Dec. 1, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it for lacking justification, being unlimited in scope, and conflicting with the requirement to accept consolidated permit applications from Oct. 1, 2026.
