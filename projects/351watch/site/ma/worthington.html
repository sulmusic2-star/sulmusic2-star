What the state has said about Worthington’s bylaws

  • Special act (moratorium), battery storage and solar. Sent to study, Jun 8, 2026.

    H.5294 (Rep. Lindsay Sabadosa, Sen. Paul Mark), approved by Worthington voters as Article 10 on Feb 24, 2026, sought a one-year moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and battery storage. The Municipalities committee sent it to a study order (H.5480) on June 8, 2026, a committee action rather than a floor vote that usually ends a bill for the session.

    • malegislature.gov: H.5294 bill history
    • malegislature.gov: H.5480 study order
    • Town of Worthington: Special Town Meeting Feb. 24, 2026 (warrant and transcript links)

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Jun 8, 2026). Article 6 (Feb 24, 2026 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Worthington adopted a town-wide moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and battery storage over 600 kWh through June 30, 2027. The Attorney General disapproved it for lacking a health, safety or welfare justification. Decision letter, case 12194 (solar letter)

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Jun 8, 2026). Articles 7 and 8 (Feb 24, 2026 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Worthington's limited-area moratoriums on large solar (Art. 7) and on large energy storage (Art. 8) were disapproved because they were not limited in scope or time; the Attorney General also struck Art. 8's ban on storage over 5 MWh as unreasonable. Decision letter, case 12194 (battery storage systems letter)
