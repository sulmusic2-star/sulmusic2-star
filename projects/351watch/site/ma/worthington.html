Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Disapproved by AG, Jun 8, 2026.

    The Feb. 24, 2026 Special Town Meeting passed three zoning moratoria (Articles 6 to 8): a town-wide pause on large ground-mounted solar and storage over 600 kWh through June 30, 2027, plus limited-area moratoria on solar and on energy storage. The Attorney General disapproved all three in a June 8, 2026 letter.

    • Daily Hampshire Gazette: AG rejects solar moratoriums in Worthington (June 22, 2026)
    • Town of Worthington: Special Town Meeting Feb. 24, 2026 (warrant and transcript links)
    • Worthington Feb. 24, 2026 STM warrant

  • Special act (moratorium), battery storage and solar. Sent to study, Jun 8, 2026.

    H.5294 (Rep. Lindsay Sabadosa, Sen. Paul Mark), approved by Worthington voters as Article 10 on Feb. 24, 2026, sought a one-year moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and battery storage. The Municipalities committee sent it to a study order (H.5480) on June 8, 2026, a committee action rather than a floor vote that usually ends a bill for the session.

    • malegislature.gov: H.5294 bill history
    • malegislature.gov: H.5480 study order
    • Town of Worthington: Special Town Meeting Feb. 24, 2026 (warrant and transcript links)

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Jun 8, 2026. Article 6 (Feb. 24, 2026 STM), moratorium.

    Worthington adopted a town-wide moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and BESS over 600 kWh through June 30, 2027. The AG disapproved it for lacking a health, safety or welfare justification. Decision letter, case 12194

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Jun 8, 2026. Articles 7 and 8 (Feb. 24, 2026 STM), moratorium.

    Worthington's limited-area moratoria on large solar (Art. 7) and on large energy storage (Art. 8) were disapproved because they were not limited in scope or time; the AG also struck Art. 8's ban on storage over 5 MWh as unreasonable. Decision letter, case 12194
