WorthingtonHampshire County Moratorium
Battery storage and solar		 Disapproved by AG Jun 8, 2026AG decision

The Feb. 24, 2026 Special Town Meeting passed three zoning moratoria (Articles 6 to 8): a town-wide pause on large ground-mounted solar and storage over 600 kWh through June 30, 2027, plus limited-area moratoria on solar and on energy storage. The Attorney General disapproved all three in a June 8, 2026 letter.

WorthingtonHampshire County Special act (moratorium)
Battery storage and solar		 Sent to study Jun 8, 2026Sent to study

H.5294 (Rep. Lindsay Sabadosa, Sen. Paul Mark), approved by Worthington voters as Article 10 on Feb. 24, 2026, sought a one-year moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and battery storage. The Municipalities committee sent it to a study order (H.5480) on June 8, 2026, a committee action rather than a floor vote that usually ends a bill for the session.

  • malegislature.gov: H.5294 bill history
  • malegislature.gov: H.5480 study order

Attorney General decisions

The Attorney General's rulings on Worthington’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. Statewide record by type of bylaw.

Jun 8, 2026 WorthingtonArticle 6 (Feb. 24, 2026 STM) Moratorium
both		 Disapproved

Worthington adopted a town-wide moratorium on large ground-mounted solar and BESS over 600 kWh through June 30, 2027. The AG disapproved it for lacking a health, safety or welfare justification.

Decision letter, case 12194
Jun 8, 2026 WorthingtonArticles 7 and 8 (Feb. 24, 2026 STM) Moratorium
both		 Disapproved

Worthington's limited-area moratoria on large solar (Art. 7) and on large energy storage (Art. 8) were disapproved because they were not limited in scope or time; the AG also struck Art. 8's ban on storage over 5 MWh as unreasonable.
