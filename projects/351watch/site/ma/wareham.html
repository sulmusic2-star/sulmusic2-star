Bylaw rulings and town actions (4)

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Nov 24, 2025). Article 16 (Apr 28, 2025 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Wareham allowed large solar and battery storage in its Business Development Overlay District. The Attorney General approved it over an opposition letter, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act. Decision letter, case 11832

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Apr 22, 2024). Article 24 (Oct 23, 2023 Annual Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Wareham's battery storage rules were approved except provisions limiting battery storage to systems tied to large-scale solar, which amounted to a complete ban on principal-use (standalone) battery storage. Decision letter, case 11191

  • Attorney General: approved in part (letter dated Sep 19, 2023). Article S15 (Apr 25, 2022 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Wareham's rewritten solar bylaw was approved except several provisions, including a visual-impact requirement, an alternative-site analysis, a limit on battery storage, consultant-selection rules and a herbicide ban. Decision letter, case 10603

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Mar 21, 2022). Article 17 (June 12, 2021 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Wareham limited large ground-mounted solar to parcels of 10 acres or less that had been cleared for at least five years. The Attorney General disapproved it as an unreasonable solar regulation with no health, safety or welfare record, and as an improper open-ended moratorium. Decision letter, case 10230
