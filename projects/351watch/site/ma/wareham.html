Attorney General decisions

Decisions on Wareham’s solar and battery-storage bylaws. What survives review statewide.

DecidedTown and articleTypeOutcomeSummary and source
Nov 24, 2025 WarehamArticle 16 (Apr. 28, 2025 STM) Zoning regulation
both		 Approved

Wareham allowed large solar and BESS in its Business Development Overlay District. The AG approved it over an opposition letter, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act.

Decision letter, case 11832
Apr 22, 2024 WarehamArticle 24 (Oct. 23, 2023 ATM) Zoning regulation
BESS		 Approved in part

Wareham's BESS rules were approved except provisions limiting BESS to systems tied to large-scale solar, which amounted to a complete ban on principal-use (standalone) BESS.

Decision letter, case 11191
Sep 19, 2023 WarehamArticle S15 (Apr. 25, 2022 STM) Zoning regulation
both		 Approved in part

Wareham's rewritten solar bylaw was approved except several provisions, including a visual-impact requirement, an alternative-site analysis, a limit on battery storage, consultant-selection rules and a herbicide ban.

Decision letter, case 10603
Mar 21, 2022 WarehamArticle 17 (June 12, 2021 STM) Zoning regulation
solar		 Disapproved

Wareham limited large ground-mounted solar to parcels of 10 acres or less that had been cleared for at least five years. The AG disapproved it as an unreasonable solar regulation with no health, safety or welfare record, and as an improper open-ended moratorium.

Decision letter, case 10230
