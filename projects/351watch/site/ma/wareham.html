Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Attorney General: approved, Nov 24, 2025. Article 16 (Apr. 28, 2025 STM), zoning regulation.

    Wareham allowed large solar and BESS in its Business Development Overlay District. The AG approved it over an opposition letter, with comments on the 2024 Climate Act. Decision letter, case 11832

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Apr 22, 2024. Article 24 (Oct. 23, 2023 ATM), zoning regulation.

    Wareham's BESS rules were approved except provisions limiting BESS to systems tied to large-scale solar, which amounted to a complete ban on principal-use (standalone) BESS. Decision letter, case 11191

  • Attorney General: approved in part, Sep 19, 2023. Article S15 (Apr. 25, 2022 STM), zoning regulation.

    Wareham's rewritten solar bylaw was approved except several provisions, including a visual-impact requirement, an alternative-site analysis, a limit on battery storage, consultant-selection rules and a herbicide ban. Decision letter, case 10603

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Mar 21, 2022. Article 17 (June 12, 2021 STM), zoning regulation.

    Wareham limited large ground-mounted solar to parcels of 10 acres or less that had been cleared for at least five years. The AG disapproved it as an unreasonable solar regulation with no health, safety or welfare record, and as an improper open-ended moratorium. Decision letter, case 10230
