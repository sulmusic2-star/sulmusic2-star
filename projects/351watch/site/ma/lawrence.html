      6 Chickering LLC (managers Juan Yepez and Luis Yepez, represented by John T. The Budget and Finance Committee reports on them; Simon Pereira, Supervisor of Energy Projects, brought the item.

      Before this: Also on the Oct 6 City Council agenda.
