Bylaw rulings and town actions (4)

  • Special act (moratorium), battery storage and solar. Pending at Legislature, Sep 21, 2026.

    H.5641 (Rep. Leigh Davis, Sen. Paul Mark), approved by Becket voters as Article 5 on March 7, 2026, would authorize a six-month moratorium on large-scale solar and battery storage, exempting residential batteries. It was referred to the Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government on Aug 4, 2026, with a written-testimony hearing set for Sep 21, 2026.

    • malegislature.gov: H.5641 bill history
    • House bill summary for H.5641 (includes AG disapproval of Becket bylaw)

  • Attorney General: disapproved (letter dated Jun 22, 2026). Article 4 (Mar 7, 2026 Special Town Meeting), moratorium.

    Becket adopted a moratorium on large solar and battery storage through Sep 30, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it for lacking a health, safety or welfare justification. Decision letter, case 12196

  • Attorney General: approved (letter dated Jan 16, 2024). Article 5 (Jan 22, 2019 Special Town Meeting), zoning regulation.

    Becket's 2019 amendments to its solar PV section were approved in 2024. The Attorney General noted that a pesticide provision already in the bylaw would be preempted. Decision letter, case 11136
