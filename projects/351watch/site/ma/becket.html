Local rules and Attorney General rulings

  • Special act (moratorium), battery storage and solar. Pending at Legislature, Sep 21, 2026.

    H.5641 (Rep. Leigh Davis, Sen. Paul Mark), approved by Becket voters as Article 5 on March 7, 2026, would authorize a six-month moratorium on large-scale solar and battery storage, exempting residential batteries. It was referred to the Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government on Aug. 4, 2026, with a written-testimony hearing set for Sept. 21, 2026.

    • malegislature.gov: H.5641 bill history
    • House bill summary for H.5641 (includes AG disapproval of Becket bylaw)

  • Moratorium, battery storage and solar. Disapproved by AG, Jun 22, 2026.

    Voters at the March 7, 2026 Special Town Meeting adopted Article 4, a zoning moratorium on large-scale ground-mounted solar and battery storage through Sept. 30, 2026. The Attorney General disapproved it on June 22, 2026, citing G.L. c. 40A, s. 3.

    • House bill summary for H.5641 (includes AG disapproval of Becket bylaw)
    • Town of Becket: Article 4 moratorium handout (Feb. 21, 2026)

  • Attorney General: disapproved, Jun 22, 2026. Article 4 (Mar. 7, 2026 STM), moratorium.

    Becket adopted a moratorium on large solar and BESS through Sept. 30, 2026. The AG disapproved it for lacking a health, safety or welfare justification. Decision letter, case 12196

  • Attorney General: approved, Jan 16, 2024. Article 5 (Jan. 22, 2019 STM), zoning regulation.

    Becket's 2019 amendments to its solar PV section were approved in 2024. The AG noted that a pesticide provision already in the bylaw would be preempted. Decision letter, case 11136
