351WatchBot

351WatchBot is the crawler behind 351 Watch. It reads public meeting agendas, warrants and notices that towns and counties post on their own websites, and flags items about solar, battery storage, data centers, moratoria, zoning changes and similar land-use decisions.

How it identifies itself

Every request carries this User-Agent:

351WatchBot/0.1 (+https://351watch.vercel.app/bot)

Requests are also signed with Web Bot Auth (HTTP Message Signatures). Our public key is at /.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory, and each request names it in a Signature-Agent header.

What it does and doesn't do

  • Checks robots.txt before every request, including each redirect, and skips anything disallowed for 351WatchBot or *.
  • Makes at most one request per second to a host, or slower if robots.txt sets a Crawl-delay.
  • Visits each meeting page about once a day and downloads each agenda document once; after that it uses its own copy.
  • Reads only public pages. It never logs in, never submits forms and never tries to get past a CAPTCHA or other bot check.
  • Skips files larger than 40 MB.

Blocking it or changing its rate

To stop it, add this to your robots.txt and it will stop on its next visit:

User-agent: 351WatchBot
Disallow: /

To slow it down, add a Crawl-delay line (in seconds). If you run a town or county website and want a different arrangement, or think the crawler misbehaved, send us a note with your site address.

351 Watch compiles public records that towns post under state open meeting laws, plus state and legislative sources. It is informational only and not legal advice. Always confirm dates and terms with the town clerk or the cited source.
