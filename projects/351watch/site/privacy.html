Privacy and contact

351 Watch has no accounts, no passwords and no email. This note explains what we store and why.

Watchlists

When you create a watchlist we store the towns and topics you chose, the optional name and the optional Slack, Teams or Discord webhook URL you enter, when it was created and changed, and whether our daily messages were delivered. It is stored privately at our hosting provider, Vercel, under a random identifier. That identifier is in your watchlist's link and feed links, and it is the only key: anyone who has the link can view, change or delete the watchlist. You can delete it at any time from its page, which removes it from our storage.

We show webhook URLs only in shortened form, because anyone with the full URL can post to your channel. Messages go only to the URL you give us.

In your browser

We don't use cookies or analytics. The watchlist pages remember the links of watchlists you made in this browser's local storage, so you can find them again; clearing your browser's site data removes that list.

Payments

Everything is free during the founding beta. If paid plans open, payment is handled by Stripe; we would store only the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers on the watchlist the plan applies to.

Earlier waitlist signups

Before watchlists existed, some people joined a waitlist. Those entries are kept privately and deleted on request, or 12 months after signup, whichever comes first. We don't send email.

About the data on this site

The tracker uses public records: agendas and warrants that towns must post under the Massachusetts Open Meeting Law, Attorney General decision letters, legislative records, and news reports. Our crawler reads only publicly posted pages, identifies itself, follows robots.txt and limits how often it visits each town's website.

Corrections, removal requests and contact

We read every message. We can't reply, because we don't collect contact details; corrections show up on the site once checked.

351 Watch compiles public records that Massachusetts towns post under the Open Meeting Law, plus state and legislative sources. It is informational only and not legal advice. Always confirm dates and terms with the town clerk or the cited source.
