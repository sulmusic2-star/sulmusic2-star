Privacy and contact

351 Watch has no accounts or passwords, and it doesn't collect email addresses.

Watchlists

When you create a watchlist we store the towns and topics you chose, the optional name, the optional Slack, Teams or Discord webhook URL, when the watchlist was created and changed, and whether each daily message was delivered. It is stored privately at our hosting provider, Vercel, under a random identifier. That identifier is part of the watchlist's link and its feed links, and it is the only key: anyone who has the link can view, change or delete the watchlist. Deleting a watchlist from its page removes it from our storage.

We show webhook URLs only in shortened form, because anyone with the full URL can post to your channel. Messages go only to the URL you give us.

In your browser

We don't use cookies or analytics. The watchlist pages keep the links of watchlists made in this browser in its local storage so you can find them again. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes that list.

Payments

Everything is free during the founding beta. If paid plans open, Stripe will handle payment, and we would store only the Stripe customer and subscription IDs on the watchlist the plan applies to.

Earlier waitlist signups

Before watchlists existed, some people joined a waitlist. We keep those entries privately and delete each one on request or 12 months after signup, whichever comes first. We don't send email.

Where the data comes from

The site is built from public records: agendas and warrants that towns post under open meeting laws, Attorney General decision letters, court rulings, legislative records and news reports. Our crawler reads only publicly posted pages, identifies itself, follows robots.txt and limits how often it visits each town's website.

Corrections, removal requests and contact

We read every message. The form doesn't collect contact details, so we can't reply; a correction shows up on the site once we've checked it.

351 Watch compiles public records: agendas and warrants that towns post under state open meeting laws, Attorney General decision letters, court rulings and legislative records. It is informational only and is not legal advice. Confirm dates and terms with the town clerk or the linked source before relying on them.
