Can a town pause data centers?

Yes, in Massachusetts. State law protects solar and battery storage, not data centers.

The Attorney General approved data-center pauses in Shutesbury (case 12047, Jan. 20, 2026), Templeton (case 12294, Sept. 22, 2026) and Gill (Sept. 24, 2026).
