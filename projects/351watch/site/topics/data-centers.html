Attorney General review

Data centers are not a use protected by G.L. c. 40A, §3, unlike solar and battery storage, so a town data-center moratorium can survive review. The Attorney General approved them in Shutesbury (case 12047, Jan. 20, 2026), Templeton (case 12294, Sept. 22, 2026) and Gill (Sept. 24, 2026, where the battery-storage half of the same article was struck).
