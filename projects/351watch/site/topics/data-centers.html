Attorney General review

Unlike solar and battery storage, data centers are not a use protected by G.L. c. 40A, §3, so town data-center moratoria can survive review: the Attorney General approved them in Shutesbury (case 12047, Jan. 20, 2026), Templeton (case 12294, Sept. 22, 2026) and Gill (Sept. 24, 2026, where the battery-storage half of the same article was struck).
