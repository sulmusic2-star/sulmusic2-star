  1. Large dual-use solar array at 299 River Road

    Whately, Conservation Commission, 7 pm, Town Hall

    The Conservation Commission held a public hearing. Hyperion Systems, LLC, on behalf of Sidney Chang, wants a wetlands permit (a Notice of Intent) to build a large-scale ground-mounted dual-use solar array, with farming and solar on the same land, at 299 River Road. Also on the agenda: Chang Farm dual-use solar array, 299 River Road: wetlands filing (Notice of Intent), and 1 more.

    Read the Oct 21 agenda (PDF): Whately Conservation Commission

    Details

    Speaking: Remote only: no in-person attendance, and public participation in the hearing is by remote means only. Join by Zoom (Meeting ID 880 5078 0029, Passcode 903907) or dial 877-853-5247 or 888-788-0099.

    • Whately · Conservation Commission: Large dual-use solar array at 299 River Road: Conservation Commission hearingHearing

      Hyperion Systems, LLC, on behalf of Sidney Chang, wants a wetlands permit (a Notice of Intent) to build a large-scale ground-mounted dual-use solar array, with farming and solar on the same land, at 299 River Road.

      Before this: The Planning Board’s Sep 29 agenda introduced the project as Chang Farm’s agrivoltaic project at 299 River Road.

    • Chang Farm dual-use solar array, 299 River Road: wetlands filing (Notice of Intent)

      Hyperion Solar Systems and Chang Farm have filed a Notice of Intent (the wetlands permit application) with the Conservation Commission for a 400 kW ac (590.40 kW dc) ground-mounted, elevated, dual-use solar array of 960 panels on 2.14 acres of farm field at 299 River Road, with farming to continue under panels set 10 feet high. Part of the array sits in the 100-foot wetland buffer and the preliminary 100-year floodplain, with the nearest post about 42.5 feet from the wetland, and the applicant asks to be heard at the commission’s next available hearing.

      Before this: The Planning Board had an introduction of Chang Farm’s agrivoltaic project at 299 River Road on Sep 29.

    • Chang Farm solar array above crops, 299 River Road: wetlands permit application

      Hyperion Solar Systems and Chang Farm have filed a Notice of Intent (the wetlands permit application) with the Conservation Commission for a 400 kW ac (590.40 kW dc) ground-mounted, dual-use solar array on 2.14 acres of farm field at 299 River Road. The 960 panels would sit 10 feet up on tracking racks so farming continues below; 20 posts fall inside the 100-foot wetland buffer and 187 inside the preliminary 100-year floodplain. Berkshire Design Group’s letter says it looks forward to discussing the project at the Commission’s next available hearing.

      Before this: Chang Farm’s agrivoltaic project was introduced on the Sep 29 Planning Board agenda.
