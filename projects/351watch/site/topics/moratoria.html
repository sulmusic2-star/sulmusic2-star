    • Marcellus, NY · Town Board: 12-month moratorium on data centers and crypto mining, Local Law E: hearingHearing

      Local Law E of 2026 would stop the development, construction or use of new commercial cryptocurrency mining operations, data mining, data processing centers and data storage facilities anywhere in the Town of Marcellus for twelve months.
