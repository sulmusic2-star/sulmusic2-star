    Winchester, Planning Board, 7 pm, Select Board Room, Winchester Town Hall, 71 Mt. Vernon Street

    The Planning Board held a public hearing. A citizen petition to change 114 Church Street from the ConservancyInstitutional District (SCI) to Apartment House Residential District B (RB-20). Also on the agenda: minimum lot width in the RDA (a request to the Conservation Commission to rule whether wetlands rules apply) zone cut from 120 to 100 feet, and 2 more.

    Read the Oct 27 agenda: Winchester Planning Board

    Details

    • Winchester · Planning Board: Rezone 114 Church Street from institutional to apartment zoning: hearing (Article 37)Hearing

      A citizen petition to change 114 Church Street from the ConservancyInstitutional District (SCI) to Apartment House Residential District B (RB-20). A similar petition, Article 38, would move 18 Raymond Place from SCI to the General Resident District (RB-6.5).

    • Minimum lot width in the RDA zone cut from 120 to 100 feet: hearing (Article 4)Hearing

      Article 4 would let a conforming house on a nonconforming lot be rebuilt, extended or changed when the problem is lot width, and would cut the minimum lot width in the RDA District from 120 feet to 100 feet. Article 5 (design review) and Article 6 (permit paperwork), also by the Planning Board, are on the same hearing.

      Before this: The Select Board heard a presentation of the Planning Board’s warrant articles Oct 5.

    • Rezone part of The Ledges Condominium, 7 Wainwright Road: hearing (Article 40)Hearing

    Hubbardston, Town Meeting, 7 pm, Hubbardston Center School, 8 Elm Street

    Town Meeting voted on this (Article 11).

    Read the Town Meeting warrant: Hubbardston Town Meeting

    Details

    • Hubbardston · Town Meeting: Zoning updates to match new state law on variances, grandfathering: vote (Article 11)Vote

      Article 11 rewrites parts of the zoning bylaw to match recent changes to the state Zoning Act: variances would turn on “practical difficulty” instead of “substantial hardship”, some additions to older nonconforming homes would be allowed without a special permit, a nonconforming use would be lost after four years of abandonment instead of two, and a permit’s protection would run twenty-four months instead of six.
