Jackson Township, Ohio

We don’t read Jackson Township’s own agendas: Jackson Township’s website has no recent agendas with dates on them.

Zoning changes still reach us through the Franklin County. Ohio law (ORC 519.12) sends a township’s zoning text and map amendments to the county or regional planning commission for a recommendation, so those appear on its agendas. We won’t see special land uses, site plans, conditional uses, moratorium votes taken by the township board itself, or anything else the county body doesn’t review. Its next meeting on these subjects is on Thu Nov 12, about request to rezone 1945 Jackson Road.
