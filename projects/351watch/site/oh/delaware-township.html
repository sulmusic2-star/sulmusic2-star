Delaware Township, Ohio

We don’t read Delaware Township’s own agendas: we couldn’t find a website for Delaware Township.

Zoning changes still reach us through the Delaware County Regional Planning Commission. Ohio law (ORC 519.12) sends a township’s zoning text and map amendments to the county or regional planning commission for a recommendation, so those appear on its agendas. We won’t see special land uses, site plans, conditional uses, moratorium votes taken by the township board itself, or anything else the county body doesn’t review. Nothing is coming up there on these subjects.
