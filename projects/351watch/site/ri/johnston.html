Tue Oct 13

  1. Rezoning two Shun Pike lots from residential to industrial

    Town Council, 7 pm, 1478 Atwood Avenue

    The Town Council held a public hearing. Ordinance 2026-8 would change the zoning map for Assessor’s Plat 43, Lots 264 and 265 on Shun Pike from R-40 (residential) to I-Industrial. It began as a petition by Bachar Sasa (2025-CP-10). The agenda doesn’t say what would be built.
