    Planning Board Assembly Room, Narragansett Town Hall, 25 Fifth Avenue, Narragansett, or join online Agenda: Narragansett Planning Board, Tue Oct 20

    • Planning Board: Boon Street Special Planning District zoning amendment: staff updateDiscussion

      Town staff update the board on Boon Street Capital, LLC’s proposed zoning ordinance amendment, Section 4A, which would create a Boon Street Special Planning District. No hearing or vote on it is scheduled at this meeting.

      Before this: The Planning Board had the amendment under recommendations to the Town Council on its Sep 15 and Sep 28 agendas; the Town Council’s Oct 19 agenda has a motion to accept the applicant’s withdrawal of the petition without prejudice.

Mon Oct 19

  1. 7 pm

    Town Council ASSEMBLY ROOM, Narragansett Town Hall, 25 Fifth Avenue, Narragansett, or join online Agenda: Narragansett Town Council, Mon Oct 19

    • Town Council: Boon Street Special District zoning text amendment: petition withdrawnWithdrawn

      Boon Street Capital, LLC has withdrawn its petition to amend the text of the Zoning Ordinance to create a “Boon Street Special District”. The council votes on a motion to accept the withdrawal without prejudice, which leaves the company free to file again.

      Before this: The Town Council continued its hearing on the text amendment to Oct 19 at its Sep 21 meeting; the Planning Board had the petition on its Sep 15 and Sep 28 agendas.

    Agenda: Narragansett Town Council, Mon Oct 19
