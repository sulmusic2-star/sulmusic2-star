Tue Oct 13

  1. Town votes on a solar carport contract (up to $1,658,677.65) for its Highway Facility

    Town Council, 7:30 pm, Town Council Chambers, 180 High Street, Wakefield

    The Town Council took this up. Also on the agenda, it took up Old Tower Hill Road form-based zoning: hearing continues from Sep 14 and Sep 28.

    Read the Oct 13 agenda (PDF): South Kingstown Town Council

    Details

    Speaking: Public comment period (30 minutes): speak once, up to five minutes, on agenda items. Public hearing. There is also a 30-minute public comment period: speak once, up to five minutes.

    • Town Council: Town votes on a solar carport contract (up to $1,658,677.65) for its Highway FacilityVote

      The council votes on hiring RISE Group, Inc. of Cranston to design and build a solar carport at the town’s Highway Facility: $1,518,022 base, plus $61,671 for the domestic-content equipment alternate, plus a 5% contingency, for a total not to exceed $1,658,677.65.

    • Old Tower Hill Road form-based zoning: hearing continues from Sep 14 and Sep 28Hearing

      The council proposes a new zoning section (610) with a ‘form-based code’ for the Old Tower Hill Road area: five new districts with rules aimed at a walkable, mixed-use neighborhood, plus when the rules apply and how projects get reviewed.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 14 (hearing opened) and Sep 28 (continued) Town Council agendas; the Planning Board followed up Oct 6. Resident emails for and against are in this agenda’s communications.
