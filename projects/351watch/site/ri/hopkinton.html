Mon Oct 19

  1. Data centers banned in every zoning district: Town Council hearing on the ordinance

    Town Council, 6:45 pm, Town Hall, 1 Town House Road, or on Zoom

    The Town Council took this up.

    Read the Oct 19 agenda: Hopkinton Town Council

    Details

    Speaking: Public comment on agenda items is at the Chair’s discretion. On Zoom, use the raise hand function or dial *9, then *6 to unmute; state your name and address before speaking. Being in person at Town Hall is the most reliable way to take part.

    • Town Council: Data centers banned in every zoning district: Town Council hearing on the ordinanceHearing

      Councilors Geary and Burns propose amending the zoning ordinance to define a data center and make data centers a prohibited use in every zoning district, with a new purpose section (5.8.1) and a fix to a numbering error in Section 5.6. Council discussion and public comment follow, then a motion to continue the hearing or close it and either set a later date for adopting the ordinance or vote on adoption now.

      Before this: Also on the Sep 21 Town Council agenda; the Planning Board’s Oct 7 agenda had a possible vote on reconsidering its earlier vote on the data center advisory.
