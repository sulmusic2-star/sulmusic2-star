      How we rank them

      Each story gets points for three things.

      • What’s at stake. What it is (a data center, battery storage, a ban or moratorium, large solar), how big it is (megawatts, acres, homes), whether it covers the whole town, and how final the step is: a vote or hearing counts more than a discussion.
      • How stirred up people are. How many boards and meetings it has been on, a delay from an earlier meeting, a citizen petition, letters from the public, an appeal or lawsuit, neighboring towns taking up the same subject, and regional news naming the town (headlines from newsrooms such as MassLive, WBUR, the Boston Globe, Syracuse.com and ecoRI News).
      • How soon. A meeting in the next week counts most.

      The grey line under a story says what lifted it.
