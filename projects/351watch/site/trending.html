      How the ranking works

      Each story gets points for what it is (a data center, battery storage, a ban or moratorium, large solar), how big it is, how final the step is (a vote or hearing counts more than a discussion), how many boards and meetings have taken it up, whether neighboring towns have the same subject on their agendas, whether regional news has named the town, and how soon the meeting is.
