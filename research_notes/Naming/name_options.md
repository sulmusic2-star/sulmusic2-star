# Name options to replace "351 Watch"

Started and finished 2026-10-12. Status: **complete** (brainstorm, domains, conflict screen, scores, shortlist, recommendation). Nothing was bought.

**Recommendation in one line:** Town Ahead (townahead.com) first, then Agenda Ahead (agendaahead.com), then Ample Notice (amplenotice.com). All three .com domains are available at $11.25 a year. See section 6.

## Brief

- **The problem with the current name.** "351" is the number of towns in Massachusetts, and the product is going national.
- **What the product does.** It reads town, township and county board agendas days before each meeting. It alerts people to land-use and siting items: solar, battery storage, data centers, moratoria, zoning changes, housing permits, cell towers and wetlands filings.
- **How alerts arrive.** Email, calendar, RSS and Slack/Teams. Search over every agenda it reads is free.
- **Who uses it.** Residents who want to know what's coming in their town and the towns next door, and pros who watch many places: developers, land-use lawyers and siting platforms.
- **Tone.** Plain, trustworthy, civic and a bit friendly. No hype, no "AI", no crypto.

**Names to steer clear of** (from `../Meeting monitor national expansion/competitor_coverage_map.md`): Hamlet, Citizen Portal, GatherGov, MyTownView, Red Tape Recordings, CivicSearch, Civic IQ, MultiState, Quorum, FiscalNote/PolicyNote/Curate, Councilmatic, Heatmap, Spark, SitePath, Site Scout, Transect, Paces, Shovels, Zoneomics, Cleanview, LandGate, OrdinanceWatch, Documenters, Agenda Watch, LocalLens, CivicTide, Council Data Project, Granicus/govDelivery/Legistar, CivicPlus (Notify Me, Agenda Center), BoardDocs, CivicClerk.

## 3. Conflict screen

**This is a first screen, not trademark clearance.** I'm not a lawyer. Before you commit to a name, have a trademark attorney run a full search: federal, state and common-law, plus a likelihood-of-confusion opinion.

**How I checked.**
- **USPTO.** I searched every candidate's word mark through the backend of the USPTO Trademark Search system (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-information) on 2026-10-12. I recorded live marks in the classes that matter:
  - 009: software
  - 035: business information
  - 038: alert transmission
  - 041: publishing and information
  - 042: SaaS
  - 045: legal services and monitoring

  Each hit links to its USPTO status record (TSDR), as `tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=<serial>`.
- **Web and app stores.** Web searches for companies and products in govtech, civic tech, real estate, permitting, energy and local news. App-store searches by name.
- **Domains.** For taken .com names that matter, an RDAP lookup and a fetch of the site.
- Justia's trademark search and uspto.report both blocked the fetcher (HTTP 403), so the USPTO backend was the only trademark source.

**Risk scale.**
- **High:** an active product with the same name in the same or a neighboring field, or a live federal mark for similar software or services.
- **Medium:** the same name in a different field, a live mark in software classes for unrelated goods, or a meaning that will confuse people.
- **Low:** nothing found beyond generic use of the words.

## 4. Scores (names that survived)

Each name gets 1–5 on each criterion, and 5 is always best:
- **Clarity:** says what it does.
- **Memorability.**
- **Pronounceability.**
- **National:** works outside New England.
- **Growth:** room to cover all local decisions, not just energy.
- **Conflict:** 5 = lowest risk, using section 3.
- **Domain:** 5 = .com at the registry price; 3 = a good variant (get-/hq .com, or .com on the aftermarket); 2 = only .app or .us.

## 5. Shortlist

All .com prices are Vercel's: $11.25 for the first year and $11.25 a year to renew. Nothing has been bought.

### 1. Town Ahead
- **Domain:** **townahead.com, $11.25 a year.** Also open: townsahead.com ($11.25), townahead.org ($9.99, renews at $10.99), townahead.app ($9.99, renews at $15).
- **Meaning:** see what's ahead for your town. "Town" is the plainest American word for local government; people say "in town", "town hall", "out of town". It stretches to townships, and to counties without much strain.
- **In a sentence:**
  - "Get Town Ahead alerts for Carver."
  - "Town Ahead found 3 items on Tuesday's planning board agenda."
  - "Town Ahead: Carver and 4 neighboring towns."
  - Calendar entry: "Town Ahead · Carver Planning Board: solar bylaw (item 4)".
- **Bot name:** TownAheadBot, or "Town Ahead" as the Slack/Teams app name.
- **Conflicts:** none found. No USPTO mark, no product, no app.
- **Watch-outs:** on its own it doesn't say "agendas" or "alerts", so the tagline has to do that work. For county users, "town" is a slight stretch.

### 2. Agenda Ahead
- **Domain:** **agendaahead.com, $11.25 a year.** Also open: agendahead.com and agendasahead.com (typo guards, $11.25 each), agendaahead.org ($9.99), agendaahead.app ($9.99).

- **In a sentence:**

  - "Agenda Ahead found 3 items on Tuesday's planning board agenda." ("agenda" appears twice, which is a little clunky.)
- **Bot name:** AgendaAheadBot.
- **Conflicts:** none on the exact name. The "agenda" root is crowded in meeting software (AGENDA KEEPER, KEYSTONE AGENDA), and the name is close to descriptive, so the mark would be weaker. Low risk.
- **Strengths:** the most self-explanatory name on the list. It works the same for towns, townships and counties, and it helps search ("agenda" is the word people type).

### 3. Ample Notice
- **Domain:** **amplenotice.com, $11.25 a year.** Also open: amplenotice.org ($9.99), amplenotice.app ($9.99).

## 6. Recommendation

**1. Town Ahead (townahead.com, $11.25 a year): my first choice.**
- It is the warmest name that is still plain, and it reads naturally in every alert, calendar entry and Slack message.
- It has no conflicts at all in the USPTO, on the web or in the app stores.
- It's suggestive rather than descriptive, so it should be easier to protect than the "Agenda ___" names.
- It covers more than energy (anything coming up in town) and makes the neighbor feature easy to say: "Town Ahead for Carver and 4 neighboring towns".
- What it doesn't say ("agendas", "alerts") a tagline can: **"Town Ahead: what's on your town's agenda, before it's decided."**

**2. Agenda Ahead (agendaahead.com, $11.25 a year): the clearest name.**
- People understand it before they finish reading it. It fits towns, townships and counties equally, and land-use lawyers and siting teams will take it at face value.
- The "agenda" keyword helps the free search pages show up in search engines.
- It loses to Town Ahead on warmth and ownability: it's close to descriptive, and "agenda" is crowded in meeting software.
- Pick it if the paid pro tier matters more than resident sign-ups.

**3. Ample Notice (amplenotice.com, $11.25 a year): the one with a story.**
- It turns the product's value into a promise. Open-meeting law gives the public 48 hours' notice; Ample Notice gives you days.
- It is distinctive, easy to say, uses the most civic word on the list ("notice"), and its .com is available.
- It ranks below the top two because "ample" is a less everyday word, and because "Ample" is a live brand in battery storage, one of the sectors the product covers.

**If you can get fairnotice.com at a reasonable price, Fair Notice beats Ample Notice.** It is the strongest phrase on the list. But someone registered getfairnotice.com a month ago, and an active Fair Notice program already works with courts, so it needs a lawyer's look before any money is spent.

**Next steps before choosing** (nothing has been bought):
1. Have a trademark attorney run a full clearance search on the chosen name, in classes 009, 035, 038, 041, 042 and 045.
2. Check the same name in state business registries and social handles (X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, YouTube).
3. Buy the .com together with the typo or plural guard in one go, for example townahead.com plus townsahead.com, or agendaahead.com plus agendahead.com.
