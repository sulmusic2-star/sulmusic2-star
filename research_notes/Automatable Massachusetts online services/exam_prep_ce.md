# Massachusetts Occupational License Exam Prep and Continuing Education: Self-Serve, Software-Delivered Opportunities

Research date: 2026-10-10. Method: web search plus page fetches. Note: mass.gov returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches from this environment, so the mass.gov facts below (BBRS minutes, forms) come from search-result excerpts of those documents, not full reads. Facts that rest only on vendor or marketing pages are labeled as such. "Estimate" or "Inference" marks my own calculations and judgments.

## Q1. Which MA license exams have meaningful annual candidate volume and an MA-specific body of knowledge? (volumes, pass rates, vendors, code editions)

### Takeaway
Two markets have hard, citable volume data: the MTEL (about 34,000 subtest attempts a year, Pearson) and the Construction Supervisor License (CSL) exam (about 1,100 attempts and about 650 first-time takers in roughly the first 14 weeks of 2025, PSI). The CSL pass rate fell to about 25% after the January 2025 switch from Prometric to PSI and to the 10th-edition code. That makes the CSL exam the clearest acute pain point. Electrician, plumber, hoisting and real estate exams are clearly MA-specific, but I found no official volume or pass-rate figures for them.

### Cited Findings

**Construction Supervisor License (CSL), regulated by BBRS within the Division of Occupational Licensure (DOL)**
- Massachusetts has no "general contractor license." The relevant credential is the CSL, regulated by the BBRS within DOL. PSI delivers the exam at test centers, and candidates pay PSI directly. — [OpenExamPrep CSL exam facts (page updated July 2026)](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- Format per OpenExamPrep: Unrestricted is 75 questions in 4 hours; Restricted (one- and two-family) is 60 questions in 4 hours; Specialty is 30–50 questions in 2–3 hours. All require 70% to pass, and PSI may add 4–7 unscored experimental items. — [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- Sources conflict on time and fee. Adapt Digital Solutions lists the Unrestricted exam at 75 questions, 3 hours, 70% and $100 per attempt. OpenExamPrep lists $75 to PSI plus a $23 application fee ($65 for Specialty), a $150 initial license fee and a $100 renewal every 2 years. — [Adapt Digital Solutions](https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/articles/massachusetts-contractor-license-requirements/); [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- Open-book references candidates must bring: 2021 IBC, 2021 IRC, 780 CMR 10th edition (effective October 11, 2024), 521 CMR (accessibility), 225 CMR (MA energy code), 2021 IECC and OSHA 1926. PSI does not supply the books, and electronic devices are not allowed. — [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- Adapt Digital says candidates may bring any edition of the referenced code books, which conflicts with OpenExamPrep's edition-specific list. — [Adapt Digital Solutions](https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/articles/massachusetts-contractor-license-requirements/)
- **Volume and pass rate, from BBRS minutes of April 8, 2025:**
  - About 1,100 CSL exams were taken with PSI "since January," about 650 of them by first-time takers.
  - The pass rate under PSI was about 25%, against 51% under Prometric.
  - Many candidates were retaking two, three or four times, almost within two weeks.
  - The exam has been based on the 10th edition of the State Building Code since January 1, 2025.
  - Source: [BBRS minutes 4/8/2025 (search-result excerpt; full PDF blocked)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-minutes-4-8-25/download)
- **From BBRS minutes of September 9, 2025:**
  - The DOL Chief meets PSI monthly to review questions with pass rates below 60%.
  - Director Gropman noted that initial pass rates were low.
  - A board member supported adding an hour to the unrestricted CSL exam.
  - Source: [BBRS minutes 9/9/2025 (search-result excerpt)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-minutes-9-9-25/download)
- A CSL Candidate Handbook dated March 2025 exists on mass.gov, and the state directs all exam questions to PSI. — [CSL Candidate Handbook](https://www.mass.gov/doc/csl-candidate-handbook/download); [Apply for a CSL (mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-construction-supervisor-license)
- The Unrestricted exam covers the full code. The Restricted exam focuses mainly on 780 CMR Chapter 51 (residential provisions). — [Adapt Digital Solutions](https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/articles/massachusetts-contractor-license-requirements/)

### Inferences
- **CSL annual volume (estimate):** 650 first-time takers in about 14 weeks (Jan 1 to about Apr 8, 2025) annualizes to roughly 2,400 first-time candidates and about 4,000 attempts a year. Caveats: early 2025 may include pent-up demand from the vendor transition, and the 25% pass rate inflates attempts.
- **CSL demand signal:** a 25% pass rate with repeat attempts within two weeks suggests candidates are paying $75–$100 per retake without changing how they prepare. A prep product aimed at "the new PSI/10th-edition exam" has a clear message.
- **CSL time limit:** OpenExamPrep (updated July 2026) lists 4 hours, while older sources list 3 hours. Together with the September 2025 board discussion, this suggests the extra hour was adopted. I could not confirm it from a primary source.
- **MTEL scale:** summing every test and subtest row in the 2023–24 report gives about 34,100 total attempts and about 30,000 first-time attempts (subtests counted separately). This is the largest MA-specific exam market found.
- **MTEL weak spots:** pass rates are very low on Elementary Mathematics (28%), Physical Education (31.5%), Middle School Mathematics (40%) and ESL (42%), and middling on Foundations of Reading (68%) and General Curriculum Math/Science (61%). Those are the strongest per-test pain points.
- **Exams that are MA-authored, not national-plus-amendments:**
  - Plumber: 248 CMR is the Massachusetts Uniform State Plumbing Code.
  - Hoisting: MGL c.146 plus 230 CMR.
  - CSL: 780 CMR amendments layered on the ICC I-Codes.
  - Electrician: 527 CMR 12 amendments to the NEC. Edition not verified.
- **Learner's permit:** high volume, but the content is generic, and a free incumbent (driving-tests.org) has library distribution. Weak fit.
- **Title 5:** bundled with a mandatory NEIWPCC class and field exam, so there is little room for independent prep. Poor fit.

### Gaps
- **Official volumes and pass rates not found** for MA electrician, plumber, hoisting, real estate (PSI) and insurance (Prometric). Board minutes and attachments on mass.gov likely hold the electrician and plumber figures, but mass.gov blocked direct fetches.
- **Unverified pass-rate claims:**
  - Real estate: vendor pages cite a first-time pass rate of about 54% (attributed to the MA Board) and say about 25% of students retake. Neither was verified against a primary source.
  - Insurance: a third-party page quotes line pass rates (Life 45.3% on 4,550 takers, A&H 60.3%, Property 49.5%, Casualty 51.0%, Personal Lines 36.2%). The originating page and year are unverified, so do not rely on them.
- **Not researched in this pass:** MA notary (believed to have no exam), Home Improvement Contractor registration, CDL knowledge test (national FMCSA content), cosmetology/barber, EMT (NREMT national), home inspector, drinking-water and wastewater operator exams, pesticide applicator core, lead/asbestos, and hunting/boating safety (state-run free courses). I found no MassDEP operator exam data.
- **Not confirmed from a primary source:** the CSL exam time (3 or 4 hours) and the exact fee. The current PSI CSL candidate handbook is needed.
- **No official RMV data** on annual permit-test volume or pass rate.

## Q3. Content and IP constraints (ICC copyright, original questions, use of real exam questions)

### Takeaway
Original practice questions that cite code section numbers, paraphrase requirements and teach navigation are standard industry practice and appear legally safe. Reproducing ICC I-Code text (IBC, IRC, IECC) or NFPA text (NEC, NFPA 54) is not: courts have held that privately authored standards keep their copyright even when incorporated into law. Massachusetts-authored regulation text (780 CMR amendments, 248 CMR, 230 CMR, MGL) is likely usable under the government-edicts doctrine, but that is my inference and has not been legally verified. Using recalled or actual PSI or Pearson exam items is off limits.

### Cited Findings
- **ICC v. UpCodes (filed 2017):** the case addresses whether ICC-drafted codes adopted into law stay copyright-protected. In 2020 the court denied both parties' summary-judgment motions. — [Construction Dive](https://constructiondive.com/news/icc-v-upcodes-can-a-private-organization-copyright-the-law/558723); [UpCodes "free law"](https://cms.up.codes/free-law)
- **Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org (Supreme Court, April 2020):** under the government-edicts doctrine, works authored by judges or legislators acting in their official capacity cannot be copyrighted. ICC argues the decision supports its position that privately drafted codes remain protected. — [ICC Building Safety Journal](https://www.iccsafe.org/building-safety-journal/bsj-hits/supreme-court-decision-bolsters-the-code-councils-copyright-case/)
- **ASTM v. Public.Resource.Org:** per ICC's account, the court held that standards incorporated by reference into regulations do not enter the public domain, and permanently enjoined unauthorized distribution of certain standards. — [ICC BSJ](https://www.iccsafe.org/building-safety-journal/bsj-hits/federal-court-rules-standards-do-not-lose-copyright-protection/)
- **ICC publicACCESS:** ICC offers free read-only access to its codes. Copying, printing and highlighting require a paid subscription. — [ICC BSJ](https://www.iccsafe.org/building-safety-journal/bsj-hits/federal-court-rules-standards-do-not-lose-copyright-protection/)
- **CSL open-book format:** candidates must bring their own copies of the I-Codes and MA regulations, and PSI supplies none. — [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- **Disclaimer practice:** independent prep sites disclaim affiliation and tell candidates to confirm details with the official sponsor. — [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
- **Plumbing code and NFPA 54:** the MA plumbing code incorporates NFPA 54 "as amended by 248 CMR 5.00." — [OpenExamPrep MA plumber](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-plumber)

### Inferences
- **What to reproduce:** the product should cite section numbers and paraphrase rather than reproduce ICC or NFPA text, and point users to their own legally purchased books. For CSL this fits the exam, since candidates must own the books anyway. The skill being sold is fast lookup and interpretation, which needs no copied text.
- **MA-authored text:** 780 CMR MA amendments, 248 CMR, 230 CMR (hoisting) and MGL c.146/c.142 are state-authored. Under Georgia v. PRO they are likely free to quote, but legal review is advisable.
- **Plumbing:** 248 CMR is the most quotable body of code because it is MA-authored. The NFPA 54 gas portion is not.
- **Electrical:** the NEC (NFPA 70) is NFPA-copyrighted, so electrician prep has the same paraphrase-only constraint as the I-Codes.
- **Exam items:** test vendors' candidate agreements typically forbid disclosing exam content. A bank of AI-generated original questions mapped to the published content outlines is the safe route. "Recalled questions" or brain-dump sourcing is not.
- **Accuracy liability:** AI-generated code questions can be wrong, as the contradictory free hoisting PDFs show. Plan for an automated verification pass, for example cross-checking each answer against the cited section, as part of the build.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve PSI's or Pearson's candidate confidentiality and non-disclosure language.
- No case law or guidance specific to exam-prep publishers using ICC section headings or table numbers was found.
- No legal opinion was found on whether 780 CMR's MA amendments are public domain. My conclusion is inference only.
- The NEC edition adopted in 527 CMR 12.00 was not verified (2023 NEC is believed but unconfirmed).
- ICC licensing terms and costs for exam-prep publishers were not researched.

## Q4. Continuing education: approval requirements, online rules, fees, and online CE prices

### Takeaway
CE is a recurring, mandatory market, but every MA board requires pre-approval and a named qualified person:
- **CSL:** a registered course coordinator ($400) and instructors meeting experience requirements. The regulation text found caps online CSL CE at 6 of the 12 required hours.
- **Electrician:** Board pre-approval of each online course. CE can be fully online (21 hours every 3 years).
- **Real estate:** school authorization plus a broker-instructor with 30 hours of instructor training.

All three can be delivered by software without per-student work. All need the owner to hold or contract a credentialed coordinator or instructor and to refresh content each code cycle.

### Cited Findings

**CSL CE (780 CMR 110.R5.4)**
- **Hours and topics:**
  - 12 hours every 2 years for the unrestricted license, 10 for restricted (one- and two-family) and 6 for specialty.
  - Required topics: code review (4 hours, or 2 for specialty), workplace safety (1), business practices and workers' compensation (1), energy (1) and lead-safe practices (1, first cycle only).
  - Source: [780 CMR 110.R5.4 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-4); [mass.gov CSL coordinators and courses list (April 2023)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/construction-supervisor-coordinators-and-courses-as-of-april-2023/download)
- **Online cap:** licensees may complete up to 6 hours online, and the rest must be in-person classroom training. The BBRS may allow fully online completion for documented disability, medical conditions or military service. — [Cornell LII 780 CMR 110.R5.4](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-4)
- **The cap is repeated on a vendor page:** Zack Academy quotes the BBRS statement "may only acquire up to a maximum of six hours of continuing education via on-line training." Yet the same page markets a 12-hour online package ($109; BBRS course numbers CS-012301 and CS-012300). — [Zack Academy CSL CE](https://www.zackacademy.com/class/contractor-licensing-and-renewal/ma-12-hour-unrestricted-construction-supervisor-license--csl--continuing-education-online-anytime/20623?referer=1)
- **Online course design requirements** (all automatable):
  - A penalty-of-perjury affirmation at the start of each session.
  - At least 5 questions per 30 minutes, which must be answered correctly to advance, with a loop back to the material on a wrong answer.
  - No skipping ahead.
  - A 20-question final requiring 16 correct, plus a second affirmation.
  - Closed captioning.
  - Content updated for code changes each renewal period.
  - Source: [Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-4)
- **Provider obligations:**
  - The coordinator must register with the BBRS and may oversee at most 12 courses.
  - Course applications are due 120 days before the offering, and approval lasts for the 24-month cycle.
  - Course and coordinator fees are charged per hour and set in 801 CMR 4.02.
  - Records must be kept 3 years, and certificates issued within 10 days.
  - The coordinator must be reachable by students during courses and must attend BBRS workshops.
  - Instructors need, for example, 5 years of practical experience in the last 10, or CSL or building-official status plus 3 years.
  - Source: [Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-4)
- **Who applies:** only the course coordinator files for approval and is responsible for instructor qualifications. — [mass.gov CSL CE application FAQ](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/construction-supervisor-licensing-continuing-education-application-frequently-asked-questions)
- **Coordinator fee:** new coordinator registration is $400, per the July 2019 form (search excerpt). — [CSL coordinator registration form](https://www.mass.gov/doc/csl-coordinator-registration/download)
- **Course forms:** BBRS uses separate online and classroom course application forms, dated 7/2019. — [CSL online course application](https://www.mass.gov/doc/csl-online-course-application-r5-0/download); [CSL classroom course application](https://www.mass.gov/doc/csl-classroom-course-application-r5/download)

**Electrician CE (Board of State Examiners of Electricians; 237 CMR 17.00)**
- **Hours:** 21 clock hours per 3-year cycle, of which 15 are code and related topics and 6 are professional development. — [At Leisure (vendor)](https://atleisurelicense.com/taxonomy/term/306)
- **Online:** fully online completion is offered (vendor claim). — [At Leisure (vendor)](https://atleisurelicense.com/taxonomy/term/306)
- **Board approval:** the Board must pre-approve every online 6- and 15-hour course, and approvals must keep meeting current requirements. Guidelines are dated June 27, 2016. — [Board online CE guidelines](https://www.mass.gov/doc/new-el-board-online-continuing-educational-policy-0/download)
- **Prices:** At Leisure charges $220–$247 for 21 hours, $169 for 15 hours and $78 for 6 hours. Mike Holt charges $178 for 15 hours (2026). — [At Leisure](https://atleisurelicense.com/taxonomy/term/306); [Mike Holt](https://www.mikeholt.com/Electrical-Continuing-Education-Massachusetts-Courses.php)

**Real estate CE (Board of Registration of Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons; 254 CMR 4.00)**
- **Hours:** 12 hours per 2-year renewal cycle. — [RealEstateU MA renewal guide](https://realestateu.com/real-estate/guides/massachusetts-real-estate-license-renewal-continuing-education)
- **Online rules:**
  - Online CE is allowed only from approved providers.
  - Distance courses need a timing device so they cannot be finished faster than their credit hours, plus an inactivity timeout.
  - Self-assessment quizzes are used, and final exams are not required.
  - Source: [RealEstateU MA renewal guide](https://realestateu.com/real-estate/guides/massachusetts-real-estate-license-renewal-continuing-education)
- **School authorization:** schools must be Board-authorized on a Board form with a fee, and authorization lasts 2 years. — [254 CMR 4.00 (mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/254-cmr-4-real-estate-school-authorization/download); [Justia 254 CMR 4.00](https://regulations.justia.com/states/massachusetts/254-cmr/title-254-cmr-4-00/section-4-00)
- **Instructors:** they must hold a current MA broker license, have 2 or more years working 25 or more hours a week as a broker, complete 30 hours of Board-approved instructor training, and supply a medical certificate. — [mass.gov instructor approval](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-real-estate-instructor-approval)
- **Online course requirements:** the Board's online CE requirements date from 2010 and were updated in 2013, with an online education attestation form. — [Real estate online education attestation](https://www.mass.gov/doc/real-estate-online-education-attestation-form/download)
- **Incumbents:** McKissock, CE Shop, Colibri, RealEstateU and KeepMeCertified. — [McKissock MA CE](https://www.mckissock.com/?p=76957); [KeepMeCertified](https://keepmecertified.com/blog/?p=1657)

### Inferences
- **CSL CE fit:**
  - An online-only CSL CE provider can capture only up to 6 of the 12 required hours (6 of 10 restricted, all 6 for specialty), if the 6-hour cap is current.
  - That makes the 6-hour specialty CE the cleanest fully online CSL CE product. A "6-hour online core" for unrestricted and restricted holders is the other product.
  - Price anchor is roughly $50–$110 (Zack Academy listed 12 hours at $109).
  - The mastery gating, quizzes, finals, affirmations, captions and automated certificates are all software features.
  - The remaining manual work: the coordinator registration ($400), course applications 120 days ahead, attending BBRS workshops, being reachable during courses, and a qualified instructor of record.
- **Electrician CE fit:**
  - This is the most attractive recurring CE market for a software-only provider: fully online, a 3-year cycle, $169–$247 per licensee, and a mandatory content refresh each NEC cycle that disrupts incumbents.
  - The bar is Board pre-approval of each course, and NEC copyright limits what text can be shown.
- **Real estate CE fit:** the most crowded market (national incumbents and white-label brokerage channels), and it needs a broker-instructor on record. Lowest priority.
- **Owner workload:** every CE route needs an initial credentialed applicant, either the owner or a contracted CSL, master electrician or broker. After approval, per-student work can be zero (automated certificates and reporting). This conflicts slightly with "no manual work," because approvals must be renewed every 2–3 years.

### Gaps
- **Current status of the CSL 6-hour online cap not confirmed.** The 10th-edition Chapter 110.R5 PDF on mass.gov was blocked, and the Cornell LII text may reflect the 9th edition. Zack Academy's page contains both the cap and a "12 hours online" claim.
- **Fees not found:** the per-hour CSL course approval fee amounts (801 CMR 4.02), the electrician CE provider application fee and the real estate school authorization fee.
- **Licensee counts not found:** I found no official number of active CSL holders, electricians or plumbers. BuildZoom's figure of about 139,000 MA "licensed contractors" mixes license types and is not usable.
- **Plumber CE:** hours and provider rules were not researched.

## Q6. Shortlist: the best 3–6 MA markets for a self-serve, software-only prep or CE product

### Takeaway
Ranked by evidence of volume, pain and MA-specific content that is weakly served by paid products:
1. CSL exam prep (10th edition, PSI format), plus CSL online CE as an add-on.
2. MTEL math and literacy subtests (largest volume and lowest pass rates, but the most competition).
3. MA electrician 15-hour code update CE plus journeyman exam prep (recurring and fully online).
4. MA journeyman and master plumber prep (248 CMR is fully MA-authored; few paid competitors found).
5. MA hoisting engineer prep (state-run exam, November 2025 regulation changes, Spanish-language angle).
6. Optional, lower priority: MA real estate state portion (crowded).

Deprioritize: the learner's permit test (free incumbent), Title 5 (bundled class) and insurance (national incumbents, Prometric).

### Cited Findings
- **CSL:**
  - About 25% pass rate under PSI against 51% under Prometric, with about 650 first-time takers and about 1,100 attempts from January to early April 2025. — [BBRS 4/8/2025](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-minutes-4-8-25/download)
  - Incumbent price $249. — [At Leisure](https://atleisurelicense.com/node/27556)
  - Code basis: 10th edition, effective October 11, 2024, on 2021 I-Codes. — [OpenExamPrep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-general-contractor/introduction/exam-facts)
  - Online CE cap of 6 hours. — [Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/780-CMR-110-R5-4)
- **MTEL:**
  - Elementary Math first-time pass rate is 28.0%, Middle School Math 40.4%, ESL 41.9%, Foundations of Reading 68.2% (2,624 first-time takers) and General Curriculum Math/Science 61.3% (2,474). — [MTEL 2023–24](https://mtel.nesinc.com/Content/TechReport/PDFs/Annual_Pass_Rate_Report_2324.pdf)
  - 240 Tutoring charges $39.99/month. — [Centenary](https://centenary.edu/academics/departments-schools/education/education-aspire/passing-the-praxis)
- **Electrician:**
  - 21 hours of CE every 3 years at $169–$247. — [At Leisure](https://atleisurelicense.com/taxonomy/term/306); [Mike Holt](https://www.mikeholt.com/Electrical-Continuing-Education-Massachusetts-Courses.php)
  - Board pre-approval of each online course. — [EL Board online guidelines](https://www.mass.gov/doc/new-el-board-online-continuing-educational-policy-0/download)
  - Exam restructured in 2024 with solar and battery-storage content; bulletin effective 11/1/2024. — [mass.gov notice](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/new-candidate-information-bulletin-from-psi-effective-212024); [PSI bulletin](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/493)
- **Plumber:**
  - PSI; $80 per attempt; up to 6 attempts in 12 months; 248 CMR 10.00 effective December 8, 2023; free OpenExamPrep guide exists. — [OpenExamPrep MA plumber](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/ma-plumber)
- **Hoisting:**
  - $75 per restriction (form revised December 2025). — [DOL hoisting application](https://www.mass.gov/doc/hoisting-engineer-exam-application/download)
  - Incumbent 4G course at $129. — [At Leisure](https://atleisurelicense.com/node/3196)
  - Language-access bills. — [SD2109](https://malegislature.gov/Bills/194/SD2109.pdf)
- **Real estate:**
  - CE Shop charges $79 for the state portion alone. — [CE Shop](https://www.theceshop.com/massachusetts/real-estate-exam-prep/ma-salesperson-exam-prep-cost/)
  - Free AI competitor EstatePass. — [EstatePass](https://www.estatepass.ai/)
- **Learner's permit:** free incumbent with library distribution. — [driving-tests.org](https://driving-tests.org/massachusetts/rmv-practice-test)

### Inferences

**1. CSL exam prep (top pick)**
- **Why:** an acute, documented pain point (pass rate halved after a vendor and code change), about 2,400 first-time candidates a year (estimate), a high willingness to pay (retakes cost $75–$100 each), and stale incumbents.
- **Product:** 10th-edition-aligned question bank by 780 CMR chapter and by IBC/IRC/IECC/OSHA/521 CMR, timed PSI-style mock exams, open-book "find it fast" drills, tab placement guide, restricted, unrestricted and specialty tracks.
- **Price:** $79–$149 one-time, or $29/month.
- **Rough size (estimate):** 2,400 × 30% conversion × $99 ≈ $70,000 a year before retakers.
- **Risks:** free AI competitors, and the board may fix exam difficulty (it is reviewing sub-60% questions).

**2. CSL online CE (add-on, same audience)**
- 6-hour specialty CE and a 6-hour online portion for unrestricted and restricted licensees.
- Needs a coordinator ($400), a qualified instructor of record and course applications 120 days ahead.
- Recurring every 2 years.
- Note: the 6-hour online cap limits it to half the requirement for most licensees. Verify the current rule first.

**3. MTEL (largest market, most competitive)**
- Focus on the low-pass subtests (General Curriculum Math 278, Elementary Math 068, Middle School Math 065, Foundations of Reading 190 including open-response practice, Communication & Literacy).
- Subscription at $19–$29/month to undercut 240 Tutoring.
- Content is MA-specific (MA Curriculum Frameworks), and Pearson publishes free official practice tests and test objectives to align to.
- Risks: strong national incumbents (240 Tutoring, Mometrix, Study.com) and a declining Communication & Literacy cohort as alternatives (CASA, MoGEA, WEST-B, MTEL-Flex) expand.

**4. Electrician 15-hour code update CE plus journeyman/master exam prep**
- Recurring revenue, fully online, a $169–$247 price point and a forced refresh every NEC cycle.
- Needs Board approval per course, and NEC text cannot be reproduced.
- Exam prep benefits from the 2024 restructure (solar and battery-storage content).

**5. Plumber (journeyman/master) prep**
- 248 CMR is fully MA-authored, so it is quotable and has no national equivalent, making it a defensible niche.
- The only competition found is a free AI guide plus local schools.
- Volume is unknown, which is the main risk.

**6. Hoisting prep**
- State-run exam, multiple license classes, November 2025 regulation changes and a 60-day retake bar (vendor claim). The retake bar raises the stakes of failing.
- Spanish and Portuguese versions would be differentiating.
- Volume is unknown.

**Not recommended**
- Real estate state portion or CE: crowded, plus a free AI entrant.
- Insurance: national players, no MA-specific pain found.
- Learner's permit: free, entrenched incumbent.
- Title 5: class and field exam bundled with NEIWPCC.
- MA notary: believed to have no exam; not verified.

### Gaps
- Annual candidate volumes are missing for electrician, plumber, hoisting, real estate and insurance, so sizing for options 4–6 is unquantified.
- The CSL annualization rests on a single early-2025 data point. Later BBRS minutes (late 2025 to 2026) may show recovered pass rates or volumes but were not retrievable.
- It is not confirmed whether the CSL exam time was extended to 4 hours, which would affect simulation design, or whether the 6-hour online CE cap still applies under the 10th edition.
- No reviews or complaints were found that directly evidence poor quality or outdated editions in specific paid MA CSL or electrician products, beyond the 9th-edition and 2015-code references still on At Leisure pages.
