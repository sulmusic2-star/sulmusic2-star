# Franchise White Space in Massachusetts: Proven Service Franchises With Few or No MA Units That Can Run Semi-Absentee or Manager-Run

Research date: 2026-10-09. Unit counts were checked between 2026-10-09 and the dates shown on each source. **How counts were checked:** "Verified" means I read the franchisor's own location finder or state directory page during this session (WebFetch). "Search-based" means no MA location appeared in web searches, press releases or third-party location data. That is weaker evidence than a locator read. A large share of the FDD Item 7/19 figures below come from third-party aggregators (franchiseinvestordata.com, Franzy, Peersense, BizBuySell/BizQuest resale listings, sharpsheets) and **not from the FDD PDFs themselves**. I did not open any FDD PDF from a state database (CA DFPI, WI DFI, MN CARDS) in this pass. Treat every Item 19 figure as "reported, to be confirmed against the FDD."

**Source-quality warning on franchiseinvestordata.com:** this site is used heavily below because it quotes FDD-year-specific Item 19 numbers, but its pages are internally inconsistent. Its Primrose page says both "570 US Locations" and "operates 400 US locations," and gives a "simple payback period" of 47.7 years beside a 12–20 year claim. Its Scenthound page says its state-level unit counts are "proportional" population-based estimates rather than real counts. Use its state counts for nothing, and its Item 19 figures only as leads ([franchiseinvestordata – Primrose](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/primrose-schools); [franchiseinvestordata – Scenthound](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/scenthound)).

## Key Question 1: Which proven service franchises have strong unit counts in other regions but few or zero units in Massachusetts?

### Takeaway
The clearest Massachusetts gaps are brands that grew up in the South and Midwest and have not yet expanded into New England. Christian Brothers Automotive has 0 MA shops (verified on its locator: 31 states, none in New England). Scenthound has no MA location found (search-based; about 141–154 units in about 25 states). Kids 'R' Kids also has none found (search-based; about 160–170 academies in 16 states). Tommy's Express Car Wash has no MA site found (search-based; 38 states as of July 2026). Woof Gang Bakery & Grooming opened its first MA store in Boston only in Dec 2025. Great Clips (31 MA salons, verified) and Take 5 Oil Change (4 MA shops found) are under-represented rather than absent. Several brands people expect to be gaps are already well established in MA: Mosquito Joe has 9, Goldfish Swim School 11, Big Blue Swim School 4, and Dogtopia at least 5. Those are not white space.

### Inferences
- **Per-capita benchmark (approximate, not separately sourced in this pass):** MA is roughly 2% of the U.S. population, so a "fair share" footprint is about 2% of a brand's U.S. units. On that yardstick:
  - Great Clips' 31 MA salons out of ~4,150+ is about 0.75%, roughly a third of fair share (fair share ≈ 85–90).
  - Take 5's ≥4 MA shops is far under fair share for a ~1,000-unit-plus system.
  - Mosquito Joe (9/173 ≈ 5.2%) and Big Blue (4/62 ≈ 6.5%) are *over*-indexed in MA.
- **Ranked shortlist.** Ranking weighs four things: (1) strength of the MA gap evidence, (2) proven economics elsewhere (Item 19 and SBA record), (3) fit with an owner who manages but does not do the service work, and (4) MA-specific fit.

- The best MA-specific candidates are businesses that (a) employ W-2 staff, which matters under the MA ABC test, (b) need no owner trade license, and (c) benefit from MA's high incomes or winter. Scenthound, Great Clips, Woof Gang, Tommy's Express and Take 5 fit this profile.
- Home-trades brands (HVAC, roofing, remodeling) were not the focus of this file. Most large home-services portfolios (Neighborly, Authority Brands) already have MA coverage, and Mosquito Joe illustrates that saturation.

## Key Question 2: For each candidate, what are total U.S. units vs MA units, semi-absentee status, Item 7 investment, royalty/ad fund, and Item 19 performance (with FDD year)?

### Takeaway
Proven economics are strongest at Christian Brothers: about $2.88M average net sales and about $315K average owner benefit after the profit split (2025 FDD, CY2024), with zero SBA charge-offs. That comes with a full-time GM-owner role and $550K–$680K of capital. Scenthound has the best low-capital semi-absentee profile: $489K average revenue and $84K average NOI (2026 FDD, FY2025) on $323K–$551K. Great Clips offers the most mature semi-absentee model: about $399K–$411K average salon sales and about $83.5K average operating cash flow (2026 FDD). Almost all of these figures come through aggregators and need confirming against the FDDs.

## Key Question 3: Are these concepts registered or available in Massachusetts (registration-state status)?

### Takeaway
Massachusetts is **not** a franchise registration state, nor a filing/notice state. A franchisor can offer and sell in MA once it complies with the FTC Franchise Rule; no state approval step exists. "Not registered in MA" is therefore never the reason a brand is absent. Absence reflects the franchisor's development strategy, territory sales, or real-estate and labor economics.

### Cited Findings
- MA is a non-registration state, with no registration or filing required before franchises are offered or sold there — [Franchise Law Solutions – MA](https://franchiselawsolutions.com/franchising/massachusetts-franchise-registration); [Franchise Law Solutions – non-registration states](https://www.franchiselawsolutions.com/learn/franchise-compliance/franchise-non-registration-states)
- The commonly cited registration states are CA, HI, IL, IN, MD, MI, MN, NY, ND, RI, VA, WA and WI. South Dakota appears on some lists and Virginia's classification is inconsistent across sources — [Cost Analysts](https://www.costanalysts.com/franchise-registration-states/); [Franchise Law Solutions](https://www.franchiselawsolutions.com/learn/franchise-compliance/franchise-non-registration-states)
- Rhode Island, MA's neighbor, *is* a registration state — [Cost Analysts](https://www.costanalysts.com/franchise-registration-states/)
- Third-party directories list Christian Brothers and Scenthound as available to MA buyers, and Franchising.com lists MA among Two Maids' offered states — [BizQuest MA automotive](https://www.bizquest.com/franchise-for-sale/automotive-franchises-in-massachusetts-ma/); [BizQuest MA pet](https://www.bizquest.com/franchise-for-sale/pet-franchises-in-massachusetts-ma/); [Franchising.com – Two Maids](https://www.franchising.com/twomaids)

### Inferences
- Because MA requires no state review, a buyer gets no state-examiner check of the FDD. Independent franchise counsel review matters more. The MA consumer-protection statute (c. 93A) may be relevant, but was not researched here.
- A brand can sell MA territories the day it decides to. Being first into MA as a multi-unit developer is negotiable, and an early developer may get better area-development terms.

### Gaps
- I did not verify directly with each franchisor which MA territories are open or sold. That needs a call to franchise development or a review of Item 12 territory maps.

## Key Question 4: What Massachusetts-specific factors help or hurt these concepts?

### Takeaway
MA helps premium and recurring-revenue consumer services. It has a ~$104K median household income and the highest center-based infant care price in the U.S. at $27,067 per year. It hurts through labor and compliance costs. The ABC test makes 1099 service labor nearly unusable. PFML costs 0.88% of wages for employers with 25+ covered individuals. HIC registration and the CSL gate home-improvement work. A home-care agency licensure law was moving through the Legislature in 2025–2026. Concepts with W-2 staff and no trade license on the owner fit MA best.

### Cited Findings
- **Income:** MA median household income was $103,960 (ACS 2020–2024 5-year, 2024 dollars) — [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/csv/MA/INC110224). The single-year 2024 figure was $104,800 — [USAFacts](https://usafacts.org/answers/what-is-the-income-of-a-us-household/state/massachusetts)
- **Housing age:** median construction year 1964 for MA (third-party, not verified against ACS table B25034) — [Point2Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/MA-Demographics.html)
- **Child-care pricing:** MA had the highest annual price of center-based infant care, $27,067, versus a low of $6,492 in Mississippi (Child Care Aware data for 2025, via First Trust, Sept 2026) — [First Trust blog](https://www.ftportfolios.com/Blogs/InvestBlog/2026/9/24/factoid---thursday,-september-24,-2026). Other estimates range from about $23K to $26K — [Playground](https://www.tryplayground.com/blog/child-care-daycare-tuition-prices-massachusetts); [Wonderschool](https://www.wonderschool.com/blog/family-resources/how-much-does-daycare-cost-in-massachusetts-in-2026)
- **ABC test (M.G.L. c. 149 §148B):**
  - In *Patel v. 7-Eleven* (2022, 489 Mass. 356), the SJC held that the three-prong independent-contractor test applies to franchisor–franchisee relationships where the franchisee is an "individual performing any service," and does not conflict with the FTC Franchise Rule — [Boston Bar Journal](https://bostonbar.org/journal/patel-v-7-eleven-sjcs-answers-to-certified-questions/); [SJC opinion via mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/doc/dhananjay-patel-et-al-v-7-eleven-inc-et-al-sjc-13166/download)
  - A second SJC decision (Sept 5, 2024) is summarized secondarily as finding that 7-Eleven did not misclassify those franchisees. **I did not read the opinion** — [vLex](https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/patel-v-7-eleven-1060160675)
  - In *Awuah v. Coverall* (D. Mass. 2010), janitorial "unit franchisees" were held to be misclassified employees of the franchisor — [Epstein Becker Green Workforce Bulletin](https://www.ebglaw.com/workforce-bulletin/tag/franchisees)
- **PFML 2026:**
  - The total contribution is 0.88% of eligible wages for employers with 25+ covered individuals: medical 0.70%, of which the employer pays at least 60%; family 0.18%.
  - Employers with fewer than 25 pay a 0.46% rate that is entirely employee-funded, with no employer share.
  - The maximum weekly benefit is $1,230.39 in 2026.
  - Sources: [Seyfarth](https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/2026-massachusetts-paid-family-and-medical-leave-updates-benefit-amount-contribution-rates-and-taxability-of-benefit-payments.html); [NFP](https://www.nfp.com/insights/2026-massachusetts-pfml-rates-and-benefits-released/)
- **Home-improvement licensing:** anyone bidding on or performing contracting work on existing owner-occupied 1–4 unit residences must register as a Home Improvement Contractor. The Construction Supervisor License is a separate credential issued by the Office of Public Safety and Inspections, and the two cannot be substituted for each other — [mass.gov HIC resources](https://mass.gov/info-details/hic-contractor-resources); [mass.gov HIC registration](https://mass.gov/info-details/home-improvement-contractor-registration-and-renewal)
- **Home care licensure (pending):**
  - MA has historically not licensed non-medical home care agencies — [Vitable compliance summary](https://www.vitablehealth.com/home-care-compliance/massachusetts/licensure)
  - The House passed H.4706 on Nov 5, 2025. It creates EOHHS licensure with ownership disclosure, workers' comp and liability insurance, training requirements, fines up to $500/day and 120-day provisional licenses — [H.4706 text](https://malegislature.gov/Bills/194/H4706.pdf); [Speaker Mariano release](https://ronmariano.com/?p=626)
  - A Senate amendment text (S.3183) dated July 16, 2026 amends H.4706 — [S.3183](https://malegislature.gov/Bills/194/S3183.pdf). Whether it became law is **unconfirmed**.
- **Competitive context for cleaning:** MaidPro was founded in Boston in 1991 — [Two Maids comparison page](https://twomaidsfranchise.com/the-maids-franchise)

### Inferences
- **Helps:**
  - High incomes support premium-priced, membership-based services: Scenthound memberships, Woof Gang grooming, Tommy's Express unlimited-wash plans, premium child care (Kids 'R' Kids, Primrose, Celebree).
  - The highest infant-care prices in the U.S. mean higher revenue per child-care seat than in the Southern markets where Kids 'R' Kids and Celebree built their Item 19 histories.
  - Harsh winters and road salt plausibly raise demand for car washes and undercarriage and corrosion repair (Tommy's Express, Christian Brothers). This is inferred; no MA-specific car-wash demand data was gathered.
  - Old housing stock (median year built ~1964, unverified) favors repair and remodel brands, though those trades need HIC or CSL credentials.
- **Hurts:**
  - The ABC test's prong B (work outside the usual course of the hirer's business) makes 1099 groomers, cleaners, technicians or instructors very hard to defend. Choose brands whose model is W-2: Two Maids, Great Clips, Scenthound, Christian Brothers.
  - Avoid unit-franchise janitorial models in the Coverall, Jan-Pro and Stratus mold, both as a franchisee-owner and as an area developer.
  - MA's high wage floor (the $15 minimum wage was supplied in the brief and not re-verified here) and PFML compress margins in labor-heavy concepts.
- **Licensing:** where licensing attaches to the business, an owner who manages but does not do the work must hire or employ licensed people. That applies to an HIC-registered entity, a CSL holder for structural work, pesticide applicators for mosquito work, EEC-qualified directors for child care, and possibly the pending home-care license. It adds hiring risk but does not stop the model.
- **Home care:** for senior home care franchises, which are saturated in MA anyway, pending licensure would add compliance cost. It could also reduce fly-by-night competition.

### Gaps
- Not verified in this pass: the MA minimum wage figure, MA pesticide applicator licensing (MDAR), MA security-guard licensing for Signal 88, MA EEC staffing ratios, MA cosmetology licensing for Great Clips stylists, MA car-wash water and permitting rules, and Mass Save heat-pump incentives.
- The final status of the home-care licensure bill after July 2026 is unknown.
